Super Monday is in the books. The Google I/O keynote, Cursor's own frontier model, and Musk v. Altman's unanimous dismissal all landed simultaneously — the AI coding tool market reshaped itself in a single 24-hour window.

## Google I/O 2026: Gemini 3.5 Flash GA + Antigravity 2.0 Goes Global

The I/O keynote's headline: **Gemini 3.5 Flash is GA**. It outputs 4x faster than other frontier models and dominates coding benchmarks — Terminal-Bench 2.1 at **76.2%**, MCP Atlas at **83.6%** ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/live/google-io-2026-live-news-updates)). Priced at $1.50/M input, $9.00/M output with a 1M-token context window ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-3-5-flash)). Gemini 3.5 Pro is slated for next month ([Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/artificial-intelligence/google-io-2026-live-updates-gemini-android-17-xr-glasses-and-more)).

**Antigravity 2.0** launched globally — not just a free preview but the full package: desktop app, CLI tool, and SDK for custom workflows ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/google-launches-antigravity-2-0-with-an-updated-desktop-app-and-cli-tool/)). Multi-agent orchestration, native voice commands, and AI Studio project export are all included. A new **AI Ultra** plan ($100/mo) offers 5x higher AI limits than Pro ([Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/05/google-io-2026-antigravity-developer-tools-ai-studio-mobile-app.html)). Google also unveiled **Gemini Spark**, a personal AI agent in the Gemini app that runs 24/7 on Google Cloud VMs ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-roundup/)).

## Cursor: Composer 2.5 — Own Frontier Model Era Begins

Cursor shipped **Composer 2.5**, its first in-house model matching frontier performance — **79.8% on SWE-Bench Multilingual**, **63.2% on CursorBench v3.1** ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/cursors-composer-2-5-matches-opus-4-7-and-gpt-5-5-benchmarks-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/)). At $0.50/M input and $2.50/M output, it dramatically undercuts competitors. Elon Musk amplified the launch within hours, and Cursor announced a partnership with xAI to train a significantly larger model using Colossus 2 ([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/blog/composer-2-5)).

## Musk v. Altman: $134B Lawsuit Unanimously Dismissed

An Oakland jury **unanimously rejected** all of Musk's claims — breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment both barred by the statute of limitations ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/18/nx-s1-5822366/musk-altman-openai-jury-verdict-claims-dismissed)). Musk called it a "calendar technicality" and vowed a 9th Circuit appeal ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/18/musk-altman-openai-trial-verdict.html)), but the ruling clears the last major legal obstacle to OpenAI's for-profit transition.
