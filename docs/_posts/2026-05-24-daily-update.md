The five-day Antigravity 2.0 auto-update disaster reached a turning point today as Google shipped the official v2.0.0 patch. Meanwhile, GitHub Copilot's 20-week decline continues with usage-based billing just 8 days away, and both Anthropic and OpenAI are racing toward historic financial milestones.

## Antigravity: Google Apologizes for "a Deeply Chaotic Week"

Google pushed the official Antigravity 2.0 v2.0.0 patch with three critical fixes: a highly visible "Open IDE" button in the top-right corner of project conversations, a one-click migration tool to restore settings, extensions, and keybindings from older installations, and a complete reset of Gemini weekly usage quotas for all users ([Piunikaweb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/23/google-antigravity-2-0-ide-update-gemini-quota-reset/)). Google acknowledged that "the team should have done better" ([Piunikaweb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/23/google-antigravity-2-0-ide-update-gemini-quota-reset/)).

The popularity score barely moved — 52 to 53. The I/O surge from 48 to 62 was completely wiped out by the auto-update fiasco, and functional recovery alone won't rebuild the trust that was broken.

## GitHub Copilot: 20-Week Decline, D-8 to Usage-Based Billing

Copilot dropped to 63, extending its streak to 20 consecutive weeks of decline. Usage-based billing goes live June 1 ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Under the new AI Credits system (1 credit = $0.01), token consumption determines cost per model. Code completions and Next Edit Suggestions remain unlimited, but Chat, CLI, cloud agents, Spaces, and Spark all consume credits. Community sentiment remains sour: *"You will get less, but pay the same price"* ([Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/04/27/devs-sound-off-on-usage-based-copilot-pricing-change-you-will-get-less-but-pay-the-same-price.aspx)).

## Anthropic's $30B+ Round Expected to Close Next Week

Anthropic's largest-ever funding round is set to close as soon as the week of May 26 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week)). At a pre-money valuation above $900B, Anthropic would surpass OpenAI's $852B to become the world's most valuable private AI startup. Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are each investing roughly $2B. This comes as Q2 revenue is projected at $10.9B — potentially Anthropic's first profitable quarter.

## OpenAI Preparing Confidential IPO Filing — September Target

OpenAI is preparing to confidentially file its IPO prospectus, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as lead banks ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/openai-ipo-filing.html)). The company targets a $1T valuation and aims to raise over $60B, making it potentially the largest tech IPO in history. The path cleared after the unanimous jury rejection of Musk's lawsuit on May 19. SpaceX filed its own IPO prospectus the same week, setting up a direct race for the biggest listing ever.
