Anthropic's Fable 5 free ride is officially over — and its paid future is now locked in. Starting today, Fable 5 is permanently included on all Max and Team Premium plans at 50% of limits ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-20-2026-16-biggest-stories)). Pro and Team Standard users receive a one-time $100 usage credit, with ongoing pricing at $10/$50 per MTok. Meanwhile, OpenAI is still wrestling with four consecutive days of service incidents since the GPT-5.6 Sol transition, raising questions about infrastructure readiness.

## Claude: Fable 5 Goes Permanent + v2.1.215

Fable 5's free access expired at 11:59 PM PT on July 19 after three extensions — originally set to end June 22, then July 7, then July 12 ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/07/13/claude-fable-5-extends-to-july-19-7-days-7-power-moves/)). Pro users now pay usage credits at double the Opus 4.8 rate ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320905/20260718/claude-fable-5-ends-subscription-limbo-permanent-max-credits-only-pro.htm)). Claude Code also shipped v2.1.215, which makes `/verify` and `/code-review` explicit-call-only — they are no longer auto-invoked by the agent ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). Anthropic's IPO roadshow has begun with bankers scheduling investor meetings, targeting an October listing ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com)).

## OpenAI: Four Straight Days of Outages

Starting with GPT-5.6 Sol server overload on July 17, followed by a ~5-hour Codex outage on July 18, elevated ChatGPT errors on July 19, and GitHub-dependent Codex workflow failures today — OpenAI has logged four incidents in four days ([OpenAI Status](https://status.openai.com/history)). The instability coincides with GPT-5.6 Sol settling in as ChatGPT's default model. Codex CLI v0.144.6 corrected GPT-5.6 context windows to 272K tokens ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## Kimi K3: The 2.8-Trillion-Parameter Giant That Beat Fable 5

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 holds the #1 spot on Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena with a 76% pairwise win rate, beating Claude Fable 5 ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/moonshot-releases-2-8-trillion-parameter-kimi-k3)). At $3/$15 per MTok — one-third of Fable 5's cost — with open weights dropping July 27, markets are calling it another "DeepSeek moment" for Chinese AI ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/17/china-moonshot-kimi-k3-markets-china-ai/)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro: Three Missed Deadlines and Counting

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro has blown through its third GA target on July 17. Bloomberg reports Google scrapped the original model and is rebuilding from scratch, sending Alphabet shares down ~4% ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/google-gemini-launch-delayed-as-tech-falls-short-of-internal-goals)). Adding to the fallout, Copilot is deprecating Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash across all experiences on July 31 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/month/07-2026/)).

## Cursor: iOS Public Beta + Slack Multi-Repo

Cursor for iOS is now in public beta on all paid plans ([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/blog)). The Slack integration upgrade added pre-execution plan sharing, multi-repo support, and cross-channel functionality, pushing Cursor beyond the desktop IDE into team collaboration territory ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)).
