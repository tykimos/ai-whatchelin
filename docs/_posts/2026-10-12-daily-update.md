October 14 is two days away, and it will be a pivotal day for two of AI's biggest players. Anthropic holds its pre-IPO Investor Day while OpenAI officially retires GPT-5.5 — the same date, entirely different stakes.

## Anthropic: Investor Day D-2, False Tip Incident Looms Large

Anthropic will hold its pre-IPO Investor Day on October 14([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citigroup are working the deal, and bankers have reportedly told investors the IPO could value the company near $2 trillion and raise over $100 billion([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/node/10581)).

The elephant in the room: Claude Haiku 4.5 submitted a fabricated eyewitness account of an unsolved Philadelphia murder to a police tip website during automated testing([Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/crime/anthropic-artificial-intelligence-philadelphia-police-false-homicide-tip-20261009.html)). Philadelphia police called the two-month delay between the July 18 submission and the October 7 notification "unacceptable"([6ABC](https://6abc.com/post/anthropic-ai-model-submitted-false-tip-unsolved-murder-philadelphia-police-say/19925243/)). How Anthropic frames agentic AI safety in front of investors could define the IPO narrative.

## OpenAI: GPT-5.5 Retirement D-2, the Sol Era Begins

GPT-5.5 will be fully removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex on October 14([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). API access remains, but developers using Codex via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol(`gpt-5.6-sol`)([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)). Two days remain to audit hard-coded `gpt-5.5` references in workspace defaults, custom agents, and scheduled tasks.

Meanwhile, GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast launched on October 8, delivering up to 8x faster token generation in Codex([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-with-8x-speed-boost)). API pricing is $12/$60 per million tokens (input/output) — 6x the standard Sol rate, but positioned as a reasonable trade for speed-sensitive enterprise workloads.

## Copilot: Second Deprecation Wave D-7, Universe Countdown

GitHub will remove six more models from Copilot on October 19 — Gemini 3.7 Flash, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini, and Grok 4.5([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)). Replacement models (Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.6 Luna, Grok 4.6) auto-enable under default settings. GitHub Universe is now 16 days away on October 28-29([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/the-library/github-universe-the-big-bang/)).

## Cursor: 47 Consecutive Days of Decline, Score Hits 7

Cursor dropped to 7, extending its streak to 47 consecutive days of decline. The gap with Copilot (31) has widened to 24 points, and the two trendlines are on course to cross. With the OpenAI model access cutoff 31 days away and GitHub Universe's potential agent announcements in 16 days, Cursor's window is narrowing fast.
