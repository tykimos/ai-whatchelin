OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80% just three weeks after its general availability launch, opening a new chapter in the AI model price war. The move signals that frontier AI labs' pricing power is eroding rapidly under global competitive pressure.

## OpenAI: GPT-5.6 Luna Down 80%, Terra Down 20%

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens on July 30 — an 80% reduction([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/ai-price-wars-openai-cuts-gpt-5-6-luna-prices-by-80-as-model-competition-shifts-toward-cost)). Terra also dropped 20%, from $2.50/$15 to $2/$12, while the flagship Sol stays unchanged at $5/$30([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/open-ai-price-cut-gpt.html)). Coming only three weeks after the July 9 GA launch, the cuts reflect inference infrastructure improvements and competitive pressure from Anthropic and Google([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-just-cut-gpt-5-013753910.html)). Since Codex CLI Auto-review runs on GPT-5.6 Luna, the price cut translates to roughly 10x lower Auto-review costs([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## GitHub Copilot: Gemini 2.5 Pro & 3 Flash Officially Deprecated Today

Today (July 31), Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash are officially deprecated across all GitHub Copilot experiences([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-02-upcoming-deprecation-of-gemini-2-5-pro-and-gemini-3-flash/)). The deprecation affects Copilot Chat, inline edits, ask and agent modes, and code completions — users must switch to Gemini 3.1 Pro or Gemini 3.5 Flash([GitHub Changelog X](https://x.com/GHchangelog/status/2072828939777560672)). Yesterday's Copilot Visual Studio July update also shipped a new Copilot SDK-based agent with built-in .NET and Azure expertise([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-30-github-copilot-in-visual-studio-july-update/)).

## Cursor: $20 Single Band Pricing + iPad Goes Full-Featured

Cursor consolidated Pro, Pro+, and Ultra under a single $20/mo individual band with usage-based scaling starting July 28([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). The rebuilt iPad layout adds split-screen chats, richer diffs, and improved markup for larger-screen editing([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/cursor)). Cursor Router's intelligent model routing now powers Auto mode, delivering 30-60% cost savings across requests.

## Antigravity: v1.1.8 — Structured Output and JSON Schema Validation

Antigravity v1.1.8 (July 28) added `json` and `stream-json` structured output formats, custom JSON schema validation, enriched subagent payloads, and a `copyOnSelect` configuration setting([Antigravity Changelog](https://antigravity.google/changelog)). Entering its 27th consecutive week at 99, Antigravity remains the most stable performer among CLI agent tools.
