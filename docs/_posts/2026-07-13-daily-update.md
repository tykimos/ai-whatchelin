Yesterday's "access ends tonight" headline aged poorly. Anthropic announced via @claudeai on X early July 13 that Fable 5 plan-included access for all paid subscribers (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) is extended through July 19 — the third extension in five weeks ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/)). Users can allocate up to 50% of weekly usage to Fable 5 at no extra cost; after July 19, $10/$50 per MTok usage credits kick in ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/13/claude-fable-5-free-access-extended-july-19/)). The repeated delays read as a defensive play against GPT-5.6 and Grok 4.5, both of which launched in the same week as the original cutoff.

## Claude Code 50% Limit Boost Also Extended to July 19

The 50% weekly limit increase for Claude Code, running since May 13 and originally ending today at 6 PM PDT, was extended to July 19 alongside Fable 5 ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/13/claude-code-weekly-limits-promotion-extended/)). Hacker News commenters noted that "nobody has ever experienced the normal limits" — raising concerns about what the post-promo baseline will actually feel like ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48883064)).

## "Claude Honeycomb EAP" Leaks in Cursor — Opus 5 Speculation Grows

An unannounced Anthropic research model called "Claude Honeycomb EAP" briefly appeared in Cursor's model picker on July 8 before being removed within hours ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48842904)). The description mentioned a 1M context window, "extra high effort" mode, and safety fallback routing to Opus 4.8 — fueling community speculation that it's an Opus 5 preview ([Explainx](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/claude-opus-5-release-speculation-july-2026)). Separately, ToolCrush reported on July 11 that Dario Amodei confirmed Sonnet 5.5 is expected next week, aiming to close the gap between Sonnet 5 and Opus 4.8 ([ToolCrush](https://toolcrush.io/blog/ai-news-this-week-july-11-2026)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro D-4 — July 17 GA Countdown

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is four days from its July 17 GA target ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a)). The 2-million-token context window and Deep Think reasoning layer are the headliners, though the model was delayed from its original June target due to coding performance and long-horizon reasoning quality concerns ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-launch-to-july-2026/)). Meanwhile, Copilot users must migrate off Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash by July 31 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-02-upcoming-deprecation-of-gemini-2-5-pro-and-gemini-3-flash/)).

## Copilot Vision GA — Image and PDF Attachments Now Live

Copilot Chat now supports image and PDF attachments for multimodal code reasoning ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/github)). The feature closes a gap with Claude and ChatGPT, but whether it can reverse Copilot's 68-week decline (now at 13) remains to be seen.
