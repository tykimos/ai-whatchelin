The AI coding tool market's cost structure is crystallizing. Cursor's iOS promotion ends today, Fable 5's free subscription inclusion and Google's Ultra AI Access both expire in two days. Developers are entering a full-price regime.

## Cursor: iOS 75% Discount Expires Today

Cursor's iOS public beta discount on Composer 2.5 mobile runs expires today ([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/blog/ios-mobile-app)). The first major consumer product since the SpaceX acquisition lets developers launch agents, review PRs, and use voice input from their phones. Starting tomorrow, full pricing applies. Teams pricing (Standard $32/seat/month, Premium $96/seat/month annual) has been live since July 1 ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). Team MCP server distribution and Organization group support are also new additions as Cursor pushes deeper into the enterprise market.

## Fable 5: Free Inclusion D-2 — $10/$50/MTok After July 7

Fable 5, restored globally on July 1 after 19 days of US government export controls, will leave subscription plans in two days ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)). After July 7, accessing its market-leading coding capability (95% SWE-bench Verified) requires usage credits at $10/$50 per MTok. However, an Anthropic engineer clarified that this is temporary: "We aim to restore Fable as a standard part of our subscriptions as soon as capacity allows" ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-isnt-permanently-leaving-subscriptions-anthropic-says/)). With Sonnet 5 settled as Claude Code's default at $2/$10 intro pricing through August 31 ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)), most teams will split daily work on Sonnet 5 and reserve Fable 5 for high-difficulty tasks.

## Copilot: 60-Week Decline Hits 21 — An Unprecedented Record

GitHub Copilot dropped to 21 on its 60th consecutive week of decline. Day 35 of usage-based billing sees developers spending $30-40/day on agentic sessions continuing to leave ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/github-copilot-users-threaten-exit-as-metered-billing-kicks-in/5249826)). GitHub added AI Credit Pools for cost centers to let enterprises cap per-team spending ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)), but individual developers remain frustrated. Reddit reports growing migration plans to OpenRouter and direct API access.

## Antigravity: Hits 93, 16-Week Winning Streak

Antigravity rose to 93 on its 16th consecutive week of gains. With Gemini CLI at shutdown Day 17, it continues absorbing residual migration demand. The July 7 convergence of Ultra AI Access removal and SPCX's Nasdaq-100 inclusion reinforces Google's AI coding ecosystem consolidating under the Antigravity brand ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/build-with-google-antigravity-our-new-agentic-development-platform/)).

## GPT-5.6: GA Expected Mid-July

GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna limited preview continues with ~20 pre-approved organizations. Cerebras will offer Sol at 750 tok/s when it launches in July ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)). General availability is expected around July 10-17.
