Anthropic's Fable 5 subscription inclusion, extended from July 7 to July 12 following user backlash, expires tonight at 11:59:59 PM PT ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/anthropic-fable-5-access-extended-july-12-2026)). Starting tomorrow, using Fable 5 requires enabling usage credits at $10/$50 per MTok — Anthropic's highest published pricing. Users who don't enable credits lose access immediately with no grace period ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319864/20260707/claude-fable-5-drops-subscriptions-tonight-enable-credits-lose-access.htm)). Anthropic has pledged to restore Fable 5 to subscription plans "as capacity allows," but no timeline has been given.

## Cursor Rises to 97 — iOS Beta and Grok 4.5 Integration Pay Off

Cursor climbed from 96 to 97, its first movement in three weeks. Adoption of 3.11's side chats is spreading as developers use `/side` for parallel agent exploration, and the iOS public beta is now available on all paid plans ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). SpaceXAI's jointly developed Grok 4.5 is now available across all Cursor plans, significantly broadening model diversity ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2211260/spacex-ai-grok-4-5-cursor/)). Grok 4.5 delivers Opus-class performance at $2/$6 per MTok — 60% cheaper than Opus 4.8.

## GPT-5.6 Day 4 — ChatGPT Work Super App Settling In

GPT-5.6's Sol/Terra/Luna trio reached Day 4 of public availability ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/ai-openai-gpt-release)). OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work alongside the models, merging Codex into the ChatGPT desktop app with a 15-integration plugin directory. Sol's "ultra" mode delegates work to submodels for complex agentic tasks. Meanwhile, Group Chat creation was discontinued as of July 9, and Atlas browser deprecation is set for August 9 ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro D-5 — July 17 GA Countdown

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is five days from its July 17 GA target ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a)). The model introduces a 2-million-token context window, a Deep Think reasoning layer, and an architectural overhaul focused on coding and math benchmarks. The delay from the original June target was caused by token-efficiency concerns, coding performance gaps, and long-horizon reasoning quality ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-launch-to-july-2026/)). Gemini CLI continues its decline at 15 on shutdown Day 24.

## Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI at $47B Revenue Run Rate

Fortune confirmed that Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate has reached $47B, surpassing OpenAI's projected $25-33B for 2026 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/02/)). Claude Code alone grew from $1B in annualized revenue at end of 2025 to over $2.5B by February 2026. The company leads on developer market share and agentic coding reliability.
