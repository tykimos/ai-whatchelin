Three countdowns are running simultaneously in the AI coding tool market. Anthropic's IPO roadshow is two days from launch, Cursor's OpenAI model access ends in five days, and GitHub Copilot's model swap arrives in nine. This week and next will define the second half of 2026 for AI coding.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow Expected Week of November 9

After filing its public S-1 with the SEC on October 26, Anthropic's IPO roadshow is expected to begin the week of November 9 ([Implicator AI](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-could-start-ipo-roadshow-week-of-nov-9-putting-debut-before-thanksgiving/)). Under SEC 15-day rules, the earliest pricing date is November 13, with first trading possible on November 16 ([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/anthropic-ipo-s1-filing-nasdaq-ant-2026/)). The company plans to list on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, targeting a debut before Thanksgiving ([Invenglobal](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27712/anthropic-files-s-1-for-ipo-plans-nasdaq-listing-under-ticker-ant)). Notably, Anthropic's S-1 warned that AI could pose "catastrophic and existential risks" if misused, drawing attention from regulators and investors alike ([ABC News](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/gallery/anthropic-ipo-filing-warns-ai-could-pose-catastrophic-existential-risks-if-misused?photo=1)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff D-5, Day 74 at Bottom

Five days remain until OpenAI fully terminates Cursor's model access on November 12 ([OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/blog/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex)). OpenAI cited compliance concerns following SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)). Cursor's CEO said OpenAI models account for only about 5% of total traffic, but the symbolic weight is significant ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). The pivot to Grok 4.7 and Anthropic models continues. Users can enter their own OpenAI API key, but core features like Tab autocomplete, cloud agents, and automations won't work with it.

## GitHub Copilot: 43-Day Streak, Hits 83

GitHub Copilot extends its winning streak to 43 consecutive days, reaching a score of 83. The November 16 model swap — removing Claude Opus 4.8/5 and GPT-5.6 Terra in favor of Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol — is now 9 days away ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). Environments with hardcoded model names must migrate before the deadline. Claude Opus 4.8 gets an exception for annual subscribers only ([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/github-copilot-claude-opus-5-deprecation-november-16-2026/)). The momentum from JetBrains Air and Xcode integrations continues.

## OpenAI Dots: Default Activation in 11 Days

OpenAI's always-on Dots agents will become default in Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces on November 18 ([OpenAI Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Admins who don't opt out will see Dots automatically enabled. Microsoft integration under Agent 365 governance is in progress ([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4229252/openai-bets-enterprises-are-ready-to-delegate-real-work-to-autonomous-agents.html)). As Goldman Sachs' recent restriction of AI coding tools demonstrated, enterprise agent governance is becoming increasingly critical.
