Three major deadlines are converging this week in the AI coding tool market. Cursor's iOS promotional pricing ends July 5, Fable 5's subscription-included access ends July 7, and Google removes Ultra AI Access from Workspace the same day. Developers have days to recalculate their cost structures.

## Cursor: iOS App 75% Discount Ends Tomorrow

Cursor's iOS public beta has been drawing strong interest since its App Store debut. The 75% discount on Composer 2.5 mobile runs ends July 5 ([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/blog/ios-mobile-app)). The app lets developers launch agents, review PRs, inspect diffs, and use voice input from their phones — the first major consumer product since the SpaceX acquisition. Teams pricing is now in effect: Standard at $32/seat/month and Premium at $96/seat/month (annual) since July 1 ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)).

## Fable 5: Free Inclusion Ends July 7 — Then $10/$50 per MTok

After 19 days of US government export controls, Fable 5 was restored globally on July 1. It's currently included in Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans at up to 50% of weekly usage limits ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)). That free window closes July 7 — after which accessing the market's highest coding capability (95% SWE-bench Verified) requires usage credits at $10/$50 per MTok. With Sonnet 5 settling in as Claude Code's default at $2/$10 intro pricing through August 31 ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)), expect most teams to split daily work on Sonnet 5 and reserve Fable 5 for high-difficulty tasks only.

## Google: Ultra AI Access Removed from Workspace July 7

Google is removing the Ultra AI Access add-on from Workspace accounts starting July 7 ([Google Workspace Help](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/gemini/ai-ultra-access)). Organizations that rely on Gemini CLI or Code Assist access must migrate to Enterprise Standard or Plus. Antigravity hit 92 on its 15th consecutive week of gains, absorbing Gemini CLI (23) migration demand, but the Ultra removal adds enterprise-level migration pressure.

## Copilot: 59-Week Decline Hits 22 — No Floor in Sight

GitHub Copilot dropped to 22 on its 59th consecutive week of decline ([NxCode](https://www.nxcode.io/resources/news/github-copilot-getting-worse-2026-developers-switching)). Day 34 of usage-based billing sees developers spending $30-40/day on agentic sessions continuing to leave. Adding Claude Sonnet 5 to Copilot ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-02-copilot-agent-session-streaming-is-now-in-public-preview/)) and launching agent session streaming in public preview haven't offset billing backlash.

## Antigravity: Hits 92, 15-Week Winning Streak

Antigravity rose to 92 on its 15th consecutive week of gains. With Gemini CLI at shutdown Day 16, it continues absorbing residual migration demand. SPCX's Nasdaq-100 inclusion on July 7 aligns with Google's AI coding ecosystem consolidating under the Antigravity brand.
