Sam Altman has proposed that all leading US AI companies give the government a 5% equity stake ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/openai-proposes-giving-the-us-government-a-5-stake-ft-says)). The proposal comes as GPT-5.6 Sol remains locked in a government-restricted limited preview and Fable 5 export controls were just lifted — signaling a fundamental shift in how AI companies navigate Washington.

## OpenAI: 5% Government Stake Proposal Reshapes AI-Washington Relations

Altman isn't proposing this for OpenAI alone — he's suggesting every leading US AI developer give the government a 5% stake ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/openai-proposes-giving-the-us-government-a-5-stake-ft-says)). With GPT-5.6 Sol already restricted to ~20 government-approved organizations, the proposal reflects OpenAI's urgency to redefine its relationship with regulators. Sol is priced at $5/$30, Terra at $2.50/$15, and Luna at $1/$6 per MTok, with Cerebras deployment at 750 tok/s on track for July ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)).

## Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI in Revenue — Fortune

Fortune reports that Anthropic has surpassed OpenAI in self-reported revenue ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/02/sam-altman-new-world-order-ai-openai-google-anthropic/)). Anthropic projects a $47B annualized run rate and expects profitability by 2029, while OpenAI estimates $25-33B. With a $965B valuation and October IPO approaching, Anthropic's momentum is formidable. Meanwhile, The Register reports Anthropic is removing covert code designed to catch Chinese competitors copying its models ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/01/anthropic-is-removing-its-covert-code-for-catching-chinese-competitors/5265366)).

## Copilot: Triple Update Can't Stop 57-Week Slide — 24 Points

GitHub Copilot shipped Kimi K2.7 Code (first open-weight model in the model picker), Vision GA, and auto model selection all in one day, but its 57-week decline marched on to 24 points ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-01-kimi-k2-7-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/)). Usage-based billing at $30-40/day for agentic sessions continues driving the exodus. Claude Sonnet 5 was also added to Copilot with Zero Data Retention ([GitHub](https://github.com/features/copilot/whats-new)).

## Claude Code v2.1.198: Chrome GA, Background Agent Auto-PR

Claude Code v2.1.198 shipped Chrome integration GA, background agents that auto-commit/push/draft PRs in worktrees, and a notification hook system ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)). Sonnet 5 is confirmed as the default model with 1M context and $2/$10 per MTok promotional pricing through August 31 ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)). Claude also launched as GA on Microsoft Foundry/Azure, expanding enterprise access with native identity and billing integration.

## Antigravity Breaks 90 — 13-Week Streak

Google Antigravity hit 90 on its 13th consecutive week of gains. CLI v2.1.4 added the antigravity_guide built-in skill, --project launch flags, OSC8 terminal hyperlinks, and a CPU compatibility crash fix. The biggest beneficiary of Gemini CLI's shutdown, it now leads Codex CLI (88) by 2 points.
