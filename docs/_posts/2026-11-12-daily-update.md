Three major developments are converging in the AI coding tool market today. Claude Fable 5.2 has become Claude Code's default model, Anthropic has locked in specific pricing for its historic IPO roadshow, and Cursor's OpenAI model access reaches its official termination date.

## Claude Fable 5.2: Faster, Cheaper, and Now the Default

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 alongside Mythos 5.2 on November 9 ([letsdatascience.com](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-52-and-mythos-52-models-66044607)). Output is 30% faster than Fable 5.1 with a 20% price cut to $8/$40 per MTok ([claudefa.st](https://claudefa.st/blog/models/claude-fable-5-2)). On FrontierCode 2.0 agentic coding, Fable 5.2 scored 49.1% — up from 32.4% for Fable 5.1 and surpassing Opus 5.5's 45.5% ([elser.ai](https://www.elser.ai/blog/claude-fable-5-2-benchmark-review-frontier-code-2-0-robo-dojo-and-terminal-bench-science)). METR flagged "stronger covert capabilities" and proximity to recursive self-improvement.

Claude Code v2.1.321, released November 10, made Fable 5.2 the default Fable model ([newreleases.io](https://newreleases.io/project/github/anthropics/claude-code/release/v2.1.321)). The release also includes an MCP OAuth refresh token redirect fix and a security patch for cp/mv deny rules not being enforced ([dev.classmethod.jp](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/20261110-cc-updates-v2-1-321/)). The npm `latest` and `next` tags point to 2.1.321, while `stable` remains at 2.1.311 ([labmemo.com](https://labmemo.com/?p=63055)).

## Anthropic IPO: $70-$78/Share, Pricing Expected November 18

Anthropic officially launched its IPO roadshow on November 10 ([anthropic.com](https://anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch)). The company plans to sell 825 million Class A shares at $70-$78 per share, targeting a base deal of $57.75B-$64.35B or up to $74B with the overallotment exercised in full ([rttnews.com](https://www.rttnews.com/3712632/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-at-70-78-per-share-launches-roadshow.aspx)). Shares will trade on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, with Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citi leading. Pricing is expected November 18, with trading potentially beginning November 19 ([renaissancecapital.com](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122417/ai-model-developer-anthropic-pbc-files-for-an-estimated-75-billion-ipo)). Revenue surged 1,300% year-on-year to $16.3B in H1 2026 ([investinglive.com](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-shows-42-billion-2025-loss-and-518-billion-spending-plans/)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Model Cutoff D-Day

OpenAI's proposed transition period for Cursor officially expires today, terminating model access after 79 days at rock bottom ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-cuts-off-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition-citing-musks-history-of-breaking-contracts/)). OpenAI cited SpaceX's "history of breaking contracts" and model distillation concerns, giving roughly two and a half months of transition notice ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/openai-to-end-partnership-with-cursor-following-spacex-acquisition/)). Users can still configure personal OpenAI API keys for some features, but Tab autocomplete, auto model selection, Cloud Agents, and CLI are not covered ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). GPT-6 Astra will never be supplied to Cursor ([Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/ai/news-openai-cursor-model-access-spacex-acquisition/)).

## GitHub Copilot: 49-Day Streak, Fusion Discount Running

Copilot rises to 93 on its 49th consecutive day of gains. The Fusion GA 30% model-cost discount runs through November 30 ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/github-copilot-fusion-orchestrates-anthropic-and-openai-models-to-balance-performance-and-cost)), and the November 6 weekly release added Agent Merge PR automation and cron tasks in VS Code 1.145 ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-06-github-copilot-weekly-releases-november-2)).
