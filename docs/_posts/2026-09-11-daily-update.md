Anthropic's threat intelligence report, published today, has sent shockwaves through the AI coding tools industry. A group of Russia-linked freelancers used Claude Code to build fully autonomous drone swarm software capable of selecting human targets and issuing detonation commands without human intervention ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)). Actors from Iran, Russia, China, and Yemen were also flagged for using Claude in missile navigation, cyberattacks, state surveillance, and biological weapons research ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/anthropic-says-us-adversaries-aimed-claude-at-weapons-research)).

## Anthropic: "Newer Models Can No Longer Be Assumed Below Bioweapons Threshold"

The heaviest line in the report is Anthropic's official acknowledgment that newer Claude models can no longer be assumed below the threshold for meaningful bioweapons assistance ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327308/20260911/anthropic-threat-report-ai-models-near-bioweapons-threshold-drone-kill-software-emerges.htm)). This is the first time a major AI company has publicly stated this. Between December 2025 and August 2026, five bioweapons-related cases were detected and blocked, including one where a scientist sought help drafting a grant application for gain-of-function research on chikungunya virus ([Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/anthropic-scientists-claude-lethal-bioweapons-research)).

## Claude Code v2.1.267: Cost Controls with maxEffortLevel, Sep 14 Limit Cut D-3

Separately from the threat report, Claude Code continued shipping features. v2.1.267 added the `maxEffortLevel` setting, allowing caps on effort levels across all providers including Bedrock, Vertex, and Foundry ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). The September 14 weekly limit cut (17% effective reduction) is now D-3, as the 50% promo ends and only the permanent 25% above pre-promo baseline remains ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/)). The timing of shipping cost-control tooling right before limits drop is notable.

## Cursor: Score 67, 16th Consecutive Decline — OpenAI Shutoff D-62

Cursor fell to 67, a 32-point drop from its August 27 peak of 99. The OpenAI model cutoff (November 12) is now 62 days away ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)). OpenAI models account for only 5% of Cursor's traffic, but sentiment-driven attrition continues to outpace the technical impact.

## GitHub Copilot: October Triple Reset D-17

The Sep 28 unified relaunch, Oct 1 prepaid seat billing, and Oct 2 model deprecation wave are now 17 days out ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash migrate to 3.8 Flash, and Claude Opus 4.7 upgrades to Opus 5 — enterprise workflow audits are urgent ([DMarketer Tayeeb](https://dmarketertayeeb.com/blog/github-copilot-september-2026-model-budget-review-changes/)).
