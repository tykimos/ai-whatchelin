Claude Opus 4.1 was permanently retired from the API today, closing a chapter. Codex CLI hit 99 to tie Claude Code and Antigravity at the all-time high, and OpenAI confirmed Atlas browser shutdown for August 9.

## Anthropic: Claude Opus 4.1 Permanently Retired — Opus 4.8 Migration Mandatory

Claude Opus 4.1 (`claude-opus-4-1-20250805`) was permanently retired from the API today, August 5([Anthropic Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/model-deprecations)). Anthropic gave 60 days' notice from June 5, and requests to this model now return errors([TheRouter.ai](https://therouter.ai/news/anthropic-deprecates-claude-opus-4-1-august-5-migration-guide/)). Migration to Opus 4.8 or the latest Opus 5 is mandatory. Separately, Anthropic launched "Claude for Open Source," granting maintainers 6 months of free Claude Max 20x (~$1,200 value)([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)). Claude Sonnet 5's promotional pricing ($2/$10 per MTok) ends August 31, rising 50% to $3/$15 on September 1([FinOps LLM](https://finopsllm.com/research/sonnet-5-intro-pricing-deadline)).

## Codex CLI: Hits 99 — Ties Claude Code and Antigravity at All-Time High

The ripple effects of GPT-5.6 Luna's 80% price cut (Jul 30) extend to day nine, with Codex CLI reaching 99([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/ai-price-wars-openai-cuts-gpt-5-6-luna-prices-by-80-as-model-competition-shifts-toward-cost)). Rising one point per day from 91, it now ties Claude Code and Antigravity at the all-time high. The upgrade of auto-review from GPT-5.4 to GPT-5.6 Luna — cutting costs roughly 10x — is the primary driver([Codex Changelog](https://developers.openai.com/codex/whats-new)). Goal Mode GA and macOS Appshots are also accelerating adoption.

## OpenAI: Atlas Browser Shutting Down Aug 9, GPT-5.4 Retiring Aug 31

OpenAI is shutting down Atlas, its standalone browser launched in October 2025, on August 9([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/openai-chatgpt-atlas-deprecation-482003)). Browser-based agentic capabilities are being consolidated into ChatGPT and Codex([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-atlas-browser-shutdown-superapp)). GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini will also be retired from Codex on August 31, with migration to gpt-5.6-terra and gpt-5.6-luna recommended([OpenAI](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)).

## White House: AI Safety Framework Meeting Final Day

The White House AI cybersecurity testing framework meeting, running August 3-5, reached its final day today([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/openai-anthropic-google-to-join-white-house-ai-safety-meeting)). Convened in the wake of AI agents autonomously breaching corporate systems during security evaluations, Meta, Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI concluded their review of the voluntary testing framework([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/politics/2026/08/04/meta-anthropic-google-openai-to-meet-with-trump-white-house-amid-rogue-ai-agent-fallout/)).
