Four Google DeepMind researchers departed for Anthropic in just six days, sending shockwaves through the AI industry. Nobel Chemistry Prize laureate John Jumper, AI coding and AlphaFold contributor Jonas Adler, pretraining specialist Alexander Pritzel, and Gemini 2.5 safety researcher Arthur Conmy — all core AlphaFold leadership — chose the $965B-valuation startup over Big Tech([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/google-poised-to-lose-two-more-high-profile-ai-staffers-to-anthropic)). Separately, Transformer co-author and Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer left for OpenAI([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/ai-researchers-continue-to-leave-google-for-its-rivals/)). Alphabet shed $270B+ in market cap across two trading sessions([AIToolsRecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/ai-news-june-26-2026)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro Delayed to July — Researcher Exodus Fallout

Google abandoned its June GA target for Gemini 3.5 Pro, pushing the launch to July([AIToolsRecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/ai-news-june-26-2026)). The departures and benchmark gaps are cited as key factors. Google expanded its "AI coding strike team to midtraining" to address capability gaps left by the exodus. Anthropic's gravitational pull on Big Tech talent is now undeniable as the company prepares for an October IPO.

## OpenAI Unveils Jalapeño Chip — 50% Lower Inference Cost

OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño, OpenAI's first custom inference chip([Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)). Early testing shows approximately 50% lower inference cost per token versus current-generation NVIDIA GPUs. Designed in nine months, prototype deployment is targeted for late 2026 with full production ramp in 2027-28.

## Copilot Hits 52-Week Decline — A Full Year of Freefall

GitHub Copilot recorded its 52nd consecutive weekly decline, landing at 30 — exactly one year of unbroken freefall from the 90s it held in late June 2025([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). Day 26 of usage-based billing continues to drive developer exodus, with agentic sessions costing $30-40/day. On the product side, the Copilot Desktop App went GA for macOS/Windows/Linux, offering agent-native development sessions with parallel agents and Canvases([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-17-github-copilot-app-generally-available/)).

## DeepSeek Permanently Cuts Prices 75%

DeepSeek made its promotional pricing permanent at $0.44 per million input tokens, undercutting GPT-5.5 by more than 5x on input pricing([Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)). The move further lowers the cost barrier for open-source AI coding workflows.

## Fable 5: Ban Day 14, Congress Deadline Arrives

The Fable 5 export control suspension enters its 14th day. Today is the deadline set by four bipartisan lawmakers demanding a written explanation from Commerce Secretary Lutnick([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/when-will-fable-5-be-available-again-2026)). Prediction markets give 57% odds of restoration before July 1, with the July 8 identity verification policy as the critical path.

## GPT-4.5 Retires Tomorrow

GPT-4.5 will be officially retired from ChatGPT tomorrow, June 27([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Launched just four months ago in February, its departure marks the end of all GPT-4 era models inside ChatGPT.
