The decisive week for Anthropic's IPO begins. The roadshow could start as early as tomorrow, Cursor's OpenAI model access ends in four days, and GitHub Copilot — armed with native Xcode integration — extends its winning streak to 44 consecutive days.

## Anthropic: Amended S-1 Filed, Price Range Still Blank

Anthropic filed an amended S-1 with the SEC on November 2 ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm)). The amendment includes Q3 financials through September 30, 2026 (quarterly revenue approximately $17B), but the share count and price range remain blank ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/a2d43b74-19f7-450e-a230-cb99d77e4a15/)). A final amended S-1 with the price range is expected just before the roadshow begins. Under SEC 15-day rules, the earliest pricing date is November 13 and first trading November 16 ([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/anthropic-ipo-s1-filing-nasdaq-ant-2026/)). The tug-of-war between institutional investors pushing for $1.5-1.6T and management's $2T target will be next week's central storyline ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-delay/)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff D-4, Day 75 at Bottom

Four days remain until OpenAI fully terminates Cursor's model access on November 12 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)). Triggered by SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere, the situation has pinned Cursor's score at 1 for 75 consecutive days. CEO Michael Truell noted OpenAI accounts for only 5% of traffic ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2246969/openai-pull-its-models-from-cursor-due-to-spacexai-acquisition/)), but the symbolic weight of the final cutoff will be unavoidable. The pivot to Grok 4.7 and Anthropic models continues, though the X Subscription bundle (Plus $8/mo to Ultra $200/mo) has failed to reverse the developer exodus.

## GitHub Copilot: Xcode Native Integration, Hits 85

GitHub Copilot extends its streak to 44 consecutive days, reaching 85. The biggest driver is its November 4 integration as a built-in coding assistant in Xcode 27.3 beta 1 ([MacObserver](https://www.macobserver.com/?p=495156)). The preview is available on all plans including Free, requiring macOS Tahoe 26.6 or later ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/?p=1077328)). The existing Copilot for Xcode extension continues to work alongside the native integration. With the November 16 model swap (Claude Opus 4.8/5 to Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Terra to GPT-6.1 Sol) now 8 days away, the gap between Copilot and Cursor has widened to 84 points (85 vs 1) ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)).

## OpenAI Dots: Default Activation in 10 Days

OpenAI's always-on Dots agents will become default in Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces on November 18 ([OpenAI Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). With the admin opt-out deadline 10 days away, discussions around enterprise agent governance are accelerating.

## Anthropic: Updated Usage Policy Takes Effect November 12

Anthropic's revised usage policy, announced October 8, goes into effect on November 12 ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3257335/venting-at-claude-is-fine-but-extreme-abuse-will-be-banned-nov-12.html)). The key change bans "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward Claude ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Anthropic-bans-abusive-behavior-toward-Claude-starting-November-12.1420910.0.html)). Ordinary frustration, dark creative themes, and model testing or research are explicitly excluded. The policy also tightens rules around election content, covert influence, surveillance, and weapons. It's notable that November 12 is also the day OpenAI cuts off Cursor — two major policy shifts landing on the same date.
