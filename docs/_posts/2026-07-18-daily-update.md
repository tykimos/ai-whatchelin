Anthropic's Fable 5 free access for paid subscribers ends tomorrow, July 19 at 11:59 PM PT ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/)). This marks the third extension since the June 12 export-control disruption, and this time there's no indication of another reprieve — continued use will require prepaid credits at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/13/claude-fable-5-free-access-extended-july-19/)). The community is watching closely for any Opus 5 announcement.

## Anaconda Acquires Kilo Code — A 10-Trillion-Token Empire

Anaconda has acquired Kilo Code, the open-source, model-agnostic coding agent used by over 3 million developers ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260715437804/en/Anaconda-Acquires-Kilo-Code-to-Power-the-Trillion-Token-Enterprise)). Kilo orchestrates nearly 10 trillion tokens per month across VS Code, JetBrains, and CLI, with early customers reporting 30–50% token consumption reductions through the Anaconda Platform ([Anaconda Blog](https://www.anaconda.com/blog/anaconda-acquires-kilo-code)). Co-founder Sid Sijbrandij called it *"a rare fit: almost no overlap, and what each of us lacks, the other already has."*

## Thinking Machines Inkling — Murati's First Open-Weight Model

Former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling, a 975B-parameter MoE model with only 41B active per task, trained on 45 trillion tokens ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/thinking-machines-amps-up-its-bet-against-one-size-fits-all-ai-with-its-first-open-model-inkling/)). It scored 84.7% on financial reasoning at one-fourteenth the run cost of competitors ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/what-is-mira-murati-thinking-machines-first-ai-model-inkling/)). The strategy targets customizability over raw benchmarks.

## Codex CLI v0.144.6 — GPT-5.6 Model Metadata Refresh

Today's Codex CLI v0.144.6 refreshes bundled instructions for GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna and corrects their context windows to 272K tokens ([GitHub](https://github.com/openai/codex/releases)). Over 10 alpha builds of v0.145.0 shipped in the past two days, signaling accelerated development on the next minor release.

## Cursor in Slack — Multi-Repo, Cross-Channel Workflows

Cursor's Slack integration received a major upgrade yesterday ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). It now shares a plan before starting, supports multi-repo work across frontend, backend, and shared code from a single Slack request, and can pull context from other channels and threads for cross-channel workflows.
