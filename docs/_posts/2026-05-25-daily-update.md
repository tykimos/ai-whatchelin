Pope Leo XIV today presented his first encyclical, *Magnifica Humanitas*, a 235-page document on protecting human dignity in the age of AI — co-presented with Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/25/europe/pope-leo-ai-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas-intl)). It is the first papal encyclical in history co-presented with a tech company founder. The pope called on AI developers to prioritize the common good over profit ([Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-05/pope-leo-xiv-first-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas.html)).

## SpaceX S-1: Anthropic Pays $1.25B/Month — $45B Total

SpaceX's IPO prospectus laid bare the full scope of the Anthropic compute contract ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/ipos/26/05/52724877/spacexs-45-billion-anthropic-deal-could-fund-its-ai-buildout-three-times-over)). Anthropic pays SpaceX $1.25 billion every month through May 2029 for 220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs and 300+ MW of compute across Colossus 1 and Colossus 2 ([Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/spacex-reveals-what-anthropic-will-pay-for-massive-compute-deal/)). That's roughly $41 million per day. The numbers confirm why Claude Code rate limits doubled overnight when the Colossus 1 deal was announced in early May.

## Copilot: 21-Week Slide Hits 62 — D-7 to Billing Overhaul

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 62 — down 28 points from 90 in early January. Usage-based billing goes live on June 1 ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Copilot CLI v1.0.54 shipped git context improvements ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli/releases)), but market share declining from 67% to 51% and billing anxiety continue to weigh on sentiment ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/22/github-copilot-reliability-crisis.html)).

## Anthropic: $30B+ Round Closing Next Week, First Profit in Sight

Anthropic is set to close its $30B+ round at $900B+ valuation the week of May 26 — surpassing OpenAI's $852B ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week)). Q2 revenue tracks at $10.9B, putting the company's first-ever profitable quarter within reach. The SpaceX contract disclosure, the Vatican AI ethics partnership, and wall-to-wall deployments at KPMG and PwC paint a picture of Anthropic expanding its influence at unprecedented speed.

## Antigravity: v2.0.0 Patch Recovery — Rebounds to 54

Google's patch delivered the "Open IDE" button and a full quota reset, pushing Antigravity from 52 to 54 ([Piunikaweb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/23/google-antigravity-2-0-ide-update-gemini-quota-reset/)). The June 18 Gemini CLI sunset deadline looms, increasing pressure on developers to migrate to Antigravity CLI.
