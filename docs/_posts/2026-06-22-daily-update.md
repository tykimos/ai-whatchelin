The week that prediction markets staked over $1.1 million on for a GPT-5.6 launch has arrived — but the market's confidence has evaporated. Meanwhile, Sensor Tower data confirms ChatGPT's market share has fallen below 50% for the first time, signaling a genuine shift in the AI tool landscape.

## GPT-5.6: Launch Window Opens, But Market Turns Skeptical

Today, June 22, marks the first day of the primary prediction window for GPT-5.6's release ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/when-will-gpt-5pt6-be-released)). Yet the "not released by June 28" contract has surged to **78%**, with the June 22–28 window now at just **40%** ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/polymarket-probability-of-gpt-5-6-not-launching-by-june-28-rises-to-78)). The model — codenamed iris-alpha with a reported 1.5M token context window and redesigned alignment pipeline — remains unconfirmed by OpenAI ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318799/20260621/gpt-56-launch-window-starts-monday-alignment-fix-15m-token-context-inside.htm)).

## ChatGPT Market Share Falls Below 50% for the First Time

Sensor Tower's 2026 State of AI Report shows ChatGPT dropped to **46.4%** market share — the first time it has fallen below 50% ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/)). Gemini rose to **27.7%** and Claude reached **10.3%**. The decline from 65.3% in December 2024 represents a 19-point drop in just 18 months. Claude leads competitors on paid conversion at 13% of users holding subscriptions ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91560276/chatgpt-loses-ground-gemini-claude-below-50-percent-market-share)).

## GLM-5.2: China's Open-Weight Model Beats GPT-5.5 at 1/6th the Cost

Zhipu AI's GLM-5.2 scored **62.1** on SWE-bench Pro versus GPT-5.5's 58.6 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/z-ais-open-weights-glm-5-2-beats-gpt-5-5-on-multiple-long-horizon-coding-benchmarks-for-1-6th-the-cost)). Output token pricing is $4.40/M compared to GPT-5.5's $30/M — approximately **6.8x cheaper** — and the model is released under MIT license with no regional restrictions. On FrontierSWE, GLM-5.2 hit 74.4%, nearly matching Opus 4.8 at 75.1% ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318543/20260617/glm-52-open-weights-live-top-coding-benchmark-api-use-carries-china-data-risk.htm)).

## OpenRouter Fusion: Budget Models Match Frontier Performance

OpenRouter's Fusion demonstrated that combining cheap models can approach frontier-level performance on the DRACO benchmark ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/fusion-beats-frontier/)). A budget panel of Gemini 3 Flash + Kimi K2.6 + DeepSeek V4 Pro scored **64.7%**, coming within 1% of Fable 5's solo 65.3% at half the cost. The quality panel (Fable 5 + GPT-5.5) hit **69.0%**, surpassing every single model tested.

## Fable 5 Suspension Day 10 — Free Trial Ends Today

Today marks both the 10th day of Fable 5's suspension and the last day it would have been available at no extra cost within subscription plans ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-22-2026)). Starting June 23, access shifts to API-rate usage credits — though with the model still offline, the billing transition is moot. Fable 5 reappeared in the Android app model selector, but selecting it returns errors; this is a UI artifact, not a partial restoration ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/is-fable-5-back-2026)).

## Codex Record & Replay Ships

OpenAI added Record & Replay to the Codex macOS app, allowing users to demonstrate a workflow once and convert it into a reusable, autonomous skill ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318759/20260620/openai-codex-automation-gains-record-replay-show-it-once-skip-script.htm)). The feature generates a natural-language SKILL.md that the model interprets against current screen state on replay. Available to Business, Enterprise, and Edu subscribers outside the EU, UK, and Switzerland.
