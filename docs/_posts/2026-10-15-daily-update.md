GitHub Copilot's second model deprecation wave on October 19 is now just 4 days away. GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5 are being removed across all Copilot experiences, and admins need to verify their model policies. Meanwhile, Cursor hit an unwanted milestone — 50 straight days of declining scores, bottoming out at 2.

## Copilot: Deprecation Wave D-4, Admins Take Note

GitHub will remove GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5 from all Copilot experiences on October 19([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)). Replacements are GPT-5.6 Sol (for GPT-5.5/5.4), GPT-5.6 Luna (for mini models), Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Grok 4.6. Enterprise and Business customers with global defaults enabled will auto-switch, but organizations with manual model policies must enable replacements themselves. Student and Free users can only access models via Auto selection. The first deprecation wave on October 2 (Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash, Kimi K2.7 Code, Claude Opus 4.7) is already in effect([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-02-selected-models-in-github-copilot-deprecated)). Copilot rises to 37, continuing its recovery trajectory.

## Cursor: 50 Consecutive Days Down, Score Hits 2

Cursor dropped to 2, marking its 50th consecutive day of decline. The freefall that began after the SpaceX acquisition closed on August 14 has now breached the single-digit floor. The OpenAI model access cutoff (Nov 12) is 28 days away, though co-founder Michael Truell said OpenAI models represent just 5% of Cursor traffic([FreePressJournal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/openai-to-end-cursor-model-access-after-spacex-acquisition)). The real damage isn't the model cutoff — it's the community exodus itself. Grok models plus Anthropic and Google offerings remain available, but developer sentiment shows no sign of reversing.

## Codex CLI: Steady After GPT-6.1 Sol Default Switch

Codex CLI v0.161.0 (Oct 7) made GPT-6.1 Sol the default model, and the tool has held stable since([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/r/openai-codex-rust-v0.161.0)). Rapid patches through v0.162.1 (Oct 9) focused on UX improvements — clickable URLs, task pinning, and git worktree management([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/releases/openai-codex/2026/10)). Day 11 of the daily improvement sprint continues delivering user-facing quality bumps.

## Anthropic: Roadshow Prep, Universe D-13

Following yesterday's Investor Day where Anthropic disclosed $16.8B in Q3 revenue, the company is preparing for a formal roadshow the week of November 9([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-q3-revenue-climbs-to-168-billion-report-221619202.html)). Anthropic is also listed as a speaker at GitHub Universe (Oct 28-29), where multi-model strategies for AI coding agents will be discussed([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/your-guide-to-github-universe-2026-is-here-the-schedule-just-launched/)). With Universe 13 days out, the question is what Copilot's HydraFusion expansion and Computer Use preview will deliver.
