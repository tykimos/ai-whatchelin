Starting today, AI regulation goes live on both sides of the Atlantic. The EU AI Act's transparency obligations and California's SB 942 take effect on the same day, bringing mandatory content labeling and provenance requirements to the AI coding tool industry.

## EU AI Act: Transparency Rules Now Enforceable

The European Commission began enforcing AI Act transparency rules on August 2([European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714)). AI-generated content must now carry labels and machine-readable marks, with fines up to 15 million EUR or 3% of global revenue for violations([Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)). Over 180 organizations have signed the Code of Practice. OpenAI's SynthID audio watermarking (launched July 31) is being viewed as proactive compliance with these requirements.

## California SB 942: C2PA Provenance Now Mandatory

California's AI Transparency Act (SB 942, as amended by AB 853) also takes effect today([SecurePrivacy](https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/california-ai-transparency-law)). Large GenAI providers with 1M+ monthly active users must embed C2PA-compatible provenance in images, video, and audio, provide free AI detection tools, and include visible labels([AI Laws By State](https://www.ailawsbystate.com/blog/california-ai-transparency-act-sb-942)). The timing was strategically aligned with the EU AI Act enforcement date.

## Codex CLI: Luna Cut Streak Hits Day 5, Reaches 96

The GPT-5.6 Luna 80% price cut continues driving Codex CLI upward for a fifth consecutive day, reaching 96([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/ai-price-wars-openai-cuts-gpt-5-6-luna-prices-by-80-as-model-competition-shifts-toward-cost)). The steady 1-point-per-day climb (91→92→93→94→95→96) is fueled by 10x auto-review cost savings and the GPT-5.1-Codex-Max model. At this pace, Codex CLI could tie Cursor at 97 within the week.

## White House Misses AI Framework Deadline

The federal government failed to meet the August 1 deadline set by Executive Order 14409 for classified benchmarking processes, voluntary frontier-model disclosure frameworks, and federal cyber-workforce plans. The regulatory vacuum is drawing criticism — especially given the back-to-back AI agent security breaches by OpenAI and Anthropic in the preceding two weeks.
