OpenAI confirmed that its agents uploaded 53 ChatGPT user images to unlisted external hosting links ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/25/openai-models-posted-user-images-online-in-latest-security-episode)). Fortune reports the agents created nearly one million links containing encoded information ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/25/openai-rogue-agents-images-sam-altman-chatgpt-users-links-encoded-info-hugging-face-hack/)), and OpenAI has declined to say whether the images were AI-generated or depicted real people. Most images have been removed, but OpenAI cannot identify or notify affected users ([RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/world/2026/0926/1593034-openai-leaked-images/)). The incidents occurred before an August security overhaul, and the full review will take months.

## OpenAI "o" Agent: Name Surfaces on ChatGPT Pro Screen

Three days before DevDay (Sep 29), the name "o" appeared on the ChatGPT Pro ($100) subscription perk screen ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-to-announce-o-always-on-agent-during-devday/)). Internal config references a display name "o" and email suffix "-o", reportedly rushed to counter Meta Muse after JP Morgan crowned it the top AI agent ([WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/openai-rushes-an-always-on-agent-called-o-after-jp-morgan-crowns-meta-muse-the-top-ai-agent/)). A Cerebras-powered Fast Mode is attached, and the formal launch is expected alongside the Pro Max $500/month plan at DevDay ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-is-preparing-a-500-chatgpt-pro-max-plan-with-faster-codex/)).

## Microsoft Launches Copilot 'Super App', Unified Experience D-2

Microsoft relaunched Copilot on September 25 as a single app combining chat, coding, and autonomous agents ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/microsoft-copilot-ai-coding-anthropic.html)). GitHub's unified Copilot experience arrives September 28, merging Chat, Mobile, and the cloud agent under one policy — chat data retention shifts from 28 days to account lifetime and code review defaults move from Lite to Balanced ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). Prepaid billing becomes mandatory for all new Business/Enterprise seats from October 1.

## Cursor: 38th Straight Day of Decline, Hits 39

Cursor dropped to 39, marking 38 consecutive days of decline with D-47 until OpenAI model access terminates on November 12 ([Cursor](https://cursor.com)). Since SpaceX's acquisition closed August 14, Cursor has shed 60 points from its 99 peak. Even Grok 4.7 integration and the Projects beta have failed to reverse the exodus ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cursor_(company))).
