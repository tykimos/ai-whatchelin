Anthropic's IPO finally has numbers attached. The S-1 registration statement filed publicly with the SEC on October 26 proposes a Nasdaq listing under the "ANT" ticker and discloses $19.7 billion in revenue for the 12 months ended June 30, 2026, alongside a $109.7 billion net loss ([IPOScoop](https://www.iposcoop.com/the-ipo-buzz-anthropic-ant-proposed-files-its-s-1/)). It's the first concrete financial disclosure since the confidential filing on June 1.

## Anthropic: S-1 Public and November Roadshow Countdown

The registration has not yet become effective, but Bloomberg reports that Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs could begin the roadshow as early as the week of November 9 ([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/anthropics-ipo-could-become-worlds-largest-ipo-surpassing-spacexs-record-breaking-ipo-2026-10-28)). Market expectations sit at a $1.5–2 trillion valuation, targeting a pre-Thanksgiving listing ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-nasdaq-ipo-listing-2026/)). The Q3 revenue of $16.8B disclosed at the October 14 investor day (46% QoQ growth) aligns with the S-1's annualized figures, confirming the growth trajectory.

## GitHub Copilot: Fusion GA Day 3 with 30% Discount

Following the Copilot SDK's rebrand to Microsoft Copilot SDK ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-github-copilot-sdk-is-becoming-microsoft-copilot-sdk/)), Copilot Fusion enters its third day of general availability. Artificial Analysis benchmarks show Fusion scoring 71 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 at $5.59 per task — roughly half of Claude Code (Opus 5.5 Max) at $11.42 ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/github-copilot-fusion-orchestrates-anthropic-and-openai-models-to-balance-performance-and-cost)). A temporary 30% model-cost discount for paid subscribers runs through November 30, and Copilot CLI v1.0.105 adds Fusion High and Fusion Max to the /model picker ([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/releases/github-copilot/2026/10)).

Copilot's popularity score rose to 69 — its 36th consecutive recovery day, now 68 points ahead of Cursor.

## Codex CLI: v0.165.0 Steady Cadence

Codex CLI shipped v0.165.0 on October 27 ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/openai/codex/releases)), maintaining a steady release cadence since GPT-6.1 Sol's default context window expanded to 500K tokens. GPT-6.1 Terra has also reportedly become available on paid plans.
