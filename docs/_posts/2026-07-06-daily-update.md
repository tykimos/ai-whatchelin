The AI coding tool market is closing the promotion era and entering full-price reality. Tomorrow, Fable 5's subscription inclusion and Google's Ultra AI Access both expire, while SPCX joins the Nasdaq-100. A cost structure watershed moment.

## Fable 5: Subscription Inclusion Expires Tomorrow — $10/$50/MTok Credits Start

Fable 5, restored globally on July 1 after 19 days of US government export controls, exits subscription plans tomorrow (July 7) ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)). After that, accessing its market-leading coding capability (95% SWE-bench Verified) requires usage credits at $10 input / $50 output per MTok. Anthropic emphasized this is not permanent: "We aim to restore Fable as a standard part of our subscriptions as soon as capacity allows" ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-isnt-permanently-leaving-subscriptions-anthropic-says/)). Most teams are expected to adopt a dual strategy — Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 promo pricing through Aug 31) for daily coding, Fable 5 reserved for high-difficulty tasks only ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)).

## Copilot: 61-Week Decline Hits 20 — First Time in the 20s

GitHub Copilot dropped to 20 on its 61st consecutive week of decline, entering the 20s for the first time ever. Day 36 of usage-based billing sees developers spending $30-40/day on agentic sessions continuing to leave ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/github-copilot-users-threaten-exit-as-metered-billing-kicks-in/5249826)). AI Credit Pools for cost centers are now available ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)), but individual developer frustration remains. A drop below 20 would signal effective abandonment of the individual market.

## Antigravity: Hits 94, 17-Week Winning Streak — 6 Points Above Codex CLI

Antigravity rose to 94 on its 17th consecutive week of gains. With Gemini CLI at shutdown Day 18, it continues absorbing residual migration demand ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/build-with-google-antigravity-our-new-agentic-development-platform/)). Tomorrow's convergence of Google Ultra AI Access removal and SPCX's Nasdaq-100 inclusion reinforces Google's AI coding ecosystem consolidating under the Antigravity brand.

## Tomorrow's Triple Event: Fable 5 + Ultra AI Access + Nasdaq-100

July 7 marks a triple convergence: Fable 5 subscription expiry, Google Ultra AI Access Workspace removal, and SPCX's Nasdaq-100 inclusion — all on the same day. Each directly impacts the cost structure and market positioning of Anthropic, Google, and SpaceX/Cursor respectively. Developers should reassess the true cost of their current tool stack.

## Codex CLI: Legacy Model Shutdown D-17

All legacy Codex API models shut down permanently on July 23 ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)). With GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna GA expected around July 10-17, the migration window is tightening. Cerebras will offer Sol at 750 tok/s when it launches in July.
