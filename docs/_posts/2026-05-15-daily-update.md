The Musk v. Altman trial entered jury deliberation after closing arguments concluded May 14. A nine-person California jury will issue an advisory verdict on whether OpenAI breached its charitable trust, whether executives enriched themselves, and whether Microsoft aided and abetted — with Judge Gonzalez Rogers making the final binding ruling([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/closing-arguments-jury-openai-musk-altman.html)). Musk's lawyers argued OpenAI deceptively abandoned its nonprofit mission, while Altman's side contended Musk is weaponizing the courts out of personal grievance([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/14/musk-closing-arguments-openai-altman)). The outcome could disrupt OpenAI's for-profit conversion structure.

## OpenAI: Apple Lawsuit + Codex Mobile + File Library + More Fundraising

OpenAI is preparing legal action against Apple over broken promises around ChatGPT integration([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-is-reportedly-preparing-legal-action-against-apple-it-wouldnt-be-the-first-partner-to-feel-burned/)). On the same day, a TanStack supply-chain attack compromised two employee devices, though no user data was accessed([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-says-hackers-stole-some-data-after-latest-code-security-issue/)). On the product side, Codex control landed in the ChatGPT mobile app for iOS and Android across all plans including free — users can view project context, approve commands, review diffs, and monitor tests from their phone([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/14/openai-brings-codex-control-to-chatgpt-for-iphone-and-android/)). File Library also expanded to Free and Go users (500MB free, up to 100GB for Pro)([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/chatgpt)). OpenAI's CFO signaled additional fundraising beyond the $122B round as compute demand intensifies([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/openai-may-raise-more-money-as-compute-crunch-deepens-cfo-says)).

## Anthropic: Small Business Push + Agent Metering

Anthropic launched Claude for Small Business — free half-day AI workshops bundled with 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows spanning finance, operations, sales, HR, and customer service([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-claude-small-business-smb)). Starting June 15, agent usage separates from chat subscriptions: Pro gets $20/month in credits, Max 5x gets $100, Max 20x gets $200([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4171274/anthropic-puts-claude-agents-on-a-meter-across-its-subscriptions.html)). Code with Claude London is set for May 19 — the same day as Google I/O.

## Windsurf: Devin for All + Cognition Eyes $25B Valuation

Cognition opened Devin Review, Quick Review, and Devin for Terminal to all Windsurf users([Cognition Blog](https://cognition.ai/blog/devin-in-windsurf)). The bigger story: Cognition is in talks to raise at a $25 billion valuation — more than doubling September 2025's $10.2B — with ARR also more than doubling since the Windsurf acquisition([Idlen](https://www.idlen.io/news/cognition-devin-25-billion-valuation-windsurf-vibe-coding-april-2026/)). Score rises 79→80.

## GitHub Copilot: Flex Allotments Confirm Price Hikes, 12-Week Slide

The fine print on Copilot's flex allotments is out: Pro usage rises from $10 to $15, Pro+ from $39 to $70([WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/05/14/github-copilot-individual-plans-introducing-flex-a-xcxwbn/)). Week 12 of the signup freeze — score holds at 72.
