Three tectonic shifts hit the AI coding tool market today. OpenAI aimed its next-generation models at Anthropic's most vulnerable moment, Anthropic expanded beyond coding into general office work, and Microsoft started questioning Copilot's fundamental reason to exist.

## GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna: Precision Strike on Fable 5's Transition Day

OpenAI's next-generation GPT-5.6 trio — Sol, Terra, and Luna — was discovered hidden in Codex app code ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/72d2cb81-4820-44a1-ab20-cfb53c7bf3db)). The internal launch deadline targets July 7–9, precisely overlapping with Fable 5's exit from subscriptions today. Sol is the flagship ($5/$30 per MTok), Terra matches GPT-5.5 at half the cost ($2.50/$15), and Luna is the budget lightweight ($1/$6) ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)). Sol sets a new state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and a Cerebras partnership will deliver 750 tokens/second in July. Currently limited to ~20 organizations, with general availability expected "in the coming weeks" ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov)).

## Fable 5: Subscription Inclusion Ends Today — Credits Start Tomorrow

Today is the last day Fable 5 is included in Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319767/20260706/fable-5-subscription-ends-tomorrow-per-token-costs-who-gets-hit-hardest.htm)). Starting tomorrow, its 95% SWE-bench performance costs $10/$50 per MTok — exactly double Opus 4.8. Anthropic emphasized this is temporary: "We aim to restore Fable as a standard part of subscriptions as soon as capacity allows" ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-isnt-permanently-leaving-subscriptions-anthropic-says/)). The emerging standard is Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 promo through Aug 31) for daily work and Fable 5 reserved for high-difficulty tasks only ([CoderSera](https://codersera.com/blog/claude-fable-5-usage-credits-july-2026/amp/)).

## Claude Cowork Goes Mobile — "90% of Users Aren't Coding"

Anthropic expanded Claude Cowork to mobile and web today ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/the-coding-agent-wars-are-spilling-into-the-rest-of-the-office-claude-cowork/)). Launched as a desktop app in January, Cowork now offers web and mobile beta for Max subscribers, with scheduled tasks running offline even after closing your laptop. The most surprising revelation was the usage data: 33.4% of usage was business process work (spreadsheets, reports), 16.4% was content creation, and software development accounted for just 8.7% ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-brings-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-as-usage-data-shows-most-users-arent-coding)).

## Microsoft: Copilot Must "Earn the Right to Exist" — M365 Adoption Under 4.5%

Microsoft EVP Jacob Andreou sent a 1,200-word memo telling the 11,000-person Copilot team the product must "earn the right to exist" ([WindowsLatest](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/07/07/microsoft-365-copilot-adoption-is-under-4-5-after-3-years-only-1-use-it-weekly-yet-prices-went-up/)). After three years, fewer than 4.5% of M365's 450 million commercial customers pay for Copilot, and only 1% use it weekly. When 150,000+ workers were given access to Copilot, ChatGPT, and Gemini simultaneously, just 8% chose Copilot ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319706/20260704/microsoft-copilot-merges-one-app-august-feature-cuts-reveal-paid-adoption-crisis.htm)). On the same day, GitHub opened Copilot App to all plans — including Free and Education — with BYOK mode for running sessions without a subscription ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-07-github-copilot-app-available-to-all/)).
