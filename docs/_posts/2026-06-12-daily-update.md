SpaceX begins trading on Nasdaq today. At $135/share and a $1.75T valuation, this is the largest IPO in history. More than 4x oversubscribed, it secures the funding for the $60B Cursor acquisition — a capital event that could reshape the AI coding tool landscape.

## SpaceX IPO: Nasdaq SPCX Trading Begins Today

SpaceX starts trading on Nasdaq under the SPCX ticker at $135/share today([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/spacex-ipo-explained-stock-price-date.html)). The offering of 556.6 million shares raises approximately $75 billion at a $1.75T valuation — the largest IPO in market history([HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/spacex-ipo-177-trillion-valuation-2026-ipo-wave/)). Bloomberg confirmed the IPO is more than 4x oversubscribed with approximately $150B in orders, and retail investors received about 30% of the public allocation([Bloomberg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QDfIsDTX0UA)). The proceeds are central to funding the $60B Cursor acquisition deal signed on June 5.

## Fable 5 Controversy Continues: Jailbreak Claims and Sabotage Backlash

The Fable 5 controversy has reignited days after launch([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318268/20260612/claude-fable-5-hit-jailbreak-claims-secret-sabotage-backlash-days-after-launch.htm)). The "secret sabotage" policy that silently degraded output for users suspected of AI research has already been reversed, but new jailbreak attempt claims have surfaced. Anthropic disputes the jailbreak claims and confirmed it has switched to explicitly routing flagged requests to Opus 4.8 with visible notification([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/10/anthropic-accu-claude-fable-5-limits-capabilities-ai-researchers-developers/)).

## Copilot CLI v1.0.61: Unified /settings Dialog

Copilot CLI shipped v1.0.61 with a new /settings command([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-11-copilot-cli-configure-everything-from-one-place-with-settings/)). It combines previously scattered options like /theme, /streamer-mode, and /experimental into a single, schema-driven full-screen dialog. Tab completion surfaces every available key with descriptions and allowed values, and /settings reset restores defaults.

## Copilot: 44, 39-Week Decline

Copilot dropped to **44** — its 39th consecutive weekly decline([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). Day 12 of usage-based billing. Since the June 1 transition, 904 community downvotes, $30-40/day agentic session costs, and 10-100x cost increases for some users have accelerated the developer exodus. Market share has plunged from 67% to 51%([MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/github-copilot-switches-to-token-based-billing-june-1-drawing-developer-backlash/)).

## Countdown: Critical Deadlines Approaching

Anthropic agent billing split (D-3, June 15) is 3 days away — Agent SDK, `claude -p`, and GitHub Actions usage moves to a dedicated credit pool. Credits are $20 for Pro, $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x monthly, with no rollover([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-agent-sdk-credits/)). Gemini CLI sunset (D-6, June 18) is 6 days out — individual and free users must migrate to Antigravity CLI([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)).
