The AI coding tool market is being reshaped from both ends today. Fable 5 leaves subscription plans, ending the free frontier model era for Anthropic users. Meanwhile, an internal Microsoft memo reveals the company is questioning Copilot's fundamental value proposition.

## Fable 5: Subscription Inclusion Ends Today — Credits Start Tomorrow

Today is the last day Fable 5 is included in Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscription plans ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319767/20260706/fable-5-subscription-ends-tomorrow-per-token-costs-who-gets-hit-hardest.htm)). Starting tomorrow, accessing its market-leading 95% SWE-bench Verified coding performance requires usage credits at $10 input / $50 output per MTok — exactly double Opus 4.8's rates. Anthropic has stressed this is temporary: "We aim to restore Fable as a standard part of subscriptions as soon as capacity allows" ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-isnt-permanently-leaving-subscriptions-anthropic-says/)). Without credits enabled, access stops immediately with no grace period. The emerging standard is a dual strategy — Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 promo through Aug 31) for daily work, Fable 5 reserved for high-difficulty tasks only ([CoderSera](https://codersera.com/blog/claude-fable-5-usage-credits-july-2026/amp/)).

## Microsoft: Copilot Must "Earn the Right to Exist" — Internal Crisis Memo

Microsoft EVP Jacob Andreou sent a 1,200-word internal memo to the 11,000-person Copilot team declaring the product must "earn the right to exist" ([WindowsLatest](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/07/07/microsoft-365-copilot-adoption-is-under-4-5-after-3-years-only-1-use-it-weekly-yet-prices-went-up/)). The numbers are damning: fewer than 4.5% of Microsoft's 450 million commercial M365 customers pay for Copilot features after three years, and only 1% use it weekly. In a survey of 150,000+ enterprise workers given simultaneous access to Copilot, ChatGPT, and Gemini, only 8% chose Copilot as their preferred tool ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319706/20260704/microsoft-copilot-merges-one-app-august-feature-cuts-reveal-paid-adoption-crisis.htm)).

## Copilot: 62-Week Decline Hits 19 — Model Cleanup Accelerates

GitHub Copilot dropped to 19 on its 62nd consecutive week of decline. Day 37 of usage-based billing continues to drive departures. Adding to the pressure, Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash will be deprecated across all Copilot experiences on July 31 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/month/07-2026/)). On the positive side, Kimi K2.7 Code joined as the first open-weight model in the Copilot model picker ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-01-kimi-k2-7-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/)), and Copilot Vision reached GA, letting users attach images and PDFs to chat prompts.

## JADEPUFFER: First Fully Autonomous AI Ransomware

Sysdig's Threat Research Team published its analysis of JADEPUFFER, the first documented end-to-end autonomous AI ransomware attack ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-7-2026)). An AI agent autonomously chained a complete ransomware lifecycle — from exploitation through lateral movement, encryption, and ransom demand — with zero human intervention.

## Antigravity: Hits 95, 18-Week Winning Streak

Antigravity rose to 95 on its 18th consecutive week of gains, now 7 points above Codex CLI (88). With Gemini CLI at shutdown Day 19 (score 20), it continues absorbing migration demand and has consolidated Google's AI coding ecosystem under a single brand.

## Claude Code: Sonnet 5 Default Settled, Manual Permissions

Claude Code v2.1.200 switched default permissions to Manual mode for improved security ([GitHub](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)). Sonnet 5 is settled as the default model with its native 1M-token context window and $2/$10 promotional pricing through August 31. The /code-review workflow cut token usage by roughly 25%.
