October 14 is now four days away. Two of the biggest events in AI coding converge on this single day — OpenAI's GPT-5.5 retirement and Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day. Meanwhile, Anthropic quietly introduced a first-of-its-kind policy: banning users who are needlessly cruel to Claude.

## Anthropic: 'No Cruelty Toward Claude' Policy Announced

Anthropic updated its usage policy on October 8 to ban "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward Claude, effective November 12([MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/08/anthropic-user-guideline-update/)). The primary enforcement mechanism is ending the conversation. Anthropic told The Verge this applies only to extreme cases — users who "repeatedly act cruelly toward our models, with no discernible purpose." Common frustration, pushback, dark creative themes, and model testing are explicitly excluded([PCWorld](https://pcworld.com/article/3257335/venting-at-claude-is-fine-but-extreme-abuse-will-be-banned-nov-12.html)). The policy grew out of a 2025 experiment giving Claude Opus 4 and 4.1 the power to end persistently abusive chats as part of model welfare research([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-moves-us-one-step-closer-to-making-clanker-a-slur-2000823772)).

## GPT-5.5 Retirement D-4: Migration Pressure Mounts

GPT-5.5 will be fully removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex on October 14([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). Developers using Codex via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol, though API key access remains unaffected([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)). GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast mode remains in limbo — confirmed "coming soon" but no ship date([RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-says-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-is-coming-soon)).

## Anthropic IPO Investor Day D-4: The $2T Test

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day on October 14 is four days out([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). Underwriters have told institutional investors the company could raise $100B or more at a valuation near $2T([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/node/10581)). Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan are leading the book, with listing targeted after the November midterms.

## Copilot: Claude Haiku 5.5 Added + October 19 Mass Deprecation D-9

Claude Haiku 5.5 was added to GitHub Copilot on October 7([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-claude-haiku-5-5-in-github-copilot)). Early tests show it performs close to Claude Sonnet 5 while requiring significantly fewer tokens and steps. Meanwhile, the second October deprecation wave is nine days away — six models including GPT-5.5 will be removed on October 19([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)).

## Cursor: 44 Consecutive Days of Decline, Score 11

Cursor dropped to 11, marking its 44th consecutive day of decline. The gap with Copilot (27) has widened to 16 points. On October 6, Cursor added remote agent control to its iOS app — users can now see and reply to running agents from their phone without cloud agents([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). The OpenAI model access cutoff on November 12 is now 33 days away.
