Cognition AI closed a $2B Series E at a $48B valuation — nearly double its $26B mark from just four months ago. The round signals investor conviction that AI coding is not a winner-take-all market but one that can sustain multiple large players simultaneously.

## Cognition AI: $2B Series E, $48B Valuation

Cognition AI, the parent company of Devin and Windsurf/Devin Desktop, closed its $2B Series E co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/cognition-hits-48b-valuation-signaling-investors-believe-ai-coding-is-far-from-a-winner-take-all-market/)). Over 30 institutional investors participated, including NVIDIA. Run-rate revenue surged from $492M at the May Series D to approximately $900M ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/cognition-raises-over-2b-series-e-at-48b-valuation-to-scale-devin-agents/)). Paired with SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Cursor, two AI coding editor megacamps are now fully capitalized.

## OpenHands 1.0: First Production Release for an Open-Source Coding Agent

The open-source autonomous coding agent OpenHands reached 1.0 with production-ready Docker sandboxing, built-in security policies, resource limits, and a plugin system ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/jamilxt/openhands-just-hit-10-heres-how-to-run-it-on-your-own-machine-without-handing-over-the-keys-5666)). It scores 68% on SWE-bench Verified — and at $0.20–$1.05 per resolved task on self-hosted H100s, it costs a fraction of commercial agents. Notably, the Devstral 24B configuration already matches Devin 2.0's published benchmark of 46.8%.

## Cursor: Score 71, 13th Consecutive Decline — D-64

Cursor dropped to 71, extending its losing streak to 13 consecutive days. The total decline from 99 on August 27 is now 28 points. With the OpenAI model cutoff (November 12) now 64 days away, CEO Michael Truell noted that OpenAI models account for roughly 5% of traffic ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)), but the psychological impact is outsized. Anthropic's pledge to increase Claude compute in Cursor remains the sole defensive line.

## OpenAI DevDay: September 29, Managed Agents Incoming

OpenAI DevDay takes place September 29 at Fort Mason in San Francisco ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026/)). The headline announcement is Managed Agents — a new platform capability allowing developers to create agents, environments, and agent sessions directly via the OpenAI Platform ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-managed-agents-devday-2026/)). Think of it as the cloud evolution of Codex CLI. Twenty days remain until the keynote.
