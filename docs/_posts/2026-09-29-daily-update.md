Today could reshape the AI coding tool landscape. OpenAI DevDay 2026 opens at Fort Mason in San Francisco, with Sam Altman's keynote livestreaming free at 10:00 a.m. Pacific ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026/)).

## OpenAI DevDay 2026: Always-On "o" Agent Imminent

The headline act is "o," an always-on agent that persists beyond chat sessions and even has its own email identity, according to code leaks from the ChatGPT app ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-to-announce-o-always-on-agent-during-devday/)). Its leaked three-tier pricing — Pro Lite at $100/mo, Pro at $200/mo, Pro Max at $500/mo — puts it squarely in the premium productivity bracket ([Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/09/openai-leaks-always-on-o-chatgpt-assistant.html)). A GPT-6 Cyber preview and the general availability of the Ultrafast tier (up to 750 tokens/sec on Cerebras hardware) are also expected on stage ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/09/21/openai-plans-to-introduce-managed-agents-at-devday-2026/)).

## Cursor: 32nd Straight Day of Decline, Drops to 33

Cursor fell to 33, extending its streak to 32 consecutive days of decline since August 28. It has now shed 66 points from its peak of 99. The OpenAI model access termination (Nov 12) is D-44 away, and layoffs continue as Cursor staff are folded into xAI teams under the SpaceX acquisition ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/cursor-staff-meet-xai-employees-layoffs-exits-mount)). Community sentiment has turned from concern to eulogy — *"32 days straight isn't a decline, it's freefall."*

## GitHub Copilot: Post-Unification Momentum Continues

GitHub Copilot's unified experience launched yesterday and the tool ticked up to 5, its second consecutive day of gains after 85 weeks at 1 ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). Major model swaps are coming October 2: Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash → Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Opus 4.7 → Opus 5, and Kimi K2.7 Code → K3 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-25-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-21/)). The break from its long stagnation is notable, but full recovery remains distant.

## Claude Code: Holds #1 on LogRocket, Fable 5.1 Dominance

Claude Code maintains its #1 position in LogRocket's September AI dev tool power rankings ([LogRocket Blog](https://blog.logrocket.com/ai-dev-tool-power-rankings/)). Fable 5.1's 1,762 Elo on WebDev Arena remains the highest score recorded for any model inside any coding tool. With Opus 5.5 (released Sep 22) cutting token costs by 40%, developer satisfaction is at an all-time high.
