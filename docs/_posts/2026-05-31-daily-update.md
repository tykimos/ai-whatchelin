June 1 is tomorrow. The day flat-rate Copilot ends and token-based AI Credit billing begins — the most critical inflection point for a tool that has declined 27 consecutive weeks to a score of 56.

## Copilot: "What a Joke" — Token Billing D-Day

TechCrunch ran a headline that speaks for itself: "'What a joke': GitHub Copilot's new token-based billing spurs consternation among devs" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/what-a-joke-github-copilots-new-token-based-billing-spurs-consternation-among-devs/)). The official community thread has drawn 400+ comments and nearly 900 downvotes. Pro gets $10 in monthly credits, Pro+ gets $39, Business gets $19/user, Enterprise gets $39/user — but a single frontier-model agent session can drain 30–40 credits before the workday ends ([TechTimes](http://www.techtimes.com/articles/317456/20260531/github-copilot-billing-switches-token-costs-today-agentic-users-face-steepest-increases.htm)). Heavy agentic users project costs jumping from $29 to $750/month. Existing Business/Enterprise subscribers get promotional allocations — 3,000 and 7,000 credits respectively through August 31. Code completions remain free.

## Microsoft Build 2026 D-1: The Polaris Counter-Narrative

Build 2026 opens tomorrow (June 2) at Fort Mason, San Francisco — Satya Nadella's keynote streams at 9:30 AM PT ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Microsoft-Build-2026-What-to-expect-from-the-June-2-keynote.1311546.0.html)). The centerpiece is Project Polaris, Microsoft's homegrown coding model built on a mixture-of-experts architecture. It reportedly outperforms GPT-4 Turbo on HumanEval and MBPP, ships with a "Code Content Guarantee" indemnifying enterprise customers against IP claims, and targets August GA ([Windows News](https://windowsnews.ai/article/microsoft-build-2026-homegrown-ai-models-to-power-github-copilot.420887)). Multimodal coding demos — sketch a UI on a tablet and Copilot generates XAML + C# — are also on the agenda. Attendance is capped at ~2,500 developers.

## Codex CLI: Pro Boost Expires, Quota Shock Incoming

OpenAI's temporary 2x usage boost for Codex CLI Pro tiers expires today ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan)). Pro 5x drops from 25x back to the 20x Plus baseline, and Pro $100 loses its doubled allocation. Community reports indicate single prompts consuming 7% of weekly limits ([OpenAI Community](https://community.openai.com/t/codex-usage-after-the-limit-reset-update-single-prompt-eats-7-of-weekly-limits-plus-tier/1365284)), suggesting post-boost sticker shock. Codex CLI drops a point to 87.

## Gemini CLI: Sunset D-18, Attrition Continues

Eighteen days remain until Gemini CLI stops serving requests for all Pro, Ultra, and free-tier users on June 18 ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)). The open-source Apache 2.0 project being replaced by a closed-source successor continues to draw community frustration. Antigravity ticks up to 60, continuing its slow post-patch recovery.
