Day 8 of the Fable 5 suspension, and today is the refund deadline. Meanwhile, GPT-5.6 prediction market odds have surged from 83% to 90% for a June 22-28 release, signaling a potential shake-up in the AI landscape next week.

## Fable 5: Refund Deadline Today — Day 8, No Reinstatement Timeline

The refund window for Fable 5 and Mythos 5 closes today at 11:59 PM PT, covering purchases made between June 9-14 only ([TechJack Solutions](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-brief/fable-5-refund-window-closes-june-20-what-anthropics-billing/)). The models remain suspended under a US Commerce Department export control directive since June 12, triggered by the SK Telecom/Project Glasswing incident. Anthropic's international MD expressed being "very confident" models would return "in the coming days" at the Seoul office launch ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318668/20260618/fable-5-export-ban-day-six-anthropic-opens-seoul-office-vows-models-back-days.htm)), but the White House demands all jailbreak vulnerabilities be eliminated first — a condition security experts call technically impossible ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/06/claude-5-release/)). AI czar David Sacks has signaled reinstatement "as soon as possible" without providing a date.

## GPT-5.6: Prediction Markets Hit 90% — Next Week Almost Certain

Polymarket odds for a June 22-28 GPT-5.6 release have jumped to **90%**, up from 83% yesterday, with over $960,325 in contract volume ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/when-will-gpt-5pt6-be-released)). The iris-alpha codename is expected to feature a 1.5M context window. OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki called it a "meaningful improvement" over GPT-5.5 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318492/20260616/gpt-56-openai-chief-scientist-calls-it-meaningful-leap-june-launch-nears.htm)). The Fable 5 vacuum creates a strategic opportunity for OpenAI to capture attention while Anthropic's most advanced model remains suspended.

## Gemini CLI Shutdown Day 2: Workaround Guides Multiply

Two days after the official shutdown, migration workarounds are flooding developer channels. CI/CD pipelines calling `gemini` continue hitting HTTP 410 errors, and the most dangerous issue remains the MCP `serverUrl` field rename that fails silently — broken configs pass undetected ([DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli-migration-june-18-2026-guide)). The replacement Antigravity CLI (`agy`) launched as free GA but caps requests at 20/day — a 98% reduction from Gemini CLI's free tier — and is closed-source, abandoning the 105,000-star open-source community ([Groundy](https://groundy.com/articles/google-sunsets-gemini-cli-on-june-18-forced-migration-to-antigravity-cli-breaks/)). Enterprise license holders retain access to Gemini CLI; individual pipelines are dead.

## Kiro iOS App Launches — Coding Sessions From Your Phone

AWS Kiro shipped an iOS app in early access, letting developers manage coding sessions and approve agent changes from their smartphone ([DevOps.com](https://devops.com/aws-previews-ios-app-to-manage-kiro-ai-coding-workflows/)). The CLI V3 preview introduces spec-driven development in the terminal with capability-based permissions and tag-based agent configuration. At AWS Summit New York, Kiro was spotlighted alongside AgentCore and Amazon Quick, using aerospace-grade EARS notation for spec-driven development ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318546/20260617/aws-summit-new-york-2026-kiro-brings-aerospace-spec-standards-ai-coding.htm)).

## Copilot 47-Week Decline — Score 36, Billing Day 20

Copilot dropped to 36, extending its record streak to 47 consecutive weeks of decline ([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/categories/copilot-news-and-announcements)). On billing Day 20, Pro plan users get $15 in monthly credits for $10/mo, but agentic sessions cost $30-40 each — some users reported burning 8% of credits within 2 hours ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/02/github-copilot-usage-based-billing-takes-effect-drawing-developer-backlash-over-rapid-credit-depletion/)). The CVSS 9.6 prompt injection (CVE-2025-53773) and newly discovered 2FA code theft vulnerability (CVE-2026-42824) continue eroding trust.

## Claude Design Beta — From Design to Code in One Flow

Anthropic launched Claude Design beta for paid plan users (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise), introducing a WYSIWYG visual canvas where UI elements can be dragged, resized, and restyled with Claude updating the underlying code in real time ([WindowsNews](https://windowsnews.ai/article/anthropic-launches-claude-design-beta-with-wysiwyg-canvas-and-code-handoff-for-paid-users.427631)). Design systems can be imported from tools like Figma, and the new `/design-sync` command provides direct handoff to Claude Code for turning prototypes into production code ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-design-june-2026-update-design-sync-2026)). Available at claude.ai/design or in the desktop app sidebar.
