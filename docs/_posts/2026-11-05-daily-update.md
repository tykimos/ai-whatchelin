Agent orchestration — running multiple AI coding agents in parallel — is emerging as the next battleground in AI coding tools. Meta just made its move by acquiring Conductor, and next week brings the Anthropic IPO roadshow and Cursor's OpenAI model cutoff on the same timeline.

## Meta: Conductor Acquired, Team Joins Superintelligence Labs

Meta acquired Conductor, a startup that runs multiple AI coding agents in parallel ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/meta-acquires-ai-coding-startup-conductor/)). CEO Charlie Holtz and CTO Jackson de Campos are joining Meta Superintelligence Labs to work alongside Nat Friedman and Alexandr Wang ([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/meta-acquires-parallel-ai-coding-startup-conductor-team-joins-superintelligence)). Conductor Cloud shuts down November 20, with data deletion to follow ([Shattered](https://shattered.io/meta-acquires-conductor-ai-coding-agents-cloud-shutdown-nov-20-2026)). The key question: Conductor orchestrated agents from Claude Code, Codex, and others without building its own models — will Meta keep the multi-provider approach or bend it toward its own models? Coming after Meta's Muse Code launch in August, this signals accelerating investment in AI coding infrastructure.

## Anthropic IPO: Roadshow D-4, Price Range Still Missing

Anthropic's IPO roadshow could begin as early as the week of November 9 ([Leverage Shares](https://leverageshares.com/us/insights/when-is-the-anthropic-ipo/)). The 15-day minimum waiting period after the October 26 public S-1 filing expires November 10, but no amended S-1 with a price range has been filed yet ([Admiral Markets](https://admiralmarkets.com/education/articles/shares/anthropic-ipo)). Some investors are pushing for a $1.5–1.6 trillion valuation, down from the $2 trillion target ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-updates-ipo-filing-investors-034041239.html)). The listing is expected before Thanksgiving on November 27 ([Bloomberg](https://news.bgov.com/banking-law/anthropic-said-to-target-mega-ipo-before-thanksgiving-holiday)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff D-7, Day 72 at Bottom

Just 7 days remain until Cursor's access to OpenAI models is fully terminated on November 12 ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). The cutoff stems from a change-of-control clause triggered by SpaceX's acquisition, and OpenAI will not ship new models to Cursor before the deadline ([VPS Ranking](https://vpsranking.com/news/ai/ai-2026-08-28-openai-cursor-contract-winddown/)). Cursor says OpenAI accounts for only 5% of traffic, but the X Subscription bundle has failed to reverse the developer exodus — 72 consecutive days at a score of 1.

## GitHub Copilot: Hits 79, 41-Day Streak

GitHub Copilot extended its recovery to 41 consecutive days at a score of 79. Momentum continues from its native Xcode 27.3 integration ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/03/xcode-27-3-adds-github-copilot-to-its-built-in-coding-assistant)), with 11 days until the November 16 model swap (Opus 4.8/5 to Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Terra to GPT-6.1 Sol) ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). The Fusion GA 30% discount promotion running through November 30 is also contributing to the upward trend.

Conductor Cloud shutdown (Nov 20), Anthropic roadshow start (as early as Nov 9), Cursor OpenAI cutoff (Nov 12), and Anthropic's usage policy update (Nov 12) all converge.
