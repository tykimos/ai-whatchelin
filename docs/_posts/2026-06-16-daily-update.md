SpaceX shattered market expectations just three trading days after the largest IPO in history, crossing $2.73 trillion in market cap while confirming a $60 billion acquisition of Cursor. On the same day, Elon Musk declared that AI will reach superhuman coding capabilities before 2026 ends.

## SpaceX: $2.73T Market Cap, Eyeing World's 4th Largest Company

SpaceX (SPCX) surged to $206.19 on Tuesday, up 53% from its $135 IPO price ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/spacex-spcx-stock-set-for-more-than-50-jump-in-just-three-sessions)). The company is now on track to overtake both Amazon and Microsoft to become the world's fourth-largest publicly traded company. On the same day, SpaceX confirmed a definitive agreement to acquire Cursor parent Anysphere for $60 billion in an all-stock deal, with Q3 close targeted pending regulatory approval ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/spacex-buys-ai-coding-startup-cursor-60-billion-rcna350262)).

## Musk: "Stockfish-Level Coding AI" by Year-End

Musk posted on X Monday: "AI will achieve Stockfish-level coding and generalized computer use" ([X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2066880262668247091)). Stockfish is the open-source chess engine that plays at a level no human can match. Applied to coding, this implies AI that takes natural-language descriptions and produces optimized binary directly — bypassing compilers and the entire traditional development stack. Coming alongside the Cursor acquisition, SpaceX's AI coding ambitions are becoming unmistakable.

## Claude Code: v2.1.178 Ships with Permission Syntax Upgrade

Claude Code v2.1.178 shipped today with `Tool(param:value)` syntax for parameter-level permission matching — e.g., `Agent(model:opus)` to restrict specific model usage ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). The release also adds nested `.claude/skills` directory support with proximity-based loading, enhanced auto mode classifier evaluation for subagent safety, and improved `/doctor` diagnostics. Developers are adjusting to yesterday's agent billing split (Pro $20, Max 5x $100, Max 20x $200 credits).

## Copilot: 43-Week Decline Hits 40 — Another Floor Broken

GitHub Copilot recorded a popularity score of 40, extending its decline to 43 consecutive weeks ([GitHub Community](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). Day 16 of usage-based billing continues to fuel developer frustration — Pro's $10/month AI Credits deplete after 2-3 hours of agentic sessions, with costs running $30-40/day for heavy users ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/github-copilot-users-threaten-exit-as-metered-billing-kicks-in/5249826)). The steady migration to Claude Code and Codex CLI shows no sign of reversing.

## Gemini CLI: D-2, Final Countdown

Gemini CLI's shutdown for individual users is just two days away ([DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli-migration-june-18-2026-guide)). Starting June 18, free and individual users lose access and must migrate to Go-based Antigravity CLI. Antigravity's popularity climbed another point to 73, but complaints about its weekly compute cap — where heavy users face multi-day cooldowns — persist.

## Fable 5: Suspension Day 4, Washington Talks Continue

Fable 5 remains suspended for a fourth day following the Commerce Department's export control order ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318342/20260613/us-government-pulls-anthropics-fable-5-offline-now-come-refunds-vanished-ai.htm)). Anthropic dispatched Tom Brown and Sarah Heck to Washington for weekend negotiations with White House officials. While David Sacks signaled willingness to lift the ban "as soon as possible," actual reinstatement is expected to take several weeks ([Octagon](https://octagonai.co/markets/politics/when-will-anthropic-restore-fable-5-access-for-us-customers/)).

## Microsoft: Work IQ APIs Go GA

Microsoft launched Work IQ APIs into general availability, giving agents programmatic access to organizational context ([Microsoft Blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/02/microsoft-build-2026-be-yourself-at-work/)). The companion Web IQ is an AI-first, model-agnostic, MCP-native search stack that returns relevant passages at nearly 2.5x the speed of the next best alternative.
