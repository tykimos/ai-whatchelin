Two days after GitHub Universe 2026 closed, the information flood continues. Anthropic's public S-1 has laid bare its financials, the first independent benchmarks for Copilot Fusion have arrived, and the first post-grandfathering reactions from OpenAI Pro 200 users are emerging.

## Anthropic: S-1 Goes Public — H1 Revenue $16.3B, IPO in Early November

Anthropic filed its public S-1 with the SEC on October 26([IPOScoop](https://www.iposcoop.com/the-ipo-buzz-anthropic-ant-proposed-files-its-s-1/)). The headline numbers: H1 2026 revenue of $16,286M with Q2 alone at $11,555M, representing roughly 14x year-over-year growth([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-anthropics-s-1-shows-16-3b-half-year-revenue-up-14x/)). The H1 net loss of $80,748M looks alarming until you note that $72,388M comes from non-cash fair-value changes on convertible notes and derivative liabilities([HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/anthropics-s-1-shows-$163b-in-half-year-revenue-a-900percent-nrr-and-an-$807b-loss-that-mostly-isnt)). The company plans to list on Nasdaq under ticker "ANT" with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as lead book-runners([AIWeekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-files-for-nasdaq-ipo-under-ant-h1-rev-163b)). Bloomberg reports the roadshow could begin as early as the week of November 9, aiming for a pre-Thanksgiving listing([ShareSansar](https://www.sharesansar.com/newsdetail/anthropics-ipo-could-become-worlds-largest-ipo-surpassing-spacexs-record-breaking-ipo-2026-10-28)). Expected valuation sits at $1.5–2 trillion with proceeds of $75–100B, potentially surpassing SpaceX's $86B IPO. Claude Code remains stable on v2.1.310.

## GitHub Copilot: First Fusion Benchmarks — Near Opus 5.5 at Half Cost

The first independent benchmarks for Copilot Fusion have dropped. Artificial Analysis reports that Fusion scores roughly two points below Claude Code (Opus 5.5) while costing about half per task([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/github-copilot-fusion-orchestrates-anthropic-and-openai-models-to-balance-performance-and-cost)). GitHub's Octoverse report reveals that 40% of all pull requests are now initiated by agents([TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/github-universe-copilot-fusion-octoverse-agents)). Enterprise admins can restrict which models Fusion routes to([TheNewStack](https://thenewstack.io/github-copilot-fusion/)), and the AI Credits-based usage billing system in effect since June is making cost optimization a central concern([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/product-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). At 67, Copilot's popularity marks its 35th consecutive recovery day, with the gap over Cursor widening to 66 points.

## OpenAI: Day One After Pro 200 Grandfathering Ends

Yesterday (October 29) marked the official end of Pro 200 grandfathering([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers)). Former $200/month subscribers now see weekly GPT-6 Pro messages drop from 200 to 100 and their usage multiplier cut from 20x to 10x([WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/09/30/openai-adds-500-chatgpt-pro-cuts-allowance-new-200-subscribers-a005-xcxwbn/)). Ultrafast access is now exclusive to the $500/month Pro 500 tier([WindowsReport](https://windowsreport.com/openai-launches-500-chatgpt-pro-500-plan-with-25x-plus-usage-and-ultrafast-access/)). The community consensus is that the Pro 200-to-500 upgrade rate over the next week will be the key metric to watch.

## Cursor: Day 66 Under X Subscription

Cursor holds at a popularity score of 1 for the 66th consecutive day. An October MEAN CEO newsletter notes the platform has multi-agent coding, mobile control, and CLI access capabilities([MEAN CEO](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11359)), but the X Subscription bundle's $30 Premium floor for Claude and GPT model access continues to block developer adoption.
