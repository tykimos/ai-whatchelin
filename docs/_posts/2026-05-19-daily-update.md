The "Super Monday" we previewed yesterday is now reality. Three mega-events are unfolding simultaneously, and Cursor just dropped its own frontier model on top — the AI coding tool market is reshaping itself in a single 24-hour window.

## Musk v. Altman: $134B Lawsuit Collapses in Two Hours

An Oakland jury **unanimously rejected** all of Elon Musk's claims against OpenAI, Sam Altman, and Greg Brockman([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/18/nx-s1-5822366/musk-altman-openai-jury-verdict-claims-dismissed)). Both the breach of charitable trust and unjust enrichment claims were found barred by the statute of limitations, and Microsoft-related claims fell with them([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/jury-finds-musk-waited-too-long-to-sue-openai-and-microsoft-finding-defendants-not-liable-on-all-claims/)). Musk called it a "calendar technicality" on X and vowed to appeal to the 9th Circuit([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/18/musk-altman-openai-trial-verdict.html)), but overturning 11 days of testimony and hundreds of exhibits will be an uphill battle.

## Cursor: Composer 2.5 — "Our Own Model Matches Opus 4.7"

Cursor shipped **Composer 2.5**, its first in-house model that matches frontier performance([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/blog/composer-2-5)). It scores **79.8% on SWE-Bench Multilingual** and **63.2% on CursorBench v3.1**, putting it alongside Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 on key benchmarks([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/cursors-composer-2-5-matches-opus-4-7-and-gpt-5-5-benchmarks-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/)). Pricing undercuts competitors significantly at $0.50/M input and $2.50/M output (standard tier). After the Kimi K2.5 scandal, Cursor went all-in on training its own model — and it paid off. Double usage promo runs for the first week.

## Google I/O 2026: Antigravity Goes Free + Gemini 3.5 Imminent

The Google I/O keynote starts today at 10 a.m. PT([io.google](https://io.google/2026/)). Based on pre-announcements and leaks: **Antigravity** launches in **free public preview** with no waitlist or credit card needed([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/build-with-google-antigravity-our-new-agentic-development-platform/)). It's an agent-first IDE with dual interfaces — Editor View for AI-powered coding and Manager Surface for orchestrating multiple agents asynchronously. Gemini 3.5 Pro is expected as the keynote centerpiece([StartupFortune](https://startupfortune.com/deepmind-employee-signals-gemini-35-ahead-of-google-io-2026-launch/)), alongside Gemini Omni video model, Android 17, and Googlebooks (Chromebook successor).

## Code with Claude London: Day 1 + v2.1.144

Anthropic's Code with Claude London runs all day today across three tracks: Research, Claude Platform, and Claude Code([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/london)). **Claude Code v2.1.144** shipped simultaneously, adding /resume for background sessions, MCP tunnel support, self-hosted sandboxes, and a startup fix for unreachable api.anthropic.com([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). An indie developer extended session follows tomorrow (May 20).

## Copilot: 15-Week Slide Hits Record Low of 68

GitHub Copilot's popularity score dropped to **68 — its 15th consecutive weekly decline**([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). With usage-based billing D-13, April usage reports have shown developers their projected costs, accelerating departures([GapVelocity](https://www.gapvelocity.ai/blog/github-copilots-new-usage-based-billing-what-changed-why-developers-are-upset-and-what-it-means)).
