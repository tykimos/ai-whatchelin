GitHub Copilot hit **50** — breaking below the psychological floor for the first time in its history. It's day 6 of usage-based billing and the 33rd consecutive week of decline. Meanwhile, two June deadlines — Claude agent billing (D-9) and Gemini CLI shutdown (D-12) — are accelerating the great reshuffling of the AI coding tool market.

## GitHub Copilot: Below 50, Copilot App Can't Stop the Bleeding

Copilot dropped to **50**, marking 33 straight weeks of decline and another all-time low. The psychological barrier of 50 is now broken. The **Copilot App** — an agent-native desktop experience — launched in technical preview at Build 2026([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/product-news/github-copilot-app-the-agent-native-desktop-experience/)), but it can't offset the usage-based billing backlash. Microsoft's first in-house coding model **MAI-Code-1-Flash** is also now in Copilot([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-02-mai-code-1-flash-is-now-available-for-github-copilot/)), but its 51.2% SWE-Bench Pro score isn't enough to stop the exodus.

## Claude Code: Agent Billing D-9, Developers Scramble to Prepare

Nine days remain until the June 15 Claude agent billing overhaul. `claude -p` (headless mode) and Agent SDK usage will split from chat subscriptions into a separate credit pool — $20 for Pro, $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317625/20260602/anthropic-ends-subscription-subsidy-agents-june-15-credit-pool-replaces-flat-rate-access.htm)). Once credits run out, automated requests stop entirely unless overflow billing is manually enabled([codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/anthropic-june-2026-billing-change-claude-code/)). Interactive use (terminal, IDE, Claude Cowork) remains unaffected.

## Gemini CLI D-12: Countdown Continues, Antigravity Rises

Twelve days until Gemini CLI shuts down. Free, Pro, and Ultra tier users will be blocked from making requests after June 18([AI Builder Club](https://www.aibuilderclub.com/blog/google-kills-gemini-cli-june-18-2026)). Antigravity CLI climbed to **65**, absorbing migration demand. Enterprise license holders are unaffected, but the backlash over Google accepting 6,000+ community PRs then locking the tool behind enterprise licensing continues to grow([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605)).

## SpaceX IPO: Pricing in 5 Days, Cursor $60B Acquisition Looms

The SpaceX IPO roadshow continues. Pricing is set for June 11, with Nasdaq trading opening June 12 at a target $1.75T valuation([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/spacex-is-said-to-plan-to-buy-startup-cursor-30-days-after-ipo)). The $60B Cursor acquisition is expected 30 days post-IPO, with a $10B breakup fee if SpaceX walks away. Cursor announced Enterprise multi-team management GA and a new Premium seat this week, maintaining its independent momentum.

## Windsurf (Devin Desktop): Hits 85, Absorbing Copilot Refugees

Windsurf climbed to **85** following the Devin Desktop rebrand. The $15/month price point and ACP (Agent Client Protocol) support are proving decisive for Copilot refugees([Devin Blog](https://devin.ai/blog/windsurf-is-now-devin-desktop/)). The ability to manage Codex, Claude Agent, OpenCode, and other ACP-compatible agents from a single command center is the key differentiator.
