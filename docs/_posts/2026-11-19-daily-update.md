Today the AI industry's map gets redrawn. Anthropic finalized its Class A offering of 980 million shares at $83 last night, well above the $70–78 range, making it the largest AI IPO in history([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/)). The fully diluted market cap reaches approximately $1.5 trillion, with the order book at 6x oversubscription and an unusually high 20% retail allocation([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-company-said-to-price-stock-at-83-begin-trading-thursday-212656452.html)). Trading begins today on Nasdaq under ticker ANT([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/anthropic-prices-81-3-billion-ipo-at-83-ahead-of-nasdaq-debut/)).

## GPT-6.2 Astra / Sol: OpenAI Fires Back

On the eve of Anthropic's IPO, OpenAI officially released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol([OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-are-now-available/1417531)). Astra targets professional work at $8/$40 per million tokens — a 20% cut from GPT-6 Astra — while Sol handles everyday tasks at $2/$10([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/17/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-and-codex-with-new-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-models/)). Both ship with tighter cyber safeguards and are available in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API as `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol`([CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-release-2026)). The timing is no coincidence — a price cut plus a new model family, dropped precisely on Anthropic's listing day.

## Codex CLI 0.170.0: Voice Comes to the Terminal

Codex CLI shipped version 0.170.0 on November 18, introducing WebRTC negotiation for realtime voice calls directly in the TUI([OpenAI Changelog](https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog)). This is the 116th release of 2026, as the terminal agent continues blurring the line between CLI and full IDE.

## Cursor: Day 8 of the OpenAI Cutoff

Eight days into the OpenAI model access termination, Cursor remains at rock bottom with a score of 1 for the 88th consecutive day([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). Despite shipping Live Apps, Voice Chat, and Multiplayer Projects this month, the paradox of innovation without an engine persists.
