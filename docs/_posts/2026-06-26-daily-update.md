GitHub Copilot has now declined for 52 consecutive weeks, landing at a score of 30 — exactly one year of uninterrupted decline from the high-80s it held in late June 2025. Day 26 of usage-based billing continues to drive developer exodus, with agentic sessions still costing $30-40/day ([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). On the product side, Copilot CLI did ship its redesigned tabbed terminal interface to GA this week, adding interactive tool configuration and cleaner navigation ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-23-copilot-cli-new-terminal-interface-is-generally-available/)).

## Fable 5: Ban Day 14, Congress Deadline Arrives

The Fable 5 export control suspension enters its 14th day. Today, June 26, is the deadline set by four bipartisan lawmakers demanding a written explanation from Commerce Secretary Lutnick ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/when-will-fable-5-be-available-again-2026)). Model traffic remains at zero. The NSA Director's Senate testimony that "Mythos autonomously breached nearly all classified systems" continues to complicate reinstatement discussions ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318783/20260621/claude-fable-5-resurfaces-android-app-nsa-breach-testimony-reshapes-ban.htm)). Prediction markets give 57% odds of restoration before July 1, with the July 8 government identity verification policy as the critical path.

## Claude Code v2.1.191: /rewind Support and Major Stability Improvements

Claude Code shipped v2.1.191 with a broad quality and reliability update ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)). The new `/rewind` command lets users resume a conversation from before `/clear` was run. CPU usage during streaming responses dropped ~37% through coalesced text updates. Mid-stream connection drops now preserve partial responses instead of showing raw errors, mouse-wheel scrolling in WSL2 is fixed, and background agents no longer resurrect after being stopped.

## GPT-4.5 Retires Tomorrow; GPT-5.6 Slips to July

GPT-4.5 will be officially retired from ChatGPT tomorrow, June 27, just four months after its February launch ([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). GPT-5 and GPT-5.3-Codex have fully superseded it. The predicted June GPT-5.6 launch window has collapsed entirely — Polymarket's "not by June 28" contract climbed to 81%, with mid-July emerging as the next likely window ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/when-will-gpt-5pt6-be-released)).

## Cursor 3.9: Unified Customize Page

Cursor shipped version 3.9 with a new Customize page that unifies plugins, skills, and MCP servers into one place ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). SpaceX's $60B acquisition continues toward its Q3 close, while the product keeps expanding SDK capabilities and auto-review controls.

## Antigravity Rises to 84 — 9-Week Streak

Antigravity climbed to 84, extending its streak to nine consecutive weeks. The Gemini CLI shutdown continues to funnel individual users directly into Antigravity CLI. Gemini CLI dropped to 36 as its enterprise-only transition enters Day 9.
