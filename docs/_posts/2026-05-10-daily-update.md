The AI coding tool war is shifting from the "editor war" to the "ecosystem war." Codex CLI introduced plugin sharing, signaling OpenAI's intent to build an agent ecosystem. Copilot enters its eighth straight week of decline with D-21 to usage-based billing. Meanwhile, Google I/O 2026 is just 9 days away and Gemini 4.0 anticipation is building.

## Codex CLI: Plugin Sharing + Remote-Control Expand the Ecosystem

Codex CLI shipped a May 9 update adding plugin sharing and a new `codex remote-control` entrypoint([GitHub](https://github.com/openai/codex/releases)). Thread pagination (unloaded, summary, or full turn item views) improves large session management. Bedrock auth now accepts AWS login credentials directly, boosting enterprise accessibility. Score rises from 78 to 79 — a three-week winning streak.

## GitHub Copilot: D-21, Eight Consecutive Weeks of Decline

Twenty-one days until the usage-based billing switch([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). With Pro/Pro+ sign-ups still paused, developer sentiment continues to sour: "You will get less but pay the same price"([Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/04/27/devs-sound-off-on-usage-based-copilot-pricing-change-you-will-get-less-but-pay-the-same-price.aspx)). Score drops from 77 to 76 — setting a record for eight consecutive weeks of decline. The gap with Claude Code (98) widens to 22 points.

## Gemini CLI: I/O 2026 D-9, Gemini 4.0 Anticipation Builds

Google I/O 2026 is 9 days out on May 19([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/article/what-to-expect-at-google-io-2026-android-17-ai-announcements-and-more-131200028.html)). An "Omni" video model has been spotted in the Gemini UI, and a Gemini 4.0 announcement is expected. With v0.41.2's voice mode and offline support settling in, the score rises from 71 to 72 — building momentum ahead of I/O.

## Snyk-Claude Security Partnership + Coder Agents Beta

Snyk integrated Claude models into its AI security platform([SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/ai/may-8-2026-ai-updates-from-the-past-week-coder-agents-launch-snyk-claude-partnership-opsera-cursor-partnership-and-more/)). A sign that security tool–AI model integration is accelerating after the vibe coding security scandal. Separately, Coder launched a beta of self-hosted, AI model-agnostic coding agents([GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/06/3288916/0/en/coder-sets-a-new-standard-for-ai-coding-with-self-hosted-ai-model-agnostic-coder-agents.html)). The pitch: keep source code on-premise, addressing the 70% of companies reportedly running agents on unsuitable infrastructure.
