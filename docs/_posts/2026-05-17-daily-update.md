OpenAI is merging ChatGPT, Codex, and its developer API into a single organization under president Greg Brockman([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-reportedly-takes-charge-of-product-strategy/)). In an internal memo, Brockman wrote the company will "invest in a single agentic platform and merge ChatGPT and Codex into one unified agentic experience for all"([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-brockman-chatgpt-codex-unified-agentic-platform)). Codex will first expand beyond coding tasks, then ChatGPT and the research tool Atlas will fold in — internally described as an agentic "super app"([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52622210/openai-merges-chatgpt-and-codex-taps-greg-brockman-to-lead-product-strategy-ahead-of-potential-ipo-r)). The restructuring comes under investor pressure ahead of a targeted Q4 2026 IPO at roughly $852B valuation.

## Monday's Triple Header: Google I/O + Code with Claude London + Musk Jury

May 19 may be the busiest single day in AI coding history. Google I/O's keynote is expected to unveil Gemini 4 and an agentic coding product to rival Claude Code and Cursor([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)). Anthropic's Code with Claude London runs as a full-day conference with Ami Vora, Boris Cherny, and Angela Jiang([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/london)). Simultaneously, Musk v. Altman jury deliberations begin — the advisory verdict could push to unwind OpenAI's 2025 restructuring and remove Altman from leadership([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/316675/20260515/openai-jury-begin-deliberations-monday-judge-weighs-altman-removal-500b-restructure-reversal.htm)).

## GitHub Copilot: 14-Week Slide Breaks Through 70

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 70, marking 14 consecutive weeks of decline. The June 1 usage-based billing transition (D-14) and removal of Opus models from Pro plans continue eroding developer trust([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Microsoft's forced Claude Code license cancellation may boost short-term Copilot usage numbers, but developer satisfaction is a separate metric entirely.

## Codex CLI: Super App Momentum Pushes Score to 83

Codex CLI's popularity rose to 83, buoyed by the ChatGPT/Codex merger announcement. Over 4 million people now use Codex weekly([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-codex-mobile-chatgpt-app/)), and mobile app availability has dramatically expanded access. The key question is whether the super app transition preserves Codex CLI as a standalone product or absorbs it.
