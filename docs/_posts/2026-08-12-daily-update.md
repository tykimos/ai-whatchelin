Cognition AI is in early funding talks that could value the company at $40 billion or more, according to Bloomberg ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/ai-startup-cognition-in-new-funding-talks-at-40-billion-value)). The AI coding startup raised $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation just three months ago, but its annualized revenue run rate has since roughly doubled to approach $1 billion ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/ai-coding-startup-cognition-reportedly-already-in-talks-to-raise-at-40b-valuation/)). The Peter Thiel-backed company is expected to raise over $1 billion in the new round, resetting valuation benchmarks for the entire AI coding agent market ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4631210-peter-thiel-backed-cognition-ai-in-talks-to-raise-funding-at-40b-valuation-report)).

## ChatGPT: Linux Desktop App + Codex on Every Platform

OpenAI shipped a preview of the ChatGPT desktop app for Linux ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/openai-launches-chatgpt-desktop-app-for-linux/)). The release supports Ubuntu 24.04/26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43/44 with both x64 and ARM64 packages. With Codex crossing 8 million users, adding Linux completes coverage across all major desktop operating systems ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openais-chatgpt-desktop-linux/)).

## GitHub Copilot: Memory + Ollama for JetBrains, MAI-Code-1.1-Flash

Copilot gained persistent memory and Ollama local model access in JetBrains IDEs ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-11-copilot-memory-and-ollama-in-github-copilot-for-jetbrains/)). Memory retains context across agent chat sessions, while Ollama enables BYOK local models. On the same day, Microsoft's MAI-Code-1.1-Flash rolled out across all Copilot surfaces — 73% cheaper than MAI-Code-1-Flash with native vision support and a 0.25x premium request multiplier ([Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/mai-code-1-1-flash-br-better-faster-at-a-quarter-of-the-cost/)). The September 1 model purge is now D-20, and the original MAI-Code-1-Flash retires September 10.

## Anthropic: Invisible Text Watermarks Spark Controversy

Anthropic began embedding invisible watermarks in Claude-generated text starting August 2, complying with EU AI Act Article 50(2) ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/08/11/claude-will-put-invisible-watermarks-on-ai-text-and-images-and-the-internet-isnt-happy/)). The marks don't affect text quality or readability and survive copy-paste, enabling machine detection of AI-generated content ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropics-claude-will-start-adding-invisible-watermarks-to-ai-generated-text-2000797759)). Heavy editing can erase the mark, and the internet reaction has been largely negative ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-claude-watermark-ai-text-police-ai-slop/)).

## SpaceX-Cursor: Deal May Close This Week

SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock Cursor acquisition could finalize this week. Cursor told employees on August 9 that the deal could close "by end of next week, or by end of August at the latest" ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4629527-cursor-says-spacex-deal-could-be-done-by-end-of-next-week---report)). Post-acquisition, the Cursor brand will likely be retired in favor of Grok branding ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/cursor-brand-name-may-not-survive-spacex-acquisition/)).
