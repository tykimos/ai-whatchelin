OpenAI today formally announced it will end its partnership with Cursor, with a proposed shutoff date of November 12 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/openai-to-end-partnership-with-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition)). The move, triggered by SpaceX's $60 billion acquisition of Cursor, marks the most significant vendor realignment in the AI coding tool market this year.

## Cursor: OpenAI Models Go Dark November 12 — "We Cannot Be Confident SpaceX Will Comply"

OpenAI stated it "cannot be confident that SpaceX will use our technology within our terms of service, based on our experience with Elon Musk's companies violating contracts" ([Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/openai-cites-distrust-of-spacex-in-decision-to-drop-cursor-partnership/)). The announcement comes just two weeks after SpaceX closed the all-stock acquisition on August 14 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/)). Cursor has already onboarded xAI's Grok 4.6 for long-running agents and visual work, and launched Origin as a GitHub alternative ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/cursor-capitalizes-on-github-frustration-launches-rival-hosting-platform/)). But this second trust crisis — following the Aur0ra ransomware incident — drops the score from 96 to 93.

## ChatGPT: DALL-E GPT Exits Tomorrow, GPT-5.6 Sol Gets 20%+ Price Cut

The official DALL-E GPT will be permanently removed from ChatGPT on August 30 ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpt/you-have-until-august-30-to-save-your-chatgpt-dall-e-images-heres-how-to-avoid-losing-them-forever)). Meanwhile, OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Sol API pricing by over 20% — input now $4/M tokens, output $20/M tokens — through November 21 ([WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/08/23/openai-cuts-gpt-5-6-sol-api-prices-by-up-to-33-percent-through-november-21-xcxwbn/)). Codex added Voice support, multi-folder projects, and a new Admin plugin ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## Copilot: D-3 — Credit Cuts + Model Deprecations + Billing Overhaul

September 1 brings a triple blow: promotional credits drop 37% for Business and 44% for Enterprise, six models are deprecated across all Copilot experiences ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-31-upcoming-august-2026-model-deprecations-in-github-copilot/)), and the August 28 billing policy update sets the stage for a unified Copilot experience relaunch around September 28 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). MAI-Code-1-Flash is also deprecated September 10, replaced by MAI-Code-1.1-Flash ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-11-upcoming-deprecation-of-mai-code-1-flash/)).

## Anthropic: Claude MHS Lets AI Control Robots and Lab Equipment

Anthropic unveiled the Model Hardware Standard (MHS) on August 27, enabling Claude to directly interface with robots and scientific/manufacturing hardware ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/anthropic-tests-new-way-for-claude-to-work-with-robots-and-scientific-lab-tools)). Alongside this, AI for Science credits and 10,000 free/discounted seats for researchers expand Claude's footprint beyond coding. Claude Code continues its steady run at the top with restricted mode, cross-session messaging, and Enterprise usage credits ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)).
