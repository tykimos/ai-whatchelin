The aftermath of yesterday's Gemini CLI shutdown is hitting developers where it hurts most — their automated pipelines. Tens of thousands of CI/CD workflows that relied on the `gemini` command are now returning HTTP 410 errors, and the silent failures are proving even more dangerous than the loud ones.

## Gemini CLI D+1: The Silent Failures Are the Worst

The real damage from yesterday's Gemini CLI shutdown is becoming clear. CI/CD pipelines, cron jobs, and orchestration bridges calling `gemini` are returning HTTP 410 errors and breaking builds across the ecosystem ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318660/20260618/gemini-cli-shutdown-takes-effect-ci-cd-pipelines-break-go-based-antigravity-cli-arrives.htm)). Developers report that the MCP `serverUrl` field rename is the most dangerous — it fails silently without throwing errors, letting broken configs pass undetected ([DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli-migration-june-18-2026-guide)). The Linux Foundation continues to cite Google's absorption of 6,000 community PRs into a closed-source replacement as a textbook "open-source bait-and-switch" ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317407/20260529/linux-foundation-tool-spotlighted-furious-developers-accuse-sickening-google-gemini-cli.htm)).

## Antigravity Rises on Gemini CLI's Grave

Antigravity's popularity score climbed to 77, absorbing Gemini CLI migration traffic. The Google & Kaggle AI Agents Intensive wrapped its final day with Antigravity vibe coding workshops ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/5-day-ai-agents-intensive-vibecoding-course-with-google/discussion/708744)). However, the replacement Antigravity CLI (`agy`) remains a closed-source Go binary with 98% fewer free daily requests (1,000 down to 20) and no 1:1 feature parity with Gemini CLI at launch ([Groundy](https://groundy.com/articles/google-sunsets-gemini-cli-on-june-18-forced-migration-to-antigravity-cli-breaks/)).

## Fable 5 Suspension Day 7 — No Reinstatement Timeline

Claude Fable 5 remains suspended under the US government's export control directive, now entering its seventh day with no official restoration date ([EisnerAmper](https://www.eisneramper.com/insights/artificial-intelligence-insights/fable-5-suspension-facts-and-timeline-0626/)). Former AI czar David Sacks signaled reinstatement "as soon as possible," but Anthropic's face-to-face Washington talks have yet to produce a concrete timeline ([Octagon AI](https://octagonai.co/markets/politics/when-will-anthropic-restore-fable-5-access-for-us-customers/)). All other Claude models (Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6) remain fully operational.

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki described GPT-5.6 internally as a "meaningful improvement" over GPT-5.5 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318492/20260616/gpt-56-openai-chief-scientist-calls-it-meaningful-leap-june-launch-nears.htm)). No official announcement yet, but the iris-alpha codename is expected to feature 1.5M context window.

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 37, extending its record streak to 46 consecutive weeks of decline. Usage-based billing is now in Day 19, with developers continuing to report burning through 8% of monthly credits in just two hours ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/02/github-copilot-usage-based-billing-takes-effect-drawing-developer-backlash-over-rapid-credit-depletion/)).
