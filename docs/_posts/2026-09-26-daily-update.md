The AI coding market is holding its breath ahead of two major events this weekend: GitHub's Copilot unified experience on September 28, and OpenAI DevDay on September 29 — both carrying the potential to redraw market lines.

## Copilot Unified Experience D-2: Final Countdown

GitHub's unified Copilot experience is now just two days away ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). Copilot Chat on github.com, GitHub Mobile, and the cloud agent will merge under a single policy, with chat data retention expanding from 28 days to account lifetime. Code review defaults shift from Lite to Balanced, which will increase AI credit consumption. Starting October 1, all new Business and Enterprise seat assignments will require prepaid billing.

## OpenAI DevDay D-3: $500 Pro Max Tier Expected

OpenAI DevDay arrives September 29 at Fort Mason in San Francisco ([OpenAI](https://devday.openai.com/)). The Pro Max $500/month plan discovered in ChatGPT's web app and Codex repository code is widely expected to headline the event, with Cerebras wafer-scale chip integration enabling ultra-fast inference ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-prepares-new-500-month-pro-max-plan-for-chatgpt/)). With GPT-6 Sol and Luna having slashed API costs by 50% just four days ago, DevDay is poised to expand the developer tooling story further.

## Cursor: 38th Straight Day of Decline, Hits 39

Cursor dropped to 39, marking its 38th consecutive day of decline with D-47 until OpenAI model access termination on November 12 ([Cursor](https://cursor.com)). Last week's Projects beta launch — featuring a coordinator agent that delegates to thousands of subagents — and the Rollouts/Security Review bots have failed to reverse the slide. Since the SpaceX acquisition closed on August 14, Cursor has shed 60 points from its 99 peak, with developer migration accelerating toward Claude Code and Codex CLI.
