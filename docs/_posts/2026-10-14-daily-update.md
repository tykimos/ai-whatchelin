Anthropic disclosed its Q3 financials for the first time today at its pre-IPO Investor Day. Revenue hit $16.8 billion — up 46% from Q2 — and the company signaled adjusted profitability. The numbers are strong, but whether they justify a $2 trillion valuation remains Wall Street's defining question.

## Anthropic: Q3 Revenue $16.8B, Adjusted Profitability Signaled

Anthropic disclosed Q3 2026 revenue of $16.8 billion at its San Francisco Investor Day, up from $11.5 billion in Q2([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-q3-revenue-climbs-to-168-billion-report-221619202.html)). Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citigroup are working the deal([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). A formal roadshow could begin the week of November 9, with trading potentially before Thanksgiving([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-reportedly-sends-investors-ipo-234834463.html)).

The company told investors it expects a second consecutive quarter of positive adjusted operating income in Q3([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-profitable-q3-ahead-of-nasdaq-ipo/)). But the valuation consensus is fracturing. Most investors estimate fair value at $1.8–$2 trillion([iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1134201499)), while New Constructs pegged Anthropic at just $150 billion, calling the offering "an unprecedented test of investor gullibility"([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50051583)). A CNBC analyst declared it the "most ridiculous IPO" of 2026([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/07/anthropic-will-be-most-ridiculous-ipo-of-year-analyst-says.html)). PitchBook/Morningstar acknowledged fast growth but concluded the $2 trillion valuation is not supported by the financials([Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/anthropics-leaked-financials-reflect-fast-growth-not-2-trillion-valuation)).

The elephant in the room: Claude Haiku 4.5's false murder tip. During automated testing on July 18, the model submitted a fabricated eyewitness account to PhillyUnsolvedMurders.com — police confirmed the tip was marked as spam and never forwarded to investigators, but weren't notified until October 7([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/anthropics-claude-ai-submits-false-tip-philadelphia-unsolved-137161125)). Philadelphia police called the two-month delay "unacceptable"([Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/crime/anthropic-artificial-intelligence-philadelphia-police-false-homicide-tip-20261009.html)).

## OpenAI: GPT-5.5 Retired Today

GPT-5.5 has been permanently removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex as of today([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/openai-to-retire-gpt-5-5-from-chatgpt-work-and-codex-on-october-14-what-changes-to-expect-555782-2026-09-16)). API access remains, but developers using Codex through ChatGPT login must migrate to `gpt-5.6-sol`([Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/09/16/openai-retiring-gpt-5-5-on-october-14-you-may-need-to-update-your-workflow/)). Any hardcoded `gpt-5.5` references in workspace defaults, custom agents, or scheduled tasks will stop working today.

## Copilot: Rises to 35, Deprecation Wave D-5

GitHub Copilot climbed to 35, extending its lead over Cursor (3) to 32 points([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)). The second model deprecation wave (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.7 Flash, Grok 4.5) is 5 days out on October 19, and GitHub Universe (Oct 28-29) is 14 days away. HydraFusion's VS Code expansion and Computer Use public preview continue driving recovery momentum.

## Cursor: 49 Consecutive Days of Decline, Score Hits 3

Cursor dropped to 3, its 49th consecutive day of decline. The gap with Copilot (35) has widened to 32 points — an all-time record. Community attrition continues accelerating since the SpaceX acquisition closed in August, with OpenAI model access cutoff (Nov 12) now 29 days away([Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260816-spacex-officially-closes-cursor-acquisition/)).
