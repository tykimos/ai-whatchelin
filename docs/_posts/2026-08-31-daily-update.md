Tomorrow is September 1. GitHub Copilot's promotional credits drop from 3,000 to 1,900 for Business (37% cut) and 7,000 to 3,900 for Enterprise (44% cut), while six models — Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 4.5/4.6, Claude Sonnet 4.5/4.6, and Raptor mini — are deprecated across all Copilot experiences ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)). Only individual subscribers on annual plans retain Claude Sonnet 4.6; everyone else must switch to Claude Sonnet 5, Gemini 3.6 Flash, or successors ([QATechTools](https://qatechtools.com/2026/08/31/github-copilot-six-model-retirements-september-qa/)). New Business and Enterprise sign-ups also reopen with mandatory credit card or PayPal payment ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)).

## Cursor: Drops to 89 — Fourth Straight Day of Decline After OpenAI Breakup

Cursor fell for a fourth consecutive day to 89. Since OpenAI notified SpaceX of its intent to cut off model access on November 12 ([OpenAI Blog](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/)), community trust has been slow to recover. CEO Michael Truell maintained that "OpenAI models serve about 5% of Cursor user traffic" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)), but the second trust crisis — following the Aur0ra ransomware incident — is accelerating developer migration. Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown pledged to "continue to increase compute to support Claude models in Cursor" ([WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/anthropic-pounces-as-openai-abandons-spacexs-cursor-vowing-to-increase-claude-compute-even-as-openai-cites-contract-distrust/)), a positive sign, but the 10-point drop from 99 to 89 in just four days reflects genuine market anxiety.

## Sonnet 5 Price Freeze Confirmed — No September 1 Hike

Anthropic made Claude Sonnet 5's $2/$10 per million token pricing permanent, officially canceling the $3/$15 increase that had been scheduled for September 1 ([Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/anthropic-claude-sonnet-5-pricing-permanent-reversal-august-2026/)). What launched in June as "introductory pricing through August 31" is now the standard rate ([Medium](https://blurbrahlab.medium.com/claude-sonnet-5-stays-at-2-10-forever-anthropic-cancels-september-price-hike-top-10-ai-518767b90c96)). Separately, Claude Code's current 50% promotional limit boost ends September 14, replaced by a permanent 25% increase — effectively a 17% cut from current levels ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/)).

## Codex CLI: Browser Extension Expands to Five Platforms

The Codex browser extension expanded to Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)). The v0.151.0 release's configurable MCP server grace period and `--approve-for-me` auto-approval flag continue to smooth the CLI workflow ([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/tools/openai-codex)).

## Antigravity: Guide Skill and Audio Rendering

Google Antigravity added a built-in Guide skill for in-tool usage guidance ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/google/antigravity)). Audio file playback (.mp3, .wav, .ogg, .m4a) is now available directly in the sidebar viewer, alongside C++/Python/Protobuf syntax highlighting and optimized file watching for improved performance ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/google/antigravity)).

## ChatGPT: GPT-5.4 Exits Codex Today — GPT-5.6 Era Begins

GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini officially retired from Codex for ChatGPT-authenticated users today ([Istar](https://istarsoft.com/guides/gpt-5-4-leaves-chatgpt-signed-codex-august-31-2026/)). The recommended replacements are gpt-5.6-terra (for GPT-5.4) and gpt-5.6-luna (for GPT-5.4 mini) ([Vellum](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-explained)). API key users retain access, but ChatGPT-based Codex sessions have fully transitioned. Separately, ChatGPT added progressive content rendering — interactive outputs now appear while still generating — and local time zone awareness ([OpenAI Release Notes](https://openai.com/products/release-notes/)).

## Claude: Infostealer Malware Hijacking Sessions

Anthropic warned that infostealer malware (Vidar, Lumma, StealC, RedLine, Atomic Stealer) on user PCs has been stealing active Claude session cookies, allowing attackers to drain paid usage quotas without passwords or 2FA ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-warns-infostealer-malware-is-hijacking-claude-sessions-to-drain-usage/)). Anthropic's own systems were not compromised; affected users are being signed out, payment methods wiped, and refunds issued ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-warns-claude-users-of-infostealer-malware-infections/)). Separately, Claude for Teachers launched as a free enterprise offering for K-12 schools and districts ([Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)).
