OpenAI's generational transition has entered its final countdown. On October 14, GPT-5.5 will be removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). The model launched just five months ago as "OpenAI's smartest frontier model," but GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol have rendered it obsolete at a pace that underscores how dramatically AI model lifecycles are compressing. API access remains unaffected, but Codex users authenticating via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol before the cutoff([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)).

## GPT-6.1 Sol: Astra Performance at 1/5 the Price, Expanding

GPT-6.1 Sol is rolling out in ChatGPT Work and Codex, starting with Pro users([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)). Unveiled at DevDay, the model nearly matches GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use, and professional work at one-fifth the token price ($2/$10/MTok)([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/29/openai-teases-20-announcements-at-devday-watch-live/)). Expansion to Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Edu is expected within weeks. Codex CLI v0.160.1 also shipped, fixing remote MCP environment handling for Windows-hosted launches([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## Copilot: Hybrid Local/Cloud Model Switching Announced

Microsoft announced that GitHub Copilot will soon automatically switch between local and cloud AI models on Windows([Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/github-copilot-will-soon-switch-between-local-and-cloud-ai)). Copilot has supported BYOK and local models (Ollama, vLLM, Foundry Local) since April([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-04-07-copilot-cli-now-supports-byok-and-local-models/)), but automatic switching opens new possibilities for offline environments and latency-sensitive workloads. Meanwhile, the October 19 model deprecation wave — retiring GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5 — is now just 10 days away.

## Cursor: 43 Consecutive Days of Decline, D-34

Cursor dropped to 13, setting yet another yearly low — 43 consecutive days of decline. The OpenAI model access cutoff on November 12 is now 34 days away. The score gap with Copilot (25) has widened to 12 points. The SpaceX acquisition ($60B) and Origin platform remain Cursor's strategic lifelines, but the daily score bleed continues unabated.

## Anthropic IPO D-5

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day on October 14 is now five days away([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). The company is targeting a pre-Thanksgiving listing, with institutional investors seeing fair value between $1.8T and $2T. Revenue of $4.6B in 2025 (12x YoY) against an $8.06B operating loss will be the key scrutiny points at the investor day.
