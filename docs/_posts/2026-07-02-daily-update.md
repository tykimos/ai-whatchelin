A day after Fable 5's dramatic return, Anthropic isn't slowing down. Claude Science, unveiled June 30, is rapidly gaining traction among researchers ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/30/1139987/claude-science-is-anthropics-newest-flagship-product/)), while Sonnet 5 settles in as Claude Code's default model. Meanwhile, Antigravity finally cracked 90, and the AI coding tool landscape continues its rapid reshaping.

## Claude Science: AI Workbench for Researchers Goes Live

Anthropic launched Claude Science in beta — a purpose-built AI workbench pre-configured with over 60 scientific databases and connectors spanning genomics, proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench)). The platform renders 3D protein structures, genome browser tracks, and chemical drawings, and runs locally on macOS/Linux or remotely via SSH/HPC ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropics-claude-science-bets-on-workflow-not-a-new-model-to-win-over-scientists/)). One Forbes researcher noted it "mapped my entire field for $26" ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johndrake/2026/06/30/anthropics-new-ai-workbench-mapped-my-field-for-26-now-imagine-it-aimed-at-the-rest-of-science/)). A grant program offering up to $30,000 in compute credits per project is accepting applications through July 15.

## Fable 5: Day One Restoration Smooth — Cloud Access Pending

Fable 5's first full day of global restoration passed without major incident ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/1/us-lifts-restrictions-on-powerful-ai-models-fable-mythos-anthropic-says)). The 99%+ jailbreak classifier is operational, with a 50% weekly usage cap in effect through July 7 ([ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/is-fable-5-back-2026)). AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure access is being restored "as soon as possible" but is not yet complete ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html)). Anthropic also joined an industry jailbreak severity framework alongside Amazon, Microsoft, and Google ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/commerce-department-gives-green-light-anthropic-bring-back-fable-5-rcna352501)).

## Antigravity Breaks 90 — 13-Week Streak, Widens Lead

Google Antigravity hit 90 on its 13th consecutive week of gains. The biggest beneficiary of Gemini CLI's shutdown, it now leads Codex CLI (88) by 2 points and has firmly established itself in the top tier behind Claude Code (99) and Cursor (96).

## Copilot Drops to 24 — 57th Straight Week of Decline

GitHub Copilot fell to 24 in its 57th consecutive week of decline ([GitHub Community](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/197089)). The first full month of usage-based billing has completed with no signs of stabilization. Agentic session costs of $30-40/day continue to drive developer exodus.

## Gemini CLI: Shutdown Day 14 — Decline Continues

Gemini CLI entered its 14th day of shutdown, declining to 25. Enterprise-only access persists, and the full Code Assist GitHub shutdown (July 17) approaches ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/17/gemini-cli-code-assist-shutting-down/)).
