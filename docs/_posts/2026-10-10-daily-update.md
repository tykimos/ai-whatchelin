October 14 is now four days away. Two of the biggest events in AI coding converge on this single day — OpenAI's GPT-5.5 retirement and Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day. Between them, Codex CLI quietly shipped a significant release, and Anthropic's IPO financials are coming into sharper focus.

## Codex CLI rust-v0.161.0: MCP Terminal Login and Voice Customization

Codex CLI released rust-v0.161.0 on October 7 with two notable features([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/r/openai-codex-rust-v0.161.0)). First, `/mcp login <name>` lets developers authenticate with MCP servers directly from an active terminal session. Second, microphone and speaker settings for voice conversations can now be customized and saved locally. GPT-6.1 Sol is the default model, and the Daybreak cybersecurity feature requires explicit opt-in via `--enable clidaybreak`.

## GPT-5.5 Retirement D-4: Migration Pressure Mounts

GPT-5.5 will be fully removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex on October 14, affecting all plans — consumer, Business, Enterprise, and Edu([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). Developers using Codex via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol (`gpt-5.6-sol`), while API key access remains unaffected([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)). GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is not yet available in Codex([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/codex-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-availability-2026/)).

## Anthropic IPO D-4: From $965B to $2T

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day on October 14 is four days out([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). The company's last private round was a $65B Series H in May at a $965B post-money valuation([fbroker](https://fbroker.kz/en/news/51473-anthropic-shareholders-expect-a-2t-valuation-following-an-october-ipo-en-2)). Investors expect annual revenue to reach $100B–$120B by year-end, more than doubling from $47B reported in May. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley are preparing a $60B+ offering after Anthropic confidentially filed with the SEC on June 1([BitMEX](https://www.bitmex.com/blog/anthropic-ipo-guide)). Listing is targeted for Nasdaq after November midterms.

## Anthropic: 'No Cruelty Toward Claude' Policy

Anthropic updated its usage policy on October 8 to ban "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward Claude, effective November 12([MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/08/anthropic-user-guideline-update/)). Common frustration, pushback, dark creative themes, and model testing are explicitly excluded([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/entertainment/claude-users-can-be-banned-for-being-cruel-to-the-chatbot-3417268/)). The update also codifies bans on deceptive political and commercial campaigns, expands weapons prohibitions, and tightens surveillance limits([RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/anthropic-usage-policy-cruel-behavior-claude)).

## Copilot: Claude Haiku 5.5 Added + October 19 Mass Deprecation D-9

Claude Haiku 5.5 was added to GitHub Copilot on October 7([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-claude-haiku-5-5-in-github-copilot)). Early tests show it performs close to Claude Sonnet 5 while requiring significantly fewer tokens and steps. Copilot CLI also shipped three releases in October (v1.0.91–v1.0.93), adding universal sandboxing and an environment picker([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/releases/github-copilot/2026/10)). The second October deprecation wave is nine days away — six models including GPT-5.5 will be removed on October 19([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)).

## Cursor: 44 Consecutive Days of Decline, Score 11

Cursor dropped to 11, marking its 44th consecutive day of decline. The gap with Copilot (27) has widened to 16 points. On October 6, Cursor added remote agent control to its iOS app — users can now see and reply to running agents from their phone without cloud agents([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). The OpenAI model access cutoff on November 12 is now 33 days away.
