Anthropic is rewriting the record books. With Q2 revenue projected at $10.9 billion and its first profitable quarter in sight, the company also claimed CNBC Disruptor 50's No. 1 spot, entered Maia chip talks with Microsoft, sealed a $200M Gates Foundation partnership, and landed a wall-to-wall KPMG deployment — five mega-deals in a single day.

## Anthropic Q2 Revenue $10.9B — First Profitable Quarter Expected

Anthropic is on track to generate $10.9 billion in revenue during Q2, topping the company's sales for all of last year in a single quarter ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)). Q1 revenue was $4.8 billion — meaning sales more than doubled in months. If the target is hit, Anthropic will post its first operating profit of $559 million. However, the company may not remain profitable for the full year due to planned compute and training spend increases. The company is currently in talks to raise at a $900 billion valuation.

## Microsoft Maia Chip Talks — Fifth Compute Source

Anthropic is in early negotiations to rent servers powered by Microsoft's custom Maia 200 inference chips, which deliver over 30% more tokens per dollar than competing accelerators ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/anthropic-in-talks-to-use-microsoft-ai-chips-information-says)). If finalized, this would add a fifth compute source after Amazon, Google, Nvidia, and SpaceX Colossus. Microsoft shares rose 2% on the news ([Investing.com](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/microsoft-rises-2-as-anthropic-eyes-deal-for-custom-maia-ai-chips-4692950)).

## Gates Foundation $200M + KPMG 276K — Social Impact and Enterprise in Parallel

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation announced a four-year, $200 million partnership spanning health, education, and agriculture ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership)) — 4x OpenAI's $50M Gates deal and the largest AI-philanthropy partnership in history. On the same day, KPMG signed a global strategic alliance deploying Claude to all 276,000+ employees across 138 countries ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-kpmg)), making it the first Big Four firm to deploy Claude wall-to-wall. Anthropic also claimed No. 1 on the 2026 CNBC Disruptor 50 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/2026-cnbc-disruptor-50-rankings-anthropic-no-1.html)).

## Claude Code v2.1.145 + Copilot CLI v1.0.51

Claude Code v2.1.145 shipped with the `/code-review` command (renamed from `/simplify`) and MCP pagination fixes ([code.claude.com](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)). GitHub Copilot CLI also released v1.0.51 with a new `/security-review` slash command for automated vulnerability detection (experimental) ([GitHub](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli/releases)). Code with Claude London Extended wrapped up with indie developer workshops.

## Antigravity 2.0 Rollback Crisis Enters Day Three — Drops to 55

The Antigravity 2.0 auto-update fiasco is now in its third day ([Google AI Developers Forum](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/antigravity-2-0-a-rushed-un-tested-release/145483/6)). A config path mismatch wipes extensions and settings, forcing manual rollbacks to v1.23.2. The score that surged to 62 on I/O day has crashed to 55 — two-thirds of the initial hype gain erased. Meanwhile, the Gemini CLI to Antigravity CLI transition deadline is June 18 ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)).

## Copilot: 17-Week Decline to 66 — Usage Billing D-11

GitHub Copilot fell to 66, marking its 17th consecutive weekly decline ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Eleven days remain until usage-based billing on June 1. GPT-5.3-Codex was force-switched as the base model, and Gemini models were purged from web chat.
