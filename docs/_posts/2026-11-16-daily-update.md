Today is the day. Claude Opus 4.8, Claude Opus 5, and GPT-5.6 Terra are being removed from every GitHub Copilot surface — Chat, inline edits, Agent mode, and code completions([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). Only annual Individual subscribers retain Claude Opus 4.8; everyone else must migrate to Claude Opus 5.5 or GPT-6.1 Sol([dev.classmethod.jp](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/github-copilot-model-deprecation-nov-2026/)). If you have hardcoded model names in config files, scripts, or agent definitions, today is the final deadline([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/github-copilot-claude-opus-5-deprecation-november-16-2026/)). In a fitting coincidence, Copilot's popularity score hits 99 on its purge day — a 54-day consecutive rally that puts it alongside Claude Code, Codex CLI, Antigravity, and ChatGPT at the top.

## Anthropic IPO: Pricing 48 Hours Out, Oversubscribed but Anxious

Anthropic's IPO pricing is expected on November 18 (EST), with trading likely commencing November 19([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-anthropics-ipo-day-by-day-pricing-18-nov-trading-19-nov/)). The company is offering 825 million Class A shares at $70–78 per share under Nasdaq ticker ANT, targeting a $61 billion raise — the second-largest IPO ever, behind SpaceX([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122657/anthropic-sets-terms-for-61-billion-ipo-targeting-a-1.2-trillion-market-cap)). The order book is multiple times oversubscribed, yet institutional investors are openly questioning whether Anthropic can sustain its AI leadership as Google and others close the gap([Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/14/exclusive-investors-flock-to-anthropic-ipo-but-question-durability-of-ai-lead)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff Day 5, Day 85 at the Bottom

Five days after OpenAI's model access cutoff, Tab autocomplete, auto model selection, and Cloud Agents remain non-functional([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). OpenAI terminated access citing SpaceX's "history of breaking contracts" after the acquisition, and CEO Michael Truell's claim that OpenAI accounted for only 5% of traffic hasn't convinced the community([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-cuts-cursor-spacex/)). The X Subscription bundle has failed to reverse the decline — Cursor marks its 85th consecutive day at a score of 1.

## Codex CLI: 0.169.0 Ships, November Release Streak Continues

Codex CLI 0.169.0 landed with terminal side-chat tabs, task search, custom model provider setup, and expanded cloud conversation controls([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)). Following 0.167.0 and 0.168.0 earlier this month, OpenAI's agent tooling pace shows no sign of slowing.

## Gemini 4 Carbon: Intro Pricing D-14, Prices Double December 1

Gemini 4 Carbon, generally available since November 10, has introductory pricing running through November 30([DEV Community](https://dev.to/aitecharchive/gemini-4-carbon-in-github-copilot-vs-argon-test-by-dec-1-4fc5)). Per-token costs in GitHub Copilot are set to double on December 1, so now is the time to evaluate. It's the second Gemini 4 model after Argon, available on AI Studio and Antigravity CLI as well.

## Copilot: Six More Models Retiring December 14

Hot on the heels of today's three-model purge, GitHub has announced another wave on December 14 — Claude Fable 5, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 4 Argon, Grok 4.6, and Kimi K3 are all scheduled for removal across every Copilot surface([AI TREND LAB](https://note.com/aitrendlab/n/nc27e6861dd15?hl=en)). Replacement models will be auto-enabled for Business and Enterprise users, though some unit prices will decrease while others increase. Budget-conscious teams should audit their model usage before the cutoff.
