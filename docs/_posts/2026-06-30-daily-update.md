Exactly 30 days after GitHub Copilot switched to token-based AI Credits billing on June 1, the first complete billing cycle has closed. The cost explosions developers feared are now confirmed in actual invoices ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319340/20260629/github-copilot-billing-shock-confirmed-agentic-users-face-10x-cost-surge.htm)).

## GitHub Copilot: From $29 to $750 — Usage-Based Reality Hits

As the first month's usage settles, staggering numbers are surfacing. Developers who heavily used agentic mode saw costs jump from $29/month to $750, with some teams reporting spikes from $50 to over $3,000 ([Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/06/04/copilot-billing-shock-hits-developers.aspx)). One developer burned 8% of their monthly credits in just 2 hours ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/what-a-joke-github-copilots-new-token-based-billing-spurs-consternation-among-devs/)). "Goodbye, Copilot" posts are going viral on Reddit and GitHub Discussions, accelerating migration to alternatives like OpenRouter, RooCode, and direct API access.

## Antigravity: Ties Codex CLI for Joint 2nd Place at 88

Antigravity climbed for a 12th consecutive week to reach 88, tying Codex CLI for second place among CLI coding tools. The influx of developers migrating from the deprecated Gemini CLI is the primary driver ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)). Its open model strategy — offering Gemini 3 Pro, Claude Sonnet 4.5, and GPT-OSS free to individual users — is proving effective.

## Windsurf: Cascade EOL Tomorrow — Final Day

Windsurf's legacy local agent Cascade officially shuts down tomorrow, July 1. Teams that haven't migrated to Devin Local risk CI pipeline breakages ([Devin Docs](https://docs.devin.ai/release-notes/2026)). Parent company Cognition recently closed over $1B in funding at a $26B valuation, with $492M annualized revenue representing 1,230% year-over-year growth ([The Agent Report](https://the-agent-report.com/2026/06/cognition-devin-1b-26b-valuation-june-2026/)).

## Fable 5: Day 18 Suspended — Restoration Timeline Still Unclear

Fable 5 remains fully suspended for its 18th day. Despite Axios reporting that reinstatement could come "as soon as this week" ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-fable-5-return-soon)), Pentagon and NSA final approvals remain outstanding. Only Mythos 5 has been partially restored for US critical infrastructure organizations ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319213/20260628/claude-fable-5-still-offline-us-clears-mythos-5-critical-infrastructure.htm)).
