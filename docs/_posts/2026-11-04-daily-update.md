If Goldman Sachs and ByteDance's access restrictions were the security turning point, Revolut's self-hosting pivot is the cost turning point. Enterprises are moving AI coding tools from simple subscriptions to managed infrastructure.

## Revolut: Self-Hosted GLM Cuts AI Coding Costs 90%

Revolut transitioned roughly 2,000 engineers from metered APIs like Claude Code to a self-hosted deployment of Z.ai's GLM-5.3, cutting AI coding inference costs by approximately 90% ([StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/revolut-says-self-hosted-ai-cut-coding-costs-90)). The setup runs on 56 Nvidia B200 GPUs. Since self-hosting in April, Revolut reports 60% improvement in coding time and 70% in lead time. These figures were shared at an AI Engineer conference talk — Revolut has not yet published an official account.

## Anthropic IPO: Amended S-1 Reveals $895B Compute Bill, Roadshow D-5

Anthropic filed its S-1 on October 26, and on November 2 the amended filing landed with major new details ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html)). The updated filing discloses roughly $895 billion in computing and data-center commitments, with ~$70 billion due within a year. Third-quarter results: approximately $17 billion in revenue and a $567 million operating loss — a swing from the prior quarter's $974 million operating profit ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html)). Some investors are pushing for a lower $1.5–1.6 trillion valuation, down from the $2 trillion target, citing AI market volatility ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-updates-ipo-filing-investors-034041239.html)). No price range has appeared in an amended S-1 yet, and the roadshow could begin as early as the week of November 9 ([Leverage Shares](https://leverageshares.com/us/insights/when-is-the-anthropic-ipo/)).

## GitHub Copilot: Hits 77, 40-Day Streak — Xcode Integration, Model Swap D-12

GitHub Copilot extended its recovery to 40 consecutive days at a score of 77. In a significant platform expansion, Apple's Xcode 27.3 beta 1, released November 3, adds GitHub Copilot as a built-in coding assistant — the first time Apple has natively integrated a third-party AI tool in Xcode ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/03/xcode-27-3-adds-github-copilot-to-its-built-in-coding-assistant)). On November 16, Claude Opus 4.8, Claude Opus 5, and GPT-5.6 Terra will be removed and replaced with Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)).

## Antigravity: Plugin Marketplace Launches with 67 Partners

Google officially opened the Antigravity Plugin Marketplace on October 29–30 ([WikiDocs](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@openwiki/35105/)). The marketplace debuts with 67 launch partners including Figma, Jira, Notion, and Stripe. Plugins install via the Customizations panel or `agy plugin install` in the terminal. A Google Cloud developer plugin offers live documentation grounding via a pre-authorized Developer Knowledge MCP server and safety/cost guardrails ([AI Agent Store](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)). This marks Antigravity's shift from IDE to platform.

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff D-8, Day 71 at Bottom

Just 8 days remain until Cursor's access to OpenAI models is fully terminated on November 12 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/openai-to-end-partnership-with-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition)). Combined with last week's supply chain attack fallout, Cursor has now spent 71 consecutive days at a score of 1. Notably, Goldman Sachs' AI tool restriction didn't even mention Cursor — its enterprise presence has effectively vanished.

## OpenAI: December Usage Pool Merge Stirs Community Backlash

OpenAI will merge ChatGPT and Codex usage allowances into a single token budget starting December for Plus, Pro, and Business plans ([ARY News](https://arynews.tv/openai-to-combine-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits-for-premium-subscribers)). Community reaction has been fierce — an OpenAI forum thread on Codex rate limits has passed 100 posts, with developers reporting hitting the cap after just 3–4 prompts ([OpenAI Community](https://community.openai.com/t/codex-usage-limit-is-reached-too-quickly-even-with-chatgpt-plus/1379502)).
