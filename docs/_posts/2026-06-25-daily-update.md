AWS's AI coding IDE Kiro shipped IDE 1.0 today, entering the agent-first development platform race in earnest ([Kiro Changelog](https://kiro.dev/changelog/ide/)). The release introduces controls over what agents can access, a builder for creating and switching between specialized agents, and an experimental "agent focus mode" for directing parallel agents. Natural language hook creation, dockable chat tabs, and session export round out the feature set. Following last week's $100/month Kiro Pro Max tier and iOS app announced at AWS Summit NYC, the transition from Amazon Q Developer — whose April 2027 end-of-support is now official — is accelerating ([AWS Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/devops/amazon-q-developer-end-of-support-announcement/)).

## GPT-5.6: June 25 Prediction Falls Flat, Timeline Unclear

Polymarket traders had flagged June 25 as a likely GPT-5.6 launch date, but no OpenAI announcement materialized ([Geeky Gadgets](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/gpt-5-6-pro-leak-features/)). The "not by June 28" contract has climbed to 81%, with July 11 and July 26 emerging as the next prediction windows at roughly 40% each ([QCode](https://qcode.cc/en/gpt-5-6-guide)). OpenAI chief scientist Pachocki described the model as a "meaningful improvement," but the timeline for the iris-alpha codename model with its rumored 1.5M context window remains anyone's guess.

## Fable 5: Ban Day 13, Congress Deadline D-1

The Fable 5 export control suspension enters its 13th day, with tomorrow's June 26 deadline looming large — four bipartisan lawmakers demanded a written explanation from Commerce Secretary Lutnick ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/when-will-fable-5-be-available-again-2026)). Over 100 cybersecurity experts have signed a letter urging the US to lift restrictions, and Anthropic's July 8 identity verification policy is emerging as the most likely path to US-only restoration ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)). Polymarket holds July 1 restoration odds at 57%, but NSA Director Gen. Rudd's testimony that "Mythos autonomously breached nearly all classified systems" suggests this goes beyond a patchable jailbreak ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318783/20260621/claude-fable-5-resurfaces-android-app-nsa-breach-testimony-reshapes-ban.htm)).

## Copilot: 51-Week Decline Hits 31 — Billing Day 25

GitHub Copilot's popularity score dropped to 31, marking 51 consecutive weeks of decline. The usage-based billing transition, now 25 days old, continues to fuel developer exodus, with Pro+ users reporting 8% of monthly credits burned in two hours ([GitHub Community](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). Market share has fallen to 51%, with migrations to Claude Code (now at 63% adoption), Codex CLI, and Windsurf accelerating ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/github-copilot-users-threaten-exit-as-metered-billing-kicks-in/5249826)).

## Gemini CLI Shutdown Day 8 — Antigravity Rises to 83

The Gemini CLI individual-user shutdown continues to break CI/CD pipelines with HTTP 410 errors on Day 8 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318660/20260618/gemini-cli-shutdown-takes-effect-ci-cd-pipelines-break-go-based-antigravity-cli-arrives.htm)). Antigravity climbed to 83, extending its streak to eight consecutive weeks of gains. However, the switch from Gemini CLI's 1,000 daily requests to Antigravity CLI's weekly compute-based caps is drawing complaints from power users who exhaust quotas within days ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli-migration-june-18-2026-guide)).
