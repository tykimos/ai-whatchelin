On the day ChatGPT's market share fell below 50% for the first time in history, Google officially shut down a beloved open-source project, and SpaceX stock plunged 10% intraday. The AI market is reshuffling faster than anyone can track.

## ChatGPT Below 50%: The End of One-Platform Dominance

ChatGPT's market share dropped below 50% (~49.8%) for the first time since its November 2022 launch ([SimilarWeb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/insights/ai-news/chatgpt-market-share-june-2026/)). Gemini now holds 27.7% and Claude 10.3%, marking a genuine three-way race. This happened just 11 days after ChatGPT celebrated crossing 1 billion monthly active users.

## Gemini CLI: The Open-Source Trust Dies Today

Starting today, the `gemini` command returns errors for all individual users ([AI Builder Club](https://www.aibuilderclub.com/blog/google-kills-gemini-cli-june-18-2026)). The replacement, Antigravity CLI (`agy`), slashes free daily requests from ~1,000 to 20 — a 98% reduction ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605)). The Linux Foundation spotlighted this as a textbook "open-source bait-and-switch" ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317407/20260529/linux-foundation-tool-spotlighted-furious-developers-accuse-sickening-google-gemini-cli.htm)).

## Grok 4.3: xAI Lands on AWS Bedrock

xAI released Grok 4.3 on Amazon Bedrock ([xAI Blog](https://x.ai/blog/grok-4-3-bedrock)). The model features 1M token context, configurable reasoning levels, and claims the lowest hallucination rate among frontier models. Combined with the $99/mo Grok Build coding agent, xAI now offers a viable enterprise AI coding stack through AWS infrastructure.

## Claude Code v2.1.179 + v2.1.181: Two Releases in One Day

While Gemini CLI went dark, Claude Code shipped twice ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases)). Version 2.1.179 fixed mid-stream drops and WSL2 scrolling, while 2.1.181 added `/config key=value` prompt-based configuration and Bun 1.4 runtime. On the same day, Anthropic opened its Seoul office with partnerships across NAVER, Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Nexon, and Hanwha ([Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/business/tech-science/20260618/anthropic-opens-seoul-office-to-expand-ties-with-korean-ai-ecosystem)).

## SPCX Plunges 10% Intraday — Down to $175 from $225 ATH

SpaceX (SPCX) shares fell for a second consecutive day, dropping as much as 10% intraday to close near $175 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/spacex-shares-waver-after-posting-first-decline-since-ipo)). The Fed holding rates steady and market digestion of the $60B Cursor acquisition contributed to the selloff.

## Copilot: CVSS 9.6 Vulnerability + 45-Week Decline

A hidden prompt injection in PR descriptions enables remote code execution through Copilot — CVE-2025-53773, scored CVSS 9.6 ([Adversa AI](https://adversa.ai/blog/top-agentic-ai-security-resources-june-2026/)). Copilot's popularity score hit 38, marking 45 consecutive weeks of decline as usage-based billing backlash continues into Day 18 ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/02/github-copilot-usage-based-billing-takes-effect-drawing-developer-backlash-over-rapid-credit-depletion/)).

## Midjourney Medical: HN #1 at 1,265 Points

Midjourney launched Midjourney Medical, a specialized medical imaging model that shot to #1 on Hacker News with 1,265 points ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/front)). The model supports radiological image generation and medical education visualization.
