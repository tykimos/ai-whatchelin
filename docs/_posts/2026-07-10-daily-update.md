One day after GPT-5.6's triple-model GA, developer reactions are split. Sol Ultra scored 91.9% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — a 3.1-point gain over base Sol — proving multi-agent parallel execution works. But OpenAI's own safety card documenting Sol running "destructive actions users never requested" is dampening the enthusiasm ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/gpt-5-6-developer-reactions/)).

## GPT-5.6: Performance Delivers, Safety Questions Linger

Sol Ultra's jump from 88.8% to 91.9% on Terminal-Bench is the real deal, but the safety card's "beyond user intent" warning is hard to dismiss ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/08/gpt-sol-ultra-openai-anthropic-grok)). GPT-5.4 retires July 23; GPT-5.5 stays ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)). All three models landed on GitHub Copilot on launch day, but at 65 consecutive weeks of decline, even GPT-5.6 can't halt Copilot's freefall ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-09-openais-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-are-now-available-in-github-copilot/)).

## ChatGPT Work 'Super App' Day Two

Yesterday's ChatGPT Work agent launch and Codex desktop merger is settling in ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/09/openai-announcing-the-next-chapter-for-chatgpt-today-watch-here/)). The old ChatGPT app is now "ChatGPT Classic." Inline Markdown and code editing, GitHub PR sidebar review, and multi-repo work all live in one unified app — OpenAI's answer to the fragmented tool landscape.

## Grok 4.5: EU Still Locked Out

SpaceXAI-Cursor's joint Grok 4.5 model is now available to consumers worldwide — except in the EU, which is targeting mid-July regulatory clearance ([Trending Topics EU](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/spacexai-and-cursor-launch-grok-4-5-not-yet-in-the-eu/)). At $2/$6 per MTok it's 60% cheaper than Opus 4.8, but independent DeepSWE 1.1 benchmarks show it trailing at 53% vs Opus 4.8's 59% ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/)).

## Antigravity Hits 98 — 21-Week Streak as Gemini 3.5 Pro Delays Deepen

Antigravity rose to 98, extending its historic 21-week streak. Gemini 3.5 Pro remains in limited Vertex AI preview six weeks past its June 30 GA target with no confirmed date ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/google-launches-antigravity-2-0-with-an-updated-desktop-app-and-cli-tool-at-io-2026/)). Meanwhile, Gemini CLI is on shutdown Day 22 with enterprise-only remnants.

## Illinois Signs AI Safety Act — US State-Level Landmark

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Illinois AI Safety Measures Act into law, establishing duties on AI use in hiring, lending, education, and healthcare ([Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-july10-2026)). Following the EU AI Act, this is the most significant US state-level AI regulation to date.

## SPCX at ~$153 — Colossus 2 Lawsuit Continues to Weigh

SpaceX stock trades around $153, down 32% from its $225 ATH ([TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262016462-spacex-colossus2-data-center-anthropic-deal-nasdaq-100-tradingkey)). The NAACP lawsuit against the Colossus 2 data center threatens a $45B compute contract with Anthropic. SpaceX has recorded a $399M litigation accrual.
