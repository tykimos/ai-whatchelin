Anthropic dropped four mega-deals in a single day. After claiming CNBC Disruptor 50's No. 1 spot, the company entered Maia chip talks with Microsoft, sealed a $200M Gates Foundation partnership, and landed a wall-to-wall KPMG deployment — the center of gravity in AI coding is shifting fast.

## Anthropic in Talks to Rent Microsoft Maia AI Chips

Anthropic is in early negotiations to rent servers powered by Microsoft's custom Maia AI chips, Bloomberg reports ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/anthropic-in-talks-to-use-microsoft-ai-chips-information-says)). The Maia 200 inference chips deliver over 30% more tokens per dollar than competing accelerators ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/26/microsoft-reveals-maia-200-ai-chip-will-use-it-in-house.html)). If finalized, this would add a fifth compute source after Amazon, Google, Nvidia, and SpaceX Colossus. Microsoft shares rose 2% on the news ([Investing.com](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/microsoft-rises-2-as-anthropic-eyes-deal-for-custom-maia-ai-chips-4692950)).

## Anthropic-Gates Foundation $200M Partnership — Largest AI-Philanthropy Deal

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation announced a four-year, $200 million partnership spanning health, education, and agriculture ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership)). The deal includes grant funding, API credits, and technical support for polio/HPV research, AI tutoring and career navigation, and precision agriculture in local languages. At 4x OpenAI's $50M Gates deal, this is the largest AI-philanthropy partnership in history ([TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/anthropic-gates-foundation-launch-200m-initiative-to-tackle-disease-and-education-gaps-with-ai/)).

## CNBC Disruptor 50 #1 + KPMG 276K Deployment

Anthropic leapfrogged OpenAI to claim No. 1 on the 2026 CNBC Disruptor 50 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/2026-cnbc-disruptor-50-rankings-anthropic-no-1.html)). Revenue grew 80x in Q1, pushing the annualized run rate past $30 billion. On the same day, KPMG signed a global strategic alliance embedding Claude inside Digital Gateway for all 276,000+ employees across 138 countries ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-kpmg)) — the first Big Four firm to deploy Claude wall-to-wall.

## Claude Code v2.1.145 + Code with Claude London Final Day

Claude Code v2.1.145 shipped with the `/code-review` command (renamed from `/simplify`) and MCP pagination fixes ([code.claude.com](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)). Code with Claude London Extended wrapped up with indie developer workshops and Applied AI team office hours.

## Antigravity 2.0 Rollback Crisis Enters Day Three

The Antigravity 2.0 auto-update fiasco is now in its third day ([Google AI Developers Forum](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/antigravity-2-0-a-rushed-un-tested-release/145483/6)). A config path mismatch wipes extensions and settings, forcing manual rollbacks to v1.23.2. The score that surged to 62 on I/O day has crashed to 55 — two-thirds of the initial hype gain erased.

## Copilot: 17-Week Decline to 66

GitHub Copilot fell to 66, marking its 17th consecutive weekly decline ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Eleven days remain until usage-based billing on June 1. GPT-5.3-Codex was force-switched as the base model, and Gemini models were purged from web chat.
