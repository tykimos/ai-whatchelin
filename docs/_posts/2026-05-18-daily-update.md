The AI coding tools market is holding its breath ahead of what may be the busiest single day in AI history. Tomorrow (May 19), Google I/O's keynote kicks off at 10 a.m. PT with a major Gemini announcement expected([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)). Simultaneously, Anthropic's Code with Claude London runs all day as a full developer conference([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/london)). And in a San Jose courtroom, Musk v. Altman jury deliberations begin — the advisory verdict could push to unwind OpenAI's 2025 restructuring([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/316675/20260515/openai-jury-begin-deliberations-monday-judge-weighs-altman-removal-500b-restructure-reversal.htm)). By Tuesday morning, the landscape could look fundamentally different.

## Google I/O: Gemini Omni Leaked + Gemini 4 Anticipation Peaks

The I/O keynote starts May 19 at 10 a.m. PT from Shoreline Amphitheatre([io.google](https://io.google/2026/)). While Gemini 4.0 isn't confirmed, reports suggest Gemini 3.2 or 3.5 is more likely([NokiaPowerUser](https://nokiapoweruser.com/google-io-2026-gemini-spark-omni-gemini-3-5-rumors/)). The most notable leak: **Gemini Omni**, a video AI model capable of remixing videos, in-chat editing, and template-based creation, has already surfaced for some users ahead of the event([Lovegen](https://lovegen.ai/blog/gemini-omni-leak-google-io-2026)). Android XR glasses are also confirmed for preview([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)). Gemini CLI's popularity sits at 78, riding pre-I/O momentum.

## Code with Claude London: Three Tracks + Extended Day

Anthropic's Code with Claude London runs tomorrow with three parallel tracks: **Research** (frontier model capabilities), **Claude Platform** (shipping production agents), and **Claude Code** (running Claude Code at scale)([AI Expert Magazine](https://www.aiexpertmagazine.com/anthropic-code-with-claude-2026-everything-you-need-to-know/)). MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a core theme across all tracks. May 20 brings an **extended session** specifically for indie developers and early-stage startups([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/london-extended)). In-person registration is closed (lottery-based), but the livestream is free for everyone.

## OpenAI: Codex Goes Mobile + Windows Sandbox

Codex is now available in the ChatGPT mobile app on iOS and Android, letting users control Codex running on a connected Mac from their phone([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-says-codex-is-coming-to-your-phone/)). It's available on all plans including Free, and weekly users have passed 4 million([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-codex-mobile-chatgpt-app/)). A new **Windows sandbox** adds firewall-backed network blocking and tighter file-write controls([OpenAI Developers](https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog)). Enterprise customers also gained Codex access tokens for CI runners and scripts.

## Cursor 3.4: /multitask and Interactive Canvases

Cursor 3.4 introduced **/multitask** in the editor for running async subagents in parallel instead of queuing them([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)). **Interactive canvases** in the Agents Window let users build dashboards, tables, and diagrams inline. Bugbot moved to usage-based billing, dropping seat fees — average run costs $1.00–$1.50 per PR([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/cursor)).

## GitHub Copilot: 14-Week Slide Hits 69

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 69, its 14th consecutive week of decline([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). With the June 1 usage-based billing transition D-14 away, Opus removed from Pro plans, and sign-ups paused, sentiment remains cold([TechSifted](https://techsifted.com/posts/github-copilot-changes-may-2026/)). The bright spot: the **Copilot App** technical preview launched May 14, offering a desktop agent experience that starts from issues and PRs([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-05-14-github-copilot-app-is-now-available-in-technical-preview/)).
