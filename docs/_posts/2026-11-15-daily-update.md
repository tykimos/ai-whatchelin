Tomorrow, three major AI models disappear from GitHub Copilot. Claude Opus 4.8, Claude Opus 5, and GPT-5.6 Terra will be fully removed on November 16 across all Copilot surfaces — Chat, inline edits, Agent mode, and code completions([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). Only annual Individual plan subscribers retain Claude Opus 4.8, and admins must verify replacement models (Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol) are enabled in policy settings([dev.classmethod.jp](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/github-copilot-model-deprecation-nov-2026/)). Copilot's popularity hits 98 — its 53rd consecutive day of gains.

## Goldman Sachs Restricts AI Coding Tools to Engineers

Goldman Sachs has pulled Claude Code and GitHub tool access from traders and bankers, limiting it to engineers only([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/goldman-sachs-pulls-ai-coding-tools-from-traders-and-bankers/)). Internal guidelines were updated in October, citing productivity and risk management concerns([CrowdfundInsider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=321098)). The move follows growing security concerns including a recent incident where malicious Claude Code extensions were distributed through the Cursor marketplace. This is the first major restriction by a Wall Street firm and could signal a broader enterprise security policy rethink around AI coding agents.

## Anthropic IPO: Roadshow Day 6, Oversubscribed but Questions Linger

Anthropic's IPO roadshow is on day six. The company is offering 825 million Class A shares at $70-78 per share, set to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT([RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3712632/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-at-70-78-per-share-launches-roadshow.aspx)). The order book is already multiple times oversubscribed, with Taiwan's Fubon Life bidding up to $700 million([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/9812aa29-cc85-47d4-a68f-946ab4e6a228)). Pricing is expected around Wednesday, November 19([BusinessDay](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-11-14-investors-flock-to-anthropic-ipo-but-question-durability-of-ai-lead/)). Yet investors are openly questioning whether Anthropic can maintain its AI leadership as competitors like Google rapidly close the gap([Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/14/exclusive-investors-flock-to-anthropic-ipo-but-question-durability-of-ai-lead)). Renaissance Capital pegs the total offering at $61 billion — the second-largest IPO ever, behind SpaceX([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122657/anthropic-sets-terms-for-61-billion-ipo-targeting-a-1.2-trillion-market-cap)).

## Cursor: Day 4 Post-OpenAI, Day 84 at the Bottom

Four days into OpenAI's model access cutoff, Tab autocomplete, auto model selection, and Cloud Agents remain non-functional([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). OpenAI cut access citing SpaceX's "history of breaking contracts" after the acquisition([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/)). CEO Michael Truell claims OpenAI was only ~5% of traffic, but the community remains skeptical([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-cuts-cursor-spacex/)). No alternative strategy has been articulated.

## ChatGPT: Chat/Work Tab Merge + Codex CLI Release Streak

OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs into a unified experience([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-finally-merging-chatgpts-chat-and-work-tabs/)). Codex CLI shipped 0.168.0 and 0.169.0 back-to-back, continuing its November release streak([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)). Version 0.167.0 added an expanded command center for managing multiple agent tasks simultaneously.
