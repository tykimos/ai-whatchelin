Apple iPhone pre-orders opened today (Sep 12), confirming the September 14 launch of iOS 27 with Siri AI ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2254005/ios-27-with-siri-ai-will-be-available-on-september-14/)). Siri AI launches as an English-only beta first, with Korean and four other languages following in October ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2986799/ios-27-new-iphone-features-release-date-beta-compatiblity-apple-intelligence-siri.html)). Support requires iPhone 15 Pro or later, and Siri now lives as a standalone app with conversation history.

## GitHub Copilot: Fable 5.1 Arrives, October 2 Model Purge Looms

Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.8 Flash are now rolling out simultaneously to Copilot users ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/)). The bigger story is the October 2 deprecation wave — Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.6 Flash, Kimi K2.7 Code, and Claude Opus 4.7 will be removed from all Copilot experiences ([DMarketer Tayeeb](https://dmarketertayeeb.com/blog/github-copilot-september-2026-model-budget-review-changes/)). On September 28, Chat, Mobile, and the cloud agent merge into a unified experience, with chat data retention shifting from 28 days to account lifetime ([Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/github-copilot-september-policy-billing-reset-2026)).

## Claude Code v2.1.269: Plugin Eval, Output Style Switching

Yesterday's v2.1.269 introduced `claude plugin eval` for running plugin eval suites with scored, reproducible results ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). The `/output-style` command enables switching output formats across session types. The September 14 weekly limit cut (17% effective reduction) is now D-2 — the 50% promo ends and only the permanent 25% increase remains ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/)).

## Nvidia/Hugging Face: $12.93B Acquisition Goes Definitive

Nvidia finalized its Hugging Face acquisition at $12.93B (~$11.9B cash + up to $1B equity retention) ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/nvidia-agrees-to-13-billion-deal-for-ai-platform-hugging-face)). With 18M+ developers and 3M models on the platform, Nvidia completes a vertical integration from silicon to model marketplace. The company pledged to keep Hugging Face as an open platform, but EU and UK regulatory reviews remain ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/03/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidias-hands/5294363)).

## Cursor: Projects Beta Day 3, Drops to 67 for 18th Straight Day

Cursor Projects beta enters its third day but hasn't reversed the decline. The coordinator agent delegating to thousands of subagents is impressive ([Cursor Blog](https://cursor.com/changelog/projects)), but with D-61 until the OpenAI model cutoff, whether product innovation alone can overcome the structural headwind remains the key question.
