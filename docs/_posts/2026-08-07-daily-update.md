OpenAI's standalone Atlas browser shuts down in two days on August 9 — just 10 months after launch. In the same week, Kimi K3 officially joined GitHub Copilot, accelerating the push of open-weight models into major platforms.

## OpenAI: Atlas Shuts Down August 9 — Browser Agent Features Absorbed into ChatGPT

OpenAI's standalone AI browser Atlas will permanently shut down on August 9([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/openai-chatgpt-atlas-deprecation-482003)). Launched in October 2025 as a macOS exclusive, Atlas lasted less than a year. Its browser agent capabilities — multi-tab support, downloads, account login — are being folded into the ChatGPT desktop app and Codex([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001371-evolving-atlas-into-chatgpt-for-browser-based-agentic-work)). Bookmarks and tab data won't auto-migrate, so users need to export manually before the deadline([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-Atlas-ends-on-August-9-Here-s-how-to-save-your-data.1358764.0.html)).

## GitHub Copilot: Kimi K3 Officially Joins — Open-Weight Models Enter Major Platforms

Moonshot AI's open-weight model Kimi K3 is now generally available in GitHub Copilot([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-06-kimi-k3-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/)). Hosted on Fireworks AI, it delivers frontier-level agentic coding at significantly lower cost. Rollout is underway across Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise tiers, though Business and Enterprise admins must manually enable the Kimi K3 policy. Reports that Microsoft is testing K3 across all Copilot surfaces([Explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/microsoft-kimi-k3-copilot-azure-testing-cost-savings-july-2026)) signal that open-weight enterprise adoption is accelerating.

## DeepSeek: Price Hike Fallout — Developer Community Scrambles for Alternatives

DeepSeek's "significant" price increase warning is now on day two of community shockwaves. Exact amounts and dates remain unconfirmed, but trust is eroding after a June permanent 75% cut was reversed and peak/off-peak pricing was introduced in July([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/deepseek-significant-api-price-increase)). Startups reliant on V4 Flash ($0.14/M input, $0.28/M output) are already evaluating Kimi K3 and Llama alternatives([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3363129/deepseek-signals-significant-price-hike-amid-surge-demand-low-cost-ai-models)).

## Codex: Goal Mode Goes GA, Prepares to Absorb Atlas Features

OpenAI Codex has shipped Goal mode to general availability across the app, IDE extension, and CLI([OpenAI](https://openai.com/products/release-notes/)). macOS users also get Appshots, letting them attach an app window to a Codex thread via hotkey. GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 mini will be deprecated for ChatGPT-authenticated Codex users on August 31, with migration recommended to GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-openai-codex-cli)). Auto-review is upgrading to GPT-5.6 Luna with an expected 10x cost reduction.

## Windsurf (Devin Desktop): Gemini 3 Pro Support Added

Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf) added Gemini 3 Pro (Low and High) as a preview([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/windsurf)). Available to Trial, Pro, and Teams subscribers first, with Enterprise expansion planned. Priority processing for GPT-5.1 models (~50 tokens/sec) has also been enabled.
