Anthropic dropped a bombshell today: Claude is actively helping develop the next, more intelligent version of itself. As of August, Claude leads 26% of Anthropic's model research and development — up from zero in February — while 90% of the company's R&D is done in "collaboration" with the model ([AP](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/09/18/anthropic-says-its-model-claude-is-helping-to-build-the-next-version-of-itself/)). Approximately 30,000 agents were doing research and engineering work as of August ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/anthropic-model-claude-helping-build-version-136547098)). The model isn't fully autonomous yet, but the six-month trajectory from 0% to 26% marks a concrete milestone in AI self-improvement.

## Anthropic: The Age of AI Building AI

"Leading" means Claude can complete most tasks "end-to-end from a high-level prompt" under human supervision, while "collaboration" means it handles "large chunks of work under close human direction" ([Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/sep/17/anthropic-says-claude-helping-build-next-version/)). This is the first official confirmation that an AI model is materially contributing to developing its own successor, sure to reignite the recursive self-improvement debate.

## OpenAI: Astra for Law Enters Legal AI Market

OpenAI launched Astra for Law, pairing GPT-6 Astra with a 230-million-URL legal search index spanning US case law, statutes, and regulations ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/)). At the highest reasoning effort, it hit 54.0% correctness on a legal research benchmark, well above Astra's 38.7% with web search alone ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-introduces-astra-for-law-with-legal-search-and-trusted-access/)). Harvey and Legora are the first API partners. AI tool verticalization is accelerating beyond coding into high-stakes professional domains.

## ChatGPT: Microsoft Word Add-In Goes Live

ChatGPT entered Microsoft Word as a sidebar add-in, enabling drafting, revising, proofreading, summarizing, and formatting without leaving the document ([The Win Central](https://thewincentral.com/chatgpt-microsoft-word-sidebar-add-in/)). Available to all tiers from Free to Enterprise, it's distinct from Microsoft's own Copilot — targeting individual creators, freelancers, and students.

## Claude Code: v2.1.276 Emergency Proxy Hotfix

Anthropic shipped Claude Code v2.1.276 to fix a critical proxy/gateway regression from v2.1.275 that caused every request to fail when `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` pointed at a proxy ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). A common enterprise setup, the bug was critical for organizations running behind gateways.

## Factory: $200M Raise at $5B Valuation — AI Coding Agent Market's New Heavyweight

AI coding agent startup Factory raised $200M, tripling its valuation to $5B from $1.5B just five months ago ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/15/factory-raises-200m-for-its-self-improving-software-development-platform/)). Backed by Blackstone, Khosla, Sequoia, and Insight, Factory is building autonomous "Droids" agents for enterprise software engineering teams ([Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L4N4571DR:0-ai-coding-agent-startup-factory-triples-valuation-to-5-billion-in-latest-funding-round/)). The scale of investment underscores how rapidly the AI coding agent market is expanding beyond individual developer tools.
