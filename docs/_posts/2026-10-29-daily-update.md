GitHub Universe 2026 wrapped its two-day run today with two market-defining events landing simultaneously: GitHub shipped Copilot Fusion as GA, and OpenAI's Pro 200 grandfathering officially expired.

## GitHub Copilot: Fusion GA, Universe Closes

GitHub made Copilot Fusion, its multi-model routing and orchestration system, generally available at Universe 2026([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/github-makes-copilot-fusion-generally-available-at-universe-2026/)). The Day 1 keynote delivered seven major announcements — Copilot Fusion, Copilot Projects (multi-repo parallel agent sessions, GA), code review Max, and more([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/)). Day 2 featured a main-stage panel with leaders from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google Cloud, followed by the closing keynote([githubuniverse.com](https://githubuniverse.com/)). Additionally, HydraFusion received a Max option on October 23, available in research preview for paid-plan users([Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/10/23/github-updates-hydrafusion-with-more-ai-models-adds-max-option.aspx)). Copilot's popularity score extended its recovery streak to 34 consecutive days, reaching 65 and widening its gap over Cursor to a record 64 points.

## ChatGPT: Decision Day for Pro 200 Subscribers

As announced at DevDay on September 29, the higher usage allowance for existing $200/month Pro subscribers officially expired today, October 29([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers)). Only users who had an active Pro 200 subscription between September 22 and 10 AM PT on September 29 qualified. The price stays at $200/month, but usage drops to the new lower limits. Ultrafast access remains exclusive to the $500/month Pro 500 tier([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/30/chatgpt-gets-500-pro-plan-as-openai-halves-pro-200-limits/)). OpenAI now operates three Pro tiers: Pro 100, Pro 200, and Pro 500.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow May Start Week of November 9

Anthropic's IPO timeline is getting more specific. Bloomberg reports that formal marketing could begin as early as the week of November 9, targeting a debut before Thanksgiving on November 26([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-delay/)). Expected proceeds are around $100 billion with a valuation target of roughly $2 trillion, though nothing is officially confirmed([Parameter.io](https://parameter.io/claude-creator-prepares-100-billion-public-offering-ahead-of-thanksgiving/)). Claude Code remains stable on v2.1.310 with Haiku 5.5 as the default model.
