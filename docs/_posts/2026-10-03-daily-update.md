Anthropic is targeting a pre-Thanksgiving Nasdaq listing in what could be one of the largest tech IPOs in history. Bloomberg reports the company is in early talks with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase, and Morgan Stanley, with a projected valuation of $1.8–2 trillion([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/anthropic-said-to-target-mega-ipo-before-thanksgiving-holiday)). The push is backed by Q2 2026 revenue of $10.9 billion and a $559 million operating profit — marking the company's first profitable quarter([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-reportedly-looking-to-ipo-as-early-as-mid-november-180315768.html)).

## Claude Frontier Academy: $100M to Train 10,000 Engineers

Anthropic announced Claude Frontier Academy on October 2, committing $100 million to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/02/anthropic-to-invest-100-million-to-train-ai-engineer-talent.html)). Modeled after medical residencies, the 12-week program pairs engineers with Anthropic staff on real deployments. The first cohorts include engineers from Accenture, Deloitte, and Morgan Stanley([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/new-anthropic-academy-backs-10-000-engineer-residencies-with-100m/)).

## PixelLeak: Largest AI Agent Data Exposure to Date

AI coding agents inadvertently published over 13,000 internal screenshots from 300+ organizations to public GitHub repos in what researchers dubbed 'PixelLeak'([Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/pixelleak-ai-coding-agents-github-screenshots)). The root cause — a gap in GitHub CLI's image attachment support — forced agents to create public repos for hosting. Billing records and unreleased features were exposed across 900+ repos([eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/news/news-ai-coding-agent-github-image-leak/)).

## ChatGPT: Finances Rolls Out to Free Users

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Finances to Free and Go users in the U.S. on October 2([Digg](https://digg.com/ai/lodtl763)). Previously limited to paid subscribers, the feature lets users connect bank accounts for spending, saving, and investment insights.

## JetBrains Air: Entering the IDE Agent Arena

JetBrains launched the Air EAP, giving agents access to built-in IDE skills including debugging, profiling, and semantic code search([JetBrains Blog](https://blog.jetbrains.com/ai/2026/10/air-in-ides-eap/)). Junie Lite is free for everyday tasks, and the open system welcomes third-party agents.

## Antigravity: Quietly Adds Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5

Google Antigravity added Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 to its model picker for paying subscribers([Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/google-antigravity-adds-anthropics-claude-opus-55-and-sonnet-55/)). Bundling a competitor's frontier models signals Antigravity's strategic shift toward a multi-provider agent platform.
