GitHub Copilot's October 19 model purge is two days away. Copilot climbs to 41 while Cursor remains frozen at 1, and the polarization between them has never been sharper. The question now: can SpaceX's freshly launched X Subscription bundle reverse the exodus?

## Copilot: Model Purge D-2, Climbs to 41

GitHub Copilot's second October model deprecation wave executes in two days on October 19([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)). Six models — GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5 — are being retired from all Copilot experiences. Replacements (GPT-5.6 Sol/Luna, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Grok 4.6) auto-enable for Business/Enterprise unless admins disabled global defaults. Combined with the Oct 2 first wave (Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash, Kimi K2.7 Code, Claude Opus 4.7), Copilot is clearing out an entire generation of models ahead of Universe (Oct 28-29). Agentic autofix also went GA, auto-fixing code scanning alerts and opening draft PRs([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/label/copilot/)).

## Cursor: X Subscription Launched, But the Floor Holds

SpaceX launched "The X Subscription" on October 13, bundling Grok, Cursor, Grok Bot, and X Premium into four paid tiers: Plus ($8/mo), Premium ($30/mo), Super ($100/mo), and Ultra ($200/mo)([Teslarati](https://teslarati.com/spacex-just-combined-grok-cursor-and-x-into-one-mega-plan)). SpaceX claims Premium replaces ~$90/mo in separate subscriptions, but four days after launch, Cursor hasn't budged from 1. The 52-day decline streak continues, with 26 days until the November 12 OpenAI model access cutoff. Developer pushback against the Grok model transition is outweighing any bundle pricing appeal.

## Codex CLI: Composer Predictions Beta — It Knows Your Next Message

Codex CLI added Composer predictions in beta([OpenAI Community](https://community.openai.com/t/codex-can-now-suggest-your-next-message-composer-predictions-beta/1404842)). After Codex finishes responding, it suggests your next message — hit Tab to accept. Works with GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, and during beta, predictions don't count toward usage limits or credits. Day 14 of the daily improvement sprint, and despite four incidents this month, Codex isn't slowing down on feature velocity.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow Likely Week of November 9

Anthropic's formal IPO roadshow is expected to begin the week of November 9([Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-could-start-ipo-roadshow-week-of-nov-9-putting-debut-before-thanksgiving/)). The October 14 pre-IPO investor day at SF headquarters drew Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citi as lead underwriters, with a $1.8-2 trillion valuation target — roughly double the $965B Series H from May([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-delay/)). A pre-Thanksgiving debut is the goal, though the false murder tip incident continues to shadow the narrative.
