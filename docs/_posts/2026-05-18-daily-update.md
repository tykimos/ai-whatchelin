The AI coding tools market is holding its breath ahead of what may be the busiest single day in AI history. Tomorrow (May 19), Google I/O's keynote is expected to unveil Gemini 4 and a major agentic coding product([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)). Simultaneously, Anthropic's Code with Claude London runs as a full-day developer conference featuring Ami Vora, Boris Cherny, and Angela Jiang([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/london)). And in a San Jose courtroom, Musk v. Altman jury deliberations begin — the advisory verdict could push to unwind OpenAI's 2025 restructuring([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/316675/20260515/openai-jury-begin-deliberations-monday-judge-weighs-altman-removal-500b-restructure-reversal.htm)). By Tuesday morning, the landscape could look very different.

## OpenAI: Codex-Spark Arrives + Super App Merges Forward

OpenAI released GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark in research preview([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-3-codex-spark/)). It is their first real-time coding model with 15x faster generation and a 128K context window, rolling out to ChatGPT Pro users first. Combined with Greg Brockman's ChatGPT-Codex "super app" consolidation, Codex CLI's popularity climbed to 84 for the second consecutive day of gains([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-reportedly-takes-charge-of-product-strategy/)). Codex mobile availability across all plans including free has pushed weekly users past 4 million([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-codex-mobile-chatgpt-app/)).

## GitHub Copilot: 14-Week Slide Hits 69

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 69, marking its 14th consecutive week of decline. With the June 1 usage-based billing transition now just D-13 away, developer attrition continues([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Microsoft's forced cancellation of Claude Code licenses for its internal E+D team (deadline June 30) hasn't helped sentiment. Opus 4.7 removal from Pro plans — retained only in Pro+ — remains a sore point([TechSifted](https://techsifted.com/posts/github-copilot-changes-may-2026/)).

## Gemini CLI: Pre-I/O Momentum Pushes to 78

Gemini CLI climbed to 78 on the eve of Google I/O. A Gemini 4 announcement could trigger a sharp spike, and the tool's free 1,000 req/day tier with Apache 2.0 open-source licensing gives it strong positioning([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/build-with-google-antigravity-our-new-agentic-development-platform/)). Antigravity also ticked up to 48 in anticipation of I/O announcements.
