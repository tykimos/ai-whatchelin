OpenAI rolled out GPT-Live-1.1 and its lightweight variant GPT-Live-1.1 Quip to ChatGPT Voice today([ChatGPT Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Meanwhile GitHub Copilot picked up pace with Gemini 4 Argon joining the model roster and local sandboxing going GA.

## ChatGPT: GPT-Live-1.1 Voice Rollout

OpenAI began deploying GPT-Live-1.1 and the mini variant GPT-Live-1.1 Quip to ChatGPT Voice on October 15([ChatGPT Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Separately, GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast mode is now available in Codex and ChatGPT Work for Pro ($500), Enterprise, and Edu plans([OpenAI Changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog)). On October 9, Codex launched Composer predictions beta — the feature suggests your next message based on the current thread, and usage doesn't count toward limits during the beta([Codex Changelog](https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog)).

## Copilot: Gemini 4 Argon Added + Sandboxing GA

GitHub Copilot added Gemini 4 Argon to its model lineup on October 13([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/label/copilot/)). Claude Haiku 5.5 is also now available to Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise users. The bigger news is local sandboxing going GA across Copilot CLI, the Copilot app, and VS Code Agent Host — limiting what agents can reach, at no extra cost([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/label/copilot/)). Copilot CLI v1.0.99 added a `--trust-folder` option for one-off prompt sessions, full-screen session sharing, and path-specific instructions. The second deprecation wave (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.7 Flash, Grok 4.5) hits October 19([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)), with Universe 13 days out. Copilot holds at 37, climbing steadily.

## Cursor: 50 Consecutive Days Down, Score at 2

Cursor marked its 50th straight day of decline, bottoming at 2. The freefall started after the SpaceX acquisition closed on August 14([FreePressJournal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/openai-to-end-cursor-model-access-after-spacex-acquisition)). Under SpaceXAI ownership, its models have shifted to the Grok family with co-trained Composer 2.5 as the flagship. The OpenAI model cutoff on November 12 is 28 days away, but the real damage is the community exodus itself, not the model loss.

## Codex CLI: Daily Improvement Sprint Day 11

Codex CLI v0.161.0 (Oct 7) made GPT-6.1 Sol the default, with patches through v0.162.1 (Oct 9) fixing a TUI crash and startup failures([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/releases/openai-codex/2026/10)). UX improvements include clickable URLs, task pinning, and git worktree management.

## Anthropic: Roadshow Prep, Universe D-13

Anthropic, fresh from disclosing $16.8B in Q3 revenue, is preparing for a formal roadshow the week of November 9([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-q3-revenue-climbs-to-168-billion-report-221619202.html)). The company is listed as a speaker at GitHub Universe (Oct 28-29) to discuss multi-model strategies for AI coding agents([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/your-guide-to-github-universe-2026-is-here-the-schedule-just-launched/)). Claude Code's latest release is v2.1.296 (Oct 9), running Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5.
