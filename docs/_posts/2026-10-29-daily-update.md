Two major events converge in the AI coding tool market today: OpenAI's ChatGPT Pro 200 grandfathering officially ends, and GitHub Universe 2026 enters its final day.

## ChatGPT: Decision Day for Pro 200 Subscribers

As announced at DevDay on September 29, the higher usage allowance for existing $200/month Pro subscribers expires today, October 29([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers)). Only users who had an active Pro 200 subscription between September 22 and 10 AM PT on September 29 qualified for grandfathering. The price stays at $200/month, but usage drops to the new lower limits. Ultrafast access remains exclusive to the $500/month Pro 500 tier, making this the decision point for Pro 200 users weighing whether to upgrade([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/30/chatgpt-gets-500-pro-plan-as-openai-halves-pro-200-limits/)). OpenAI now operates three Pro tiers: Pro 100, Pro 200, and Pro 500.

## GitHub Copilot: Universe Day 2 Closing Keynote Ahead

GitHub Universe 2026 reaches its second and final day. Breakout sessions run from 9 AM to 2 PM PT, with the closing keynote scheduled for 2 PM PT([githubuniverse.com](https://githubuniverse.com/)). Yesterday's Day 1 keynote delivered seven major announcements — Copilot Fusion (multi-model orchestration), Copilot projects (multi-repo parallel agent sessions, GA), code review Max, and more([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/)). Copilot's popularity score extends its recovery streak to 34 consecutive days, reaching 65 and widening the gap over Cursor to a record 64 points.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow Imminent

Anthropic confidentially filed its S-1 with the SEC in June, and a listing "as soon as this fall" remains widely expected([InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/anthropic-files-for-ipo-as-ai-giant-nears-1t-valuation/266819)). At the October 14 Investor Day, a $1.8-2T valuation target and November roadshow timeline were discussed. Annual revenue run rate surged from $47B in May to $65B by August. Claude Code remains stable on v2.1.310 with Haiku 5.5 as the default model.
