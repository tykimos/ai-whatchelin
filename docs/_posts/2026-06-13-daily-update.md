An unprecedented event in AI history: the US government forced a publicly deployed frontier model offline for the first time. Fable 5, hailed as "a major-version-bump-deserving step change" just days ago, lasted exactly three days — launched June 9, pulled June 12.

## Fable 5 + Mythos 5: Commerce Department Export Control Suspension

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick sent a directive to Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei ordering that all foreign nationals be barred from accessing Fable 5 and Mythos 5 ([Anthropic official statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)). Since nationality-based access separation was technically infeasible, Anthropic suspended the models for all users. The company received the directive at 5:21pm ET on June 12 and acted immediately ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-pulls-claude-mythos-5-and-claude-fable-5-following-us-government-directive/)).

The most shocking revelation: the company that reported the jailbreak to the Commerce Department was **Amazon** — Anthropic's own investor and cloud partner ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-amazon-white-house)). Amazon shared a report Thursday night showing how Mythos's cybersecurity capabilities could be unlocked through a specific jailbreak that poses a national security threat.

Anthropic called the jailbreak "narrow and non-universal," arguing it only unlocks cybersecurity capabilities in one specific instance and that the same vulnerability exists in GPT-5.5 and other publicly available models ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-fable-mythos-export-controls-national-security-threat/)). With Stripe's 50-million-line migration and NEC's 30,000-employee deployment already underway, the shockwave is rippling across the industry.

## Copilot Marks 40 Consecutive Weeks of Decline at 43

GitHub Copilot reached a historic 40th consecutive week of decline, landing at 43 ([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). On day 13 of usage-based billing, there is zero sign of stabilization. Copilot CLI shipped a broad June update with /agents polish, a unified /settings dialog, and faster monorepo search, but it hasn't stemmed the exodus ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-02-copilot-cli-improved-ui-rubber-duck-prompt-scheduling-and-voice-input/)).

## Gemini CLI Shutdown D-5: Antigravity Crosses 70

Five days remain until Gemini CLI stops serving non-enterprise users on June 18 ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)). Antigravity's popularity crossed 70 while Gemini CLI dropped to 62. The Go-based Antigravity CLI offers async processing and multi-agent orchestration, but the "bait-and-switch" criticism lingers ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605)).

## Claude Code v2.1.173-175: Governance Hardening

Claude Code shipped three versions in two days with 16 changes ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). Key additions include usage attribution in VS Code (/usage now shows cache miss, sub-agent, and Skills/Agents/Plugins/MCP breakdowns), the enforceAvailableModels admin setting, and a fix for background sessions inheriting provider environment variables. Normal service on Opus 4.8 is unaffected by the Fable 5 suspension.
