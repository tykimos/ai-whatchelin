SpaceX rewrote history on its very first day of trading. SPCX opened at $150, surged to an intraday high of $176.52, and closed near $161 — a 19% gain over the $135 IPO price([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html)). SpaceX's market cap jumped to approximately $1.96 trillion, and Elon Musk officially became the world's first trillionaire at roughly $1.05 trillion net worth([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/elon-musk-spacex-ipo-trillionaire-wealth/)).

## SpaceX SPCX Day 1: IPO Price $135 → Close ~$161

Musk and Gwynne Shotwell rang the opening bell simultaneously from NYC and Texas to kick off an explosive first trading day. The 555.6 million share offering raised $75 billion at a $1.77T valuation — the largest IPO in market history — with 4x oversubscription ($150B in orders)([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/spacex-ipo-set-to-price-thursday-night-ahead-of-friday-nasdaq-debut--heres-whats-next-101955450.html)). The Hyperliquid SPCX-USDC perpetual contract traded around $176, a 30% premium over IPO price([WEEX](https://www.weex.com/wiki/article/spacex-stock-price-135-ipo-valuation-and-how-to-trade-spcx-gcr5v4bpve9uqp8x8eqim91f)). These proceeds are central to funding the $60B Cursor acquisition signed on June 5.

## Claude Sonnet 4 & Opus 4 Hard Retirement D-3: June 15 at 9 AM PT

On June 15 at 9 AM PT, claude-sonnet-4-20250514 and claude-opus-4-20250514 stop accepting API calls — no grace period, no silent fallback, requests return hard errors([MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/claude-sonnet-4-opus-4-deprecation-migration-guide)). Migration is a one-line model string change to Sonnet 4.6 or Opus 4.8([ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/guides/anthropic-claude-sonnet-4-opus-4-deprecation-june-15-2026/)). A Claude Sonnet 4.8 builder preview is expected in the June 16-18 window([ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/claude-sonnet-4-8-preview-june-2026-dynamic-workflows-builder-guide/)).

## Claude Code v2.1.172: Nested Sub-Agents Up to 5 Levels Deep

Claude Code shipped v2.1.172 with nested sub-agents — the headline feature allows sub-agents to spawn their own sub-agents up to 5 levels deep, each carrying its own system prompt and model([DevelopersIO](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/20260611-cc-updates-v2-1-172/)). Amazon Bedrock now reads the AWS region from ~/.aws config files, and a search bar was added to the plugin marketplace([ofox.ai](https://ofox.ai/blog/claude-code-nested-subagents-2026/)).

## Fable 5 Controversy: Jailbreak Claims and Sabotage Backlash Reignite

The Fable 5 controversy reignited days after launch([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318268/20260612/claude-fable-5-hit-jailbreak-claims-secret-sabotage-backlash-days-after-launch.htm)). Red-teamer Pliny the Liberator claimed to have extracted the roughly 120,000-character system prompt. The "secret sabotage" policy was already reversed, with Anthropic stating *"We made the wrong tradeoff, and we apologize for not getting the balance right"*([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/10/anthropic-accu-claude-fable-5-limits-capabilities-ai-researchers-developers/)).

## Copilot: 44, 39-Week Decline — Billing Day 12

Copilot dropped to **44** — its 39th consecutive weekly decline([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). Promotional credits (Business $30/mo, Enterprise $70/mo) and a new Copilot Max plan at $200/month haven't stemmed the bleeding — 904 community downvotes and 10-100x cost increases for some users continue to accelerate the exodus([MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/github-copilot-switches-to-token-based-billing-june-1-drawing-developer-backlash/)).
