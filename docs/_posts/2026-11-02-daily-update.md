Day two of November, and the tension in the AI coding tool market is rising. A next-generation model reference has appeared on Anthropic's Mythos page, Cursor's OpenAI model cutoff is now just ten days away, and market-shaping events are stacking up across the first half of the month.

## Claude: Mythos 5.5 Reference First Spotted

TestingCatalog reported on November 1 that a reference to Claude Mythos 5.5 appeared on Anthropic's Mythos page, with a possible release "next week" — though timing remains unconfirmed ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-prepares-to-release-claude-mythos-5-5-model/)). Social media posts also suggest Anthropic has been A/B testing Fable 5.5 checkpoints with some users ([TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-prepares-to-release-claude-mythos-5-5-model/)). Given that Mythos 5.1 launched September 1 with access restricted to Glasswing and vetted researchers, expect similar gating for 5.5 at launch.

## Cursor: OpenAI Model Cutoff D-10

OpenAI's August 29 announcement to terminate its partnership with Cursor is now just ten days from reality. When GPT model access is fully cut off on November 12, the roughly 5% of Cursor traffic relying on OpenAI models will need to register their own API keys or switch to Grok or Claude ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/openai-to-end-partnership-with-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition)). Cursor's CEO has downplayed OpenAI's share at 5% of traffic, but the symbolic weight is heavier — Cursor sits at a score of 1 for the 69th consecutive day since the SpaceX acquisition fallout, with no signs of recovery.

## GitHub Copilot: Climbs to 73, 38-Day Streak

Copilot rose to 73, extending its 38-day consecutive recovery. The momentum from Universe 2026's Copilot Fusion GA and projects features continues to drive adoption. A November 16 model deprecation wave (Claude Opus 4.8/5 to Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Terra to GPT-6.1 Sol/GPT-6 Luna) will modernize the model lineup further ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/)).

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow One Week Away

Anthropic's S-1 registration statement was filed with the SEC on October 26, and the roadshow is expected the week of November 9. The filing disclosed $19.7B in revenue (12 months ended June 30) and a $109.7B net loss under proposed Nasdaq ticker "ANT" ([IPO Scoop](https://www.iposcoop.com/the-ipo-buzz-anthropic-ant-proposed-files-its-s-1/)). The potential timing overlap between a Mythos 5.5 launch and the roadshow has drawn speculation about strategic release coordination.

## Model Retirements: Sonnet 4.5 Countdown

Claude Sonnet 4.5 is scheduled for API retirement on November 30 ([ScriptByAI](https://www.scriptbyai.com/anthropic-claude-timeline/)). Opus 4.5 is not expected to retire before November 24. As the 5.5 generation fully settles in, legacy model cleanup is accelerating across the board.
