The biggest story today: OpenAI and Anthropic both lifted usage restrictions on the same day. Neither company wants to be the one losing users to the other's promo, and it shows.

## OpenAI: GPT-5.6 Sol 5-Hour Limit Temporarily Removed

OpenAI temporarily removed the five-hour usage restriction on GPT-5.6 Sol for Plus, Pro, and Business plans, resetting current usage for all subscribers after a 48-hour demand surge ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/13/openai-lifts-gpt-5-6-sol-usage-limits-temporarily/)). The company also deployed inference optimizations yielding roughly 10% more usage across all Sol subscriptions ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-temporarily-relaxes-gpt-56-sol-usage-limits/)). Separately, OpenAI rolled its "banked reset" feature to approximately 500,000 ChatGPT Work and Codex users — a mechanism that lets users save a usage-limit reset and activate it on demand, though each reset expires after 30 days ([Explainx](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/chatgpt-codex-5-hour-limit-removed-weekly-reset-july-2026)).

## Anthropic: Fable 5 + Claude Code Limit Boost Extended to July 19

On the same day, Anthropic extended both Fable 5 plan-included access and the Claude Code 50% weekly limit boost through July 19 — the third extension in five weeks ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/13/ai-model-wars-anthropic-extends-fable-access-again-after-openais-sol-release/)). Forbes framed the move as a direct defensive response to OpenAI's Sol launch. On Hacker News, commenters noted that "nobody has ever experienced the normal limits," raising concerns about the eventual post-promo baseline ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48883064)).

## Cursor: Honeycomb EAP Leak and iOS App Momentum

An unannounced "Claude Honeycomb EAP" research model briefly appeared in Cursor's model picker on July 8, featuring a 1M context window and "extra high effort" mode — fueling Opus 5 speculation ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48842904)). Meanwhile, Cursor for iOS is now in public beta on all paid plans, and the company's ARR has reportedly crossed $4B ([GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/cursor.com)). Cursor 3.11's side chats and agent transcript search are gaining adoption ([Cursor Changelog](https://cursor.com/changelog)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro D-4 — July 17 GA Countdown

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is four days from its July 17 GA target ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm)). A 2-million-token context window, Deep Think reasoning, and API pricing near $1.25/$10 per MTok are expected, though Google has yet to officially confirm ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a)). Copilot users must migrate off Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Flash by July 31 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-02-upcoming-deprecation-of-gemini-2-5-pro-and-gemini-3-flash/)).

## Copilot Vision GA — Can It Reverse the Slide?

Copilot Chat now supports image and PDF attachments for multimodal code reasoning ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/github)). At 68 weeks of continuous decline and a score of 13, Vision GA is the clearest candidate for a reversal, though the gap to close is enormous.
