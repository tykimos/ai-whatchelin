Tomorrow marks the pricing of the largest AI IPO in history. Anthropic will offer 825 million Class A shares at $70–78 per share under Nasdaq ticker ANT, targeting a $61 billion raise — the second-largest IPO ever, behind SpaceX([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/122657/anthropic-sets-terms-for-61-billion-ipo-targeting-a-1.2-trillion-market-cap)). The order book is multiple times oversubscribed, but Fortune reports that Anthropic is proceeding at a steeper discount than the market initially anticipated([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-lower-than-expected-ipo-valuation-smart-strategy-or-red-flag/)). Trading is expected to begin November 19([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-anthropics-ipo-day-by-day-pricing-18-nov-trading-19-nov/)).

## GitHub Copilot: Day After Model Purge, 55-Day Streak Intact

Yesterday's removal of Claude Opus 4.8, Claude Opus 5, and GPT-5.6 Terra from all Copilot surfaces went ahead as planned([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). Only annual Individual subscribers retain Opus 4.8; everyone else must have migrated to Claude Opus 5.5 or GPT-6.1 Sol([dev.classmethod.jp](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/github-copilot-model-deprecation-nov-2026/)). Despite the purge, Copilot holds at 99, extending its streak to 55 consecutive days. The next wave on December 14 will retire six more models including Claude Fable 5, Claude Haiku 4.5, and Gemini 3.8 Flash([AI TREND LAB](https://note.com/aitrendlab/n/nc27e6861dd15?hl=en)).

## Claude Code: v2.1.322 Adds Vertex Fast Mode

Claude Code v2.1.322 shipped with Fast Mode support on Vertex AI across global, us, and eu regions([Havoptic](https://www.havoptic.com/r/claude-code-2.1.322)). If Vertex declines Fast Mode, the client automatically falls back to standard speed. Fable 5.2 continues as the default model on day 8, running steadily at $8/$40 per MTok with a 1M context window.

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff Day 6, Day 86 at Bottom

Six days after OpenAI's model access cutoff, Tab autocomplete, auto model selection, and Cloud Agents remain non-functional([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/openai-is-leaving-cursor-in-november-here-are-your-3-options)). Despite a $29.3B valuation post-SpaceX acquisition, developer migration continues to accelerate. Cursor now relies solely on Grok 4.7 and Anthropic models, marking its 86th consecutive day at a score of 1.

## Gemini 4 Carbon: Intro Pricing D-13

Gemini 4 Carbon's introductory pricing expires in 13 days([DEV Community](https://dev.to/aitecharchive/gemini-4-carbon-in-github-copilot-vs-argon-test-by-dec-1-4fc5)). Per-token costs in GitHub Copilot will double on December 1 — now is the time to evaluate.
