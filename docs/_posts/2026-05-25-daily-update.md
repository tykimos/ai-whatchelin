Copilot's usage-based billing transition is now just seven days away. After 21 straight weeks of declining popularity, GitHub Copilot will replace premium requests with token-based AI Credits on June 1 ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/)). Monthly subscribers auto-migrate; annual subscribers keep their existing plans until expiry. The community's dominant sentiment remains: "You will get less, but pay the same price."

## Anthropic: $30B+ Round Closing Next Week

Anthropic is set to close a funding round exceeding $30 billion at a pre-money valuation above $900 billion as soon as the week of May 26 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week)). Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are co-leading at roughly $2B each, with Founders Fund and General Catalyst also participating. If closed, Anthropic surpasses OpenAI's $852B to become the world's most valuable private AI startup. This would be their second $30B round in a single calendar year, and with Q2 revenue tracking at $10.9B, the company's first profitable quarter is within reach ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-q2-revenue.html)).

## Copilot: 21-Week Slide Hits 62

Copilot's popularity score dropped to 62 — down 28 points from 90 in early January. Copilot CLI v1.0.54 shipped git context improvements and shell command safety filters ([GitHub Releases](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli/releases)), but billing transition anxiety and declining market share (67% to 51%) continue to weigh on sentiment ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/22/github-copilot-reliability-crisis.html)).

## Gemini CLI: v0.43.0 Stable, but Sunset D-24

Gemini CLI v0.43.0 landed with surgical code edits, session portability, adaptive token estimation, and a unified subagent protocol architecture ([geminicli.com](https://geminicli.com/docs/changelogs/latest/)). Technically impressive, but the June 18 sunset date is confirmed and developers are preparing to migrate to Antigravity CLI ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)). Score dipped to 82.

## Antigravity: v2.0.0 Patch Stabilizing — Rebounds to 54

Google's v2.0.0 patch delivered the "Open IDE" button, one-click migration tool, and a full Gemini quota reset, pushing Antigravity from 52 back up to 54 ([Piunikaweb](https://www.piunikaweb.com/2026/05/24/antigravity-v2-patch/)). The AI Ultra $100 credit offer expires today (May 25), likely driving a last-minute signup wave over the weekend.

## PwC-Anthropic Alliance — Two Big Four Firms Now Run Claude Wall-to-Wall

PwC is rolling out Claude Code and Cowork to hundreds of thousands of professionals and certifying 30,000 on Claude ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/anthropic-pwc-expand-alliance-agentic-enterprise.html)). Following KPMG's 276,000-employee deployment, this makes two Big Four firms running Claude at full scale. Insurance underwriting cycles compressed from ten weeks to ten days. Claude's enterprise dominance is hardening.
