Three countdowns are running simultaneously in the AI coding tool market. Anthropic's IPO roadshow is two days from launch, Cursor's OpenAI model access ends in five days, and GitHub Copilot's model swap arrives in nine. Meanwhile, ChatGPT Images 2.6 brought GIF creation and Devin Desktop shipped a new CLI agent — all fronts are active this week.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow Expected Week of November 9

After filing its public S-1 with the SEC on October 26, Anthropic's IPO roadshow is expected to begin the week of November 9 ([Implicator AI](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-could-start-ipo-roadshow-week-of-nov-9-putting-debut-before-thanksgiving/)). Under SEC 15-day rules, the earliest pricing date is November 13, with first trading possible on November 16 ([SmartScope](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/anthropic-ipo-s1-filing-nasdaq-ant-2026/)). The company plans to list on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, but institutional investors are pushing for a valuation cut to $1.5-1.6T against management's $2T target — the final price will be closely watched ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-delay/)).

## Cursor: OpenAI Cutoff D-5, Day 74 at Bottom

Five days remain until OpenAI fully terminates Cursor's model access on November 12 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)). OpenAI cited compliance concerns following SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/spacex-to-acquire-cursor-for-60b-in-stock-days-after-blockbuster-ipo/)). Cursor's CEO said OpenAI models account for only about 5% of total traffic, but the symbolic weight is significant ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2246969/openai-pull-its-models-from-cursor-due-to-spacexai-acquisition/)). The pivot to Grok 4.7 and Anthropic models continues, with Anthropic pledging to increase Claude compute in Cursor.

## ChatGPT Images 2.6: The GIF Era Begins

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Images 2.6 on November 4, introducing GIF creation and editing ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/chatgpt-adds-animated-gif-creation-and-editing-with-images-2-6/)). Users can start from a text prompt or photo, then adjust playback speed, revise individual frames, and save characters or pets as reference images for reuse ([XDA](https://www.xda-developers.com/chatgpt-is-getting-gif-generation-tools-to-make-creating-animated-images-a-lot-easier/)). Two API models are available: Flare (speed-focused) and Sunburst (higher quality). OpenAI reports approximately 4 billion images per week across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API ([CravingTech](https://www.cravingtech.com/chatgpt-images-2-6-brings-gif-creation-faster-image-generation-and-more-editing-controls.html)).

## Devin Desktop: New CLI Agent Launched

Cognition added a new local CLI agent to Devin Desktop on November 6 ([Windsurf Changelog](https://windsurf.com/changelog)). Existing Windsurf subscribers can use it at no additional cost, and it runs on the user's local machine. Since the June rebrand from Windsurf to Devin Desktop, the product has been doubling down on its agent-first direction with the Agent Command Center and Devin Local (the Rust-rewritten successor to Cascade) ([APIdog](https://apidog.com/blog/whats-new-in-devin-2026/)).

## GitHub Copilot: 43-Day Streak, Hits 83

GitHub Copilot extends its winning streak to 43 consecutive days, reaching a score of 83. The November 16 model swap — removing Claude Opus 4.8/5 and GPT-5.6 Terra in favor of Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol — is now 9 days away ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-november)). Its integration as a built-in coding assistant in Xcode 27.3 beta 1 marks a significant milestone in the Apple ecosystem ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/)).

## OpenAI Dots: Default Activation in 11 Days

OpenAI's always-on Dots agents will become default in Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces on November 18 ([OpenAI Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Admins who don't opt out will see Dots automatically enabled, underscoring the growing importance of enterprise agent governance.
