The AI coding tool market is in upheaval as multiple deadlines converge in mid-June. Gemini CLI shuts down in 5 days, Claude legacy models retire in 2 days, Anthropic's agent billing split in 2 days — developers are handling several migrations simultaneously.

## Gemini CLI Shutdown D-5: Antigravity Crosses 70

Gemini CLI will stop serving non-enterprise users on June 18, just five days away ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)). The migration to Antigravity CLI has entered its final stretch — Antigravity's popularity score crossed 70 while Gemini CLI dropped to 62. The new Go-based Antigravity CLI offers native multi-agent orchestration and faster response times, but the Linux Foundation's "bait-and-switch" criticism continues to shadow the transition ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/20/bye-bye-gemini-cli-google-nudges-devs-toward-antigravity/5243605)).

## Copilot Marks 40 Consecutive Weeks of Decline at 43

GitHub Copilot hit **43** — a historic 40th consecutive week of decline ([GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/192948)). On day 13 of usage-based billing, there is zero sign of stabilization. Developers continue migrating to Claude Code ($20/mo flat) and Codex CLI (API pricing), and promotional credits (Business $30, Enterprise $70) have failed to stem the exodus ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/31/github-copilot-usage-based-billing-changes/)).

## GPT-5.2 Fully Removed from ChatGPT

As of June 12, GPT-5.2 Instant, Thinking, and Pro are no longer available in ChatGPT ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Existing conversations automatically continue on the corresponding GPT-5.5 model. Combined with GPT-4.5 (June 27) and o3 (August 26) retirements, OpenAI is accelerating consolidation around the GPT-5.5 family.

## Codex Integrated into ChatGPT — 6 Role Plugins

OpenAI merged Codex directly into ChatGPT with six role-specific plugins ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/02/openai-putting-codex-inside-chatgpt-app-everywhere-releasing-6-business-plugins/)). Data Analytics, Sales, Product Design, and three financial plugins connect to 62 business apps with 110 pre-built skills. Windows Computer Use and macOS Appshots extend agent capabilities beyond code ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-every-role-tool-workflow/)). Codex is pivoting from coding-only to general-purpose work agent.

## Claude Sonnet 4 & Opus 4 Retirement D-2

Claude Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 will be permanently retired at 9AM PT on June 15 — no grace period, API requests will return errors immediately ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/overview)). On the same day, Anthropic's agent billing credit pool split takes effect, routing Agent SDK/CLI usage to a separate credit pool. This weekend is the last practical migration window.

## SpaceX SPCX Day 2: Stabilizing Near $161

After its historic 19% first-day surge, SPCX is stabilizing around $161 ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/equities/spacex)). Musk's net worth remains above $1.05 trillion, cementing his status as the world's first trillionaire. The IPO proceeds will fund execution of the $60B Cursor acquisition.
