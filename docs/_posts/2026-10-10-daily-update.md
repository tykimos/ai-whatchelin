October 14 is now four days away. Two of the biggest events in AI coding converge on this single day — OpenAI's GPT-5.5 retirement and Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day. A frontier model going obsolete in five months alongside a $2T IPO roadshow happening on the same date captures just how fast this industry is moving.

## GPT-5.5 Retirement D-4: Migration Pressure Mounts

GPT-5.5 will be fully removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex on October 14([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). Developers using Codex via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol, though API key access remains unaffected([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)). GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast mode remains in limbo — OpenAI product lead Tibo Sottiaux confirmed "6.1 coming soon" on October 4 but gave no ship date([RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-says-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-is-coming-soon)). Codex CLI v0.160.1 continues stable operations([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## Anthropic IPO Investor Day D-4: The $2T Test

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day on October 14 is four days out([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). Underwriters have told institutional investors the company could raise $100B or more at a valuation near $2T([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/node/10581)). Anthropic has reportedly indicated its 2026 revenue run rate could reach $100–120B by year-end. Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan are leading the book, with listing targeted after the November midterms.

## Copilot: October 19 Mass Deprecation D-9

GitHub Copilot's second October deprecation wave is nine days away. Six models — GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5 — will be removed on October 19([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)). Replacements are GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.6 Luna, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Grok 4.6([QCode](https://qcode.cc/en/github-copilot-model-retirement-2026-10-19)). Two mass deprecation waves in a single month is unprecedented.

## Cursor: 44 Consecutive Days of Decline, Score 11

Cursor dropped to 11, marking its 44th consecutive day of decline. The gap with Copilot (27) has widened to 16 points. The OpenAI model access cutoff on November 12 is now 33 days away. The SpaceX acquisition ($60B) closed on August 15([CrowdFund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=297180)), but brand integration plans and the Origin platform launch remain unclear.
