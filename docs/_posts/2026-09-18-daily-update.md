OpenAI made its most aggressive move into the legal market yet. On September 17, it unveiled "Astra for Law," a GPT-6 Astra configuration purpose-built for legal research with a 230-million-URL legal index ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/)). The same day, ChatGPT landed inside Microsoft Word as a sidebar add-in, and Anthropic rushed out a critical proxy hotfix for Claude Code.

## OpenAI: Astra for Law Enters the Legal AI Market

OpenAI launched Astra for Law, pairing GPT-6 Astra with a dedicated legal search index spanning US case law, statutes, regulations, court rules, and administrative decisions ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/17/openai-launches-astra-for-law-a-gpt-6-configuration-for-legal-research/)). At the highest reasoning effort, it hit 54.0% correctness on a legal research benchmark — well above the 38.7% that Astra achieves with web search alone ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-introduces-astra-for-law-with-legal-search-and-trusted-access/)). Harvey and Legora are the first API partners, with initial access through Trusted Access in ChatGPT and Codex for selected law firms. The move signals AI tool verticalization is accelerating beyond coding into high-stakes professional domains.

## ChatGPT: Microsoft Word Add-In Goes Live

ChatGPT entered Microsoft Word as a sidebar add-in on September 17, enabling drafting, revising, proofreading, summarizing, and formatting without leaving the document ([The Win Central](https://thewincentral.com/chatgpt-microsoft-word-sidebar-add-in/)). Available to all ChatGPT tiers from Free to Enterprise, it's installable from the Microsoft Marketplace. This is distinct from Microsoft's own Copilot — targeting individual creators, freelancers, and students rather than enterprise workflows. With Excel and PowerPoint already covered, ChatGPT's penetration of the Office ecosystem is deepening.

## Claude Code: v2.1.276 Emergency Proxy Hotfix

Anthropic shipped Claude Code v2.1.276 today to fix a critical regression from v2.1.275 ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude-code)). The bug caused every request to fail with `400 Input tag 'advisor_20260301'` when `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` pointed at a proxy or gateway — a common enterprise setup. The underlying v2.1.275 release brought useful features including a Ctrl+Enter send-now key for interrupting turns, claude.ai skills synced to terminal sessions, and various stability fixes, but the proxy regression demanded an immediate follow-up.

## Cursor: 55 Points, Day 29 of Decline

Cursor hit 55, extending its streak to 29 consecutive days of decline. The fall from 99 started after the SpaceX acquisition closed on August 14 and shows no sign of stabilizing ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/)). With 55 days remaining until OpenAI's November 12 model access cutoff, the 50-point psychological floor is approaching fast.
