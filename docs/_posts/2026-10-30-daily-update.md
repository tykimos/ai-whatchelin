One day after GitHub Universe 2026 wrapped, the industry is digesting yesterday's Copilot Fusion GA announcement and the seven major product launches that accompanied it. Copilot's popularity score hit 67 on its 35th consecutive recovery day, and the AI coding tool landscape continues to shift.

## GitHub Copilot: Universe Aftershock, 35-Day Streak

Developer reactions to Copilot Fusion GA are flooding in. Yesterday's Universe Day 2 featured a main-stage panel with leaders from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google Cloud discussing the multi-model era's direction([githubuniverse.com](https://githubuniverse.com/)). Copilot Projects, the multi-repo parallel agent system, also went GA, significantly expanding use cases for large-scale projects([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/)). At 67, Copilot's popularity gap over Cursor widens to 66 points, accelerating its recovery momentum.

## Anthropic: IPO Roadshow Eyed for Week of November 9

Anthropic's IPO timeline is crystallizing rapidly. Bloomberg reports that formal marketing could begin as early as the week of November 9, targeting a debut before Thanksgiving on November 26([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-delay/)). Expected proceeds are around $100 billion with a valuation target of roughly $2 trillion, though nothing is officially confirmed([Parameter.io](https://parameter.io/claude-creator-prepares-100-billion-public-offering-ahead-of-thanksgiving/)). Claude Code remains stable on v2.1.310, with the Code Review feature having reached GA on October 15.

## Codex CLI: GPT-6.1 Sol Default Model Settling In

Codex CLI has been running smoothly since switching to GPT-6.1 Sol as its default model on October 7([havoptic.com](https://www.havoptic.com/releases/openai-codex/2026/10)). The latest release is rust-v0.162.1 (October 9), and GPT-5.5 was retired from ChatGPT and Codex on October 14. With yesterday's Pro 200 grandfathering expiration, usage patterns under the three-tier Pro 100/200/500 structure are worth watching.

## Cursor: Day 65 at the Bottom Under X Subscription

Cursor remains stuck at a popularity score of 1 for the 65th consecutive day since transitioning to the X Subscription bundle. The $8 Plus tier's Grok-only model access isn't retaining developers, and the $30 Premium minimum for Claude and GPT models continues to act as a barrier([jetadmin.io](https://www.jetadmin.io/blog/untitled-31/)).
