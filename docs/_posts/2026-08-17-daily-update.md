The AI infrastructure landscape is shifting fast. Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire AI model routing startup OpenRouter for more than $7 billion ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)) — a 5x+ markup from OpenRouter's $1.3B Series B valuation in May. With 8 million users accessing 400+ AI models, OpenRouter's routing and billing technology is set to be integrated into Stripe's payment infrastructure, potentially establishing a new standard for AI service billing ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/)).

## DeepSeek: API Prices Surge Up to 1,100% Ahead of Potential IPO

DeepSeek raised API prices dramatically as of 16:00 UTC on August 16 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/deepseek-increases-prices-for-ai-services-by-multiple-times)). V4-Flash output tokens jumped from $0.28 to $1.32/M at peak hours (371% increase), while V4-Pro output rose from $0.87 to $3.96/M (355% increase) ([Quartz](https://qz.com/deepseek-api-price-increase-v4-peak-off-peak-081326)). A new peak/off-peak dual billing structure was introduced, widely interpreted as a monetization push ahead of a potential DeepSeek IPO ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/deepseek-raising-api-prices-1-174027670.html)).

## Cursor: First Major Feature Drop After SpaceX Acquisition

Days after the $60B SpaceX acquisition closed, Cursor shipped a wave of features ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/cursor)). Cloud Agent Builds prepare ready-to-use dev environments in the background, making agent startups up to 3x faster. Side Chats enable parallel agent conversations, and Conversation Search lets users browse long transcripts. Cursor for iPad also launched alongside Google Workspace Plugins for Gmail, Drive, and Calendar integration. The SpaceX GPU cluster's impact on product velocity appears to already be materializing.

## Grok 4.6: 1.5-Trillion Parameter Frontier Model Deployed to Cursor

xAI launched Grok 4.6, a 1.5-trillion parameter frontier model with 500K context and $2/$6 per million token pricing ([Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/xai-launches-grok-4-6-1753-elo-half-the-price-of-rival-frontier-models)). It matches GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index ([Codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/grok-4-6-launch-guide-2026/)). Available in Cursor, Grok Build, and the API, Grok 4.6 is the first concrete signal of what the SpaceX-Cursor integration will look like under the Grok brand.

## Gemini: App Crosses 1 Billion Monthly Users

The Google Gemini app has surpassed 1 billion monthly active users ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/google/gemini)). Growth coincides with the Gemini 3.7 Flash launch, now serving AI Pro and Ultra subscribers across 160+ countries via Gemini Spark ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/13/google-gemini-37-flash)).
