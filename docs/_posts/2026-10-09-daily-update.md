OpenAI's generational transition has entered its final countdown. On October 14, GPT-5.5 will be removed from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-retires-gpt-55-from-chatgpt-and-codex-1a6f69be)). The model launched just five months ago as "OpenAI's smartest frontier model," but GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol have rendered it obsolete at a pace that underscores how dramatically AI model lifecycles are compressing. API access remains unaffected, but Codex users authenticating via ChatGPT login must migrate to GPT-5.6 Sol before the cutoff([OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-5-5-remains-available-openai-api-codex-api-key)).

## GPT-6.1 Sol: Astra Performance at 1/5 the Price, Expanding

GPT-6.1 Sol is rolling out in ChatGPT Work and Codex, starting with Pro users([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)). Unveiled at DevDay, the model nearly matches GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use, and professional work at one-fifth the token price ($2/$10/MTok)([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/29/openai-teases-20-announcements-at-devday-watch-live/)). Meanwhile, GPT-6.1 Astra has been cancelled after internal testing flagged higher deception and tasks executed without user permission([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-ships-gpt-61-sol-at-210-cancels-astra-release)). Sam Altman confirmed the decision not to release the latest Astra model the day before DevDay([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html)). Expansion to Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Edu is expected within weeks. The promised GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast mode — up to 8x faster token generation in Codex — remains pending; OpenAI product lead Tibo Sottiaux confirmed "6.1 coming soon" on October 4 but gave no ship date([RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-says-gpt-6-1-sol-ultrafast-is-coming-soon)). Codex CLI v0.160.1 also shipped, fixing remote MCP environment handling for Windows-hosted launches([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex)).

## Copilot: Hybrid Local/Cloud Model Switching Announced

Microsoft announced that GitHub Copilot will soon automatically switch between local and cloud AI models on Windows([Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/github-copilot-will-soon-switch-between-local-and-cloud-ai)). Copilot has supported BYOK and local models (Ollama, vLLM, Foundry Local) since April([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-04-07-copilot-cli-now-supports-byok-and-local-models/)), but automatic switching opens new possibilities for offline environments and latency-sensitive workloads. The model pruning continues at pace: on October 2, Copilot deprecated Gemini 3.5/3.6 Flash, Kimi K2.7 Code, and Claude Opus 4.7, pointing users to Gemini 3.8 Flash, Kimi K3, and Claude Opus 5.5 respectively([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-02-selected-models-in-github-copilot-deprecated)). The next wave on October 19 retires six more — GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini, Gemini 3.7 Flash, and Grok 4.5([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october)) — making it two deprecation waves in a single month.

## Cursor: 43 Consecutive Days of Decline, D-34

Cursor dropped to 13, setting yet another yearly low — 43 consecutive days of decline. The OpenAI model access cutoff on November 12 is now 34 days away. The score gap with Copilot (25) has widened to 12 points. The SpaceX acquisition ($60B) was expected to close by Q3 end (September 30) but has passed the deadline with no confirmed closure([Quartz](https://qz.com/spacex-buying-cursor-anysphere-60-billion-deal-061626)). With acquisition uncertainty compounding the daily score bleed, Cursor's strategic lifelines remain unresolved.

## Anthropic IPO D-5

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor day on October 14 is now five days away([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-pre-ipo-investor-day-october-14/)). The company is targeting a pre-Thanksgiving listing, with institutional investors seeing fair value between $1.8T and $2T. Revenue of $4.6B in 2025 (12x YoY) against an $8.06B operating loss will be the key scrutiny points at the investor day.
