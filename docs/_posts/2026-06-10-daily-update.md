Anthropic's Code with Claude Tokyo opened today. The heat from Fable 5's bombshell release two days ago hasn't cooled, and the three-track conference signals Anthropic's serious push into the Japanese market.

## Code with Claude Tokyo: NEC Partnership and 30K Deployment

Code with Claude Tokyo kicked off with Research, Platform, and Code tracks([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/tokyo)). NEC was announced as Anthropic's first Japan-based global partner, deploying Claude to over 30,000 NEC Group employees worldwide([ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/code-with-claude-tokyo-june-10-builder-preview-guide/)). Overwhelming demand from April's lottery prompted an Extended session tomorrow for indie developers([claude.com](https://claude.com/code-with-claude/tokyo-extended)). With Fable 5 freshly launched, expectations for new capability announcements are running high.

## Fable 5 First Reactions: "Something of a Beast"

Day two of developer reactions to Fable 5 paint a picture of raw power with trade-offs. Simon Willison called it "something of a beast" that happily churned through every task thrown at it, while noting the slow speed and high cost at $10/$50 per million tokens([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/9/claude-fable-5/)). Latent Space highlighted controversy around updated terms of service([Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-claude-fable-5-mythos)). Same-day availability on AWS Bedrock secured enterprise access([AWS Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropic-claude-fable-5-on-aws-mythos-class-capabilities-with-built-in-safeguards-now-available/)), and Fable 5 is already GA on GitHub Copilot([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-09-claude-fable-5-is-generally-available-for-github-copilot/)).

## Copilot: 46, 37-Week Decline

Copilot dropped to **46** — its 37th consecutive weekly decline([ghacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/02/github-copilot-usage-based-billing-takes-effect-drawing-developer-backlash-over-rapid-credit-depletion/)). Day 10 of usage-based billing. A JetBrains survey found that among developers with 10+ years of experience, 46% chose Claude Code for daily use versus just 9% for Copilot([NxCode](https://www.nxcode.io/resources/news/github-copilot-getting-worse-2026-developers-switching)). Stack Overflow's 2026 survey shows market share plunging from 67% to 51%.

## SpaceX IPO D-2: Largest IPO in History Approaches

SpaceX IPO pricing is tomorrow (June 11), with Nasdaq trading starting June 12([TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261904604-spacex-ipo-spcx-date-set-for-june-12-175-trillion-valuation-tradingkey)). At $135/share and a $1.75T valuation, it will be the largest IPO ever. A Nasdaq fast-track rule enables Nasdaq-100 index inclusion within 15 trading days, triggering an estimated $7B in index fund purchases([eciks.org](https://eciks.org/7803-24831-qqq-spacex-ipo-nasdaq-100-june-2026)). This IPO secures funding for the $60B Cursor acquisition.

## Countdown

Anthropic agent billing split (D-5, June 15) separates Agent SDK and `claude -p` usage into a dedicated credit pool — Pro $20, Max 5x $100, Max 20x $200([codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/anthropic-june-2026-billing-change-claude-code/)). Gemini CLI sunset (D-8, June 18) restricts access to enterprise customers only — everyone else must migrate to Antigravity CLI([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/)).
