Three extensions in five weeks. Anthropic can't let go of Fable 5 as the GPT-5.6 Sol rivalry intensifies.

## Fable 5 Extended to July 19 — Plus an Opus 5 Leak

Anthropic has extended Fable 5 free access for paid subscribers through July 19, 11:59 PM PT — the third extension in five weeks ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/07/13/claude-fable-5-extends-to-july-19-7-days-7-power-moves/)). Pro, Max, Team, and premium Enterprise plans get up to 50% of weekly usage limits at no extra cost ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/claude-fable-5-stays-free-for-paid-users-until-july-19-as-anthropic-buys-more-time/)). The pattern — July 7 to July 12 to July 19 — tracks directly against OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol launch, with Anthropic resetting weekly limits each time ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/13/ai-model-wars-anthropic-extends-fable-access-again-after-openais-sol-release/)).

More intriguing: on July 8, an unreleased model called "Claude Honeycomb EAP" briefly appeared in Cursor's model picker before being pulled within hours ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320265/20260712/fable-5-free-through-july-19-anthropic-blinks-again-opus-5-leak-surfaces-cursor.htm)). Community consensus is hardening that this is Opus 5's early access build, though Anthropic has refused to confirm ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/fable-5-honeycomb-opus/)).

## Codex Micro — OpenAI's First Hardware, Launching Tomorrow

OpenAI's programmable macro pad Codex Micro, co-built with Work Louder, launches July 15 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319389/20260630/openai-codex-micro-launches-july-15-macro-pad-built-work-louder.htm)). The device packs 13 mechanical keys, a joystick, a rotary encoder, and six programmable layers — a physical extension of the Codex platform that now serves over 5 million weekly active users ([DevOps.com](https://devops.com/openai-expands-into-developer-hardware-with-codex-micro-keyboard/)). Pricing is expected in the $144–$174 range based on the Creator Micro 2 baseline.

## jscrambler Supply Chain Attack — AI Tool Credentials Are the New Target

The jscrambler npm supply chain attack disclosed July 11 injected a Rust-based infostealer targeting config files for Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, and VS Code — harvesting API keys and MCP server credentials ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/compromised-jscrambler-8140-npm-release.html)). With 15,800 weekly downloads weaponized, affected projects need immediate credential rotation ([Socket](https://socket.dev/blog/jscrambler-supply-chain-attack)).

## Gemini 3.5 Pro — D-3, July 17 GA Countdown

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is three days from its July 17 GA target ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm)). A 2-million-token context window and Deep Think reasoning are expected, but Google has not officially confirmed specs. Copilot users on Gemini 2.5 Pro and 3 Flash must migrate by July 31 ([GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-02-upcoming-deprecation-of-gemini-2-5-pro-and-gemini-3-flash/)).

## Anthropic-Samsung 2nm Chip Partnership Talks

Anthropic is in talks with Samsung to manufacture a custom AI chip using Samsung's 2-nanometer process ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/anthropic-in-talks-with-samsung-for-custom-ai-chip-information-mr3l34t4)). No design or manufacturing work has begun, but the move follows OpenAI's Broadcom collaboration on "Jalapeño" and signals the custom silicon race is expanding ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/anthropic-is-discussing-a-new-custom-chip-with-samsung/)).
