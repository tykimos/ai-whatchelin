Anthropic is shutting down every known workaround Chinese companies use to access Claude — offshore subsidiaries, cloud platforms, and VPNs ([BanklessTimes](https://www.banklesstimes.com/articles/2026/07/03/anthropic-moves-to-block-chinese-firms-using-claude-via-offshore-workarounds/)). Coming after Alibaba's Qwen lab ran a 25,000-account distillation attack generating 28.8 million interactions, the crackdown signals that AI model access is becoming a geopolitical weapon.

## Anthropic: Full Chinese Access Crackdown with Identity Verification

Ant Group gave staff corporate Claude accounts through a Singapore subsidiary, while ByteDance reimbursed engineers for personal subscriptions purchased via VPN ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4609813-anthropic-cracks-down-on-chinese-workaround-access-to-claude-ft-reports)). Anthropic now monitors computer time zones and usage patterns to detect "transfer station" accounts, and requires government-issued IDs and live selfies for flagged users. The company has already rolled back some covert tracking code after user pushback ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/01/anthropic-is-removing-its-covert-code-for-catching-chinese-competitors/5265366)).

## US June Jobs Report: 57K Added — AI Employment Shock Goes Mainstream

The BLS June payroll report released July 3 showed just 57,000 jobs added, far below the 185,000 consensus estimate ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-3-2026)). Tech sector layoffs hit 142,000 year-to-date in 2026, with 78,000 workers losing jobs the same week their employer announced AI productivity gains ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/)). AI engineer and prompt engineer demand is at all-time highs, but entry-level admin, coding, and customer support roles are being replaced at an accelerating pace.

## Claude Science Launches — AI Workbench for Researchers

Anthropic unveiled Claude Science, a dedicated AI workbench for scientists that integrates commonly used research tools and packages, produces auditable artifacts, and provides flexible computing resource access ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench)). This continues Claude's industry-specific app strategy, following the legal sector launch earlier this year.

## Google: Ultra AI Access Removed July 7, Antigravity Hits 91

Google's Ultra AI Access add-on will be removed from Workspace accounts starting July 7. Gemini CLI and Code Assist access must migrate to Enterprise Standard or Plus ([Google Workspace Help](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/gemini/ai-ultra-access)). Meanwhile, Antigravity hit 91 on its 14th consecutive week of gains, extending its lead over Codex CLI (88) to 3 points.

## Copilot: 58-Week Slide Continues — 23 Points

GitHub Copilot fell to 23 on its 58th consecutive week of decline. Copilot CLI v1.0.68 added the Kimi K2.7 Code model, but usage-based billing backlash continues unabated ([NxCode](https://www.nxcode.io/resources/news/github-copilot-getting-worse-2026-developers-switching)).
